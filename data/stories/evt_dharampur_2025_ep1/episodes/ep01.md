# Episode 1 — The Dead Woman Walks In

# Episode 1 — "The Dead Woman Walks In"

SFX: police courtyard applause, microphone squeal

COMPERE: For solving Kaveri's murder, Dharampur Police honours Inspector Devraj—

SFX: metal gate bangs open

KAVERI: Stop! I am Kaveri. Which murder did he solve?

SFX: applause cuts off, plastic chair falls

TARA: Maa?

SFX: hurried footsteps on stone

KAVERI: Tara. Yes. Come here, child. You and Munni, come to me.

SFX: wooden staff strikes stone once

OMKAR: Girls, stay where you are.

MUNNI: Didi... she knows my name.

TARA: Lots of people know our names.

KAVERI: Look at me, Tara. Look properly. That scar beside your ear, you got it falling from my cot.

TARA: Don't... don't say things like that.

SFX: police whistle, gate scrapes shut

DEVRAJ: Constable, close the gate. Nobody leaves the courtyard.

SFX: crowd murmurs rising

KAVERI: Devraj, tell them. You came to our house twice. You drank tea from the blue steel cup.

DEVRAJ: Let us be precise. You know my name because it was announced on the microphone.

KAVERI: No. I know your face. I know my father. I know my daughters.

SFX: walking stick taps forward

HARILAL: Do not call me that.

KAVERI: Baba...

HARILAL: My daughter is dead.

SFX: crowd murmurs agreement

WOMAN IN CROWD: Shameless woman. Today of all days, she came to claim a dead mother's children.

KAVERI: I am not claiming them. I gave birth to them.

SFX: medal case snaps shut

OMKAR: Inspector, remove this disturbance. The district officers are waiting to present your medal.

SFX: bangles clink as someone pushes through chairs

FARIDA: No, let her speak. You gave medals before giving four innocent men a trial.

DEVRAJ: Farida, this ceremony is not about your brother.

SFX: crowd erupts

FARIDA: You arrested Rafiq for killing Kaveri. Kaveri has just walked through your gate.

KAVERI: Rafiq? The mechanic? What did you do to him?

FARIDA: Eighteen months in jail. Rafiq, Mahesh, Bansi and Irfan. Your murder made Inspector Devraj famous.

KAVERI: My murder... no. No, I was gone, but I wasn't—

SFX: police baton knocks railing

DEVRAJ: Enough. A solved case does not collapse because a stranger memorised village gossip.

MEERA: Sir, identity verification should be recorded before—

DEVRAJ: Meera, you are here to manage certificates, not investigations.

SFX: papers gathered quickly

MEERA: Yes, sir.

SFX: loose paper flutters beneath wooden dais

NARRATOR: Before the first clap that morning, one page had already been cut from the corpse register. Nobody in that courtyard saw who took it.

KAVERI: Take my fingerprints. Ask me anything. Tara was born just before dawn. Munni hates milk skin. Baba keeps mother's anklet inside the grain tin.

HARILAL: Quiet.

KAVERI: You know that is true.

HARILAL: A thief can learn where a family keeps its jewellery.

MUNNI: Nana, she sounds like Maa.

OMKAR: Munni, your mother received proper final rites. Do not insult her memory by staring at this woman.

KAVERI: You brought my children to watch a man receive a prize for my death?

TARA: They said you screamed. They said those men killed you near the canal.

KAVERI: Tara, I did not die near any canal.

TARA: Then where were you?

KAVERI: I... I will tell you. Just come here first.

TARA: No. Answer me first.

DEVRAJ: She will answer inside, after we establish who she is. Children should not hear an impostor's inventions.

KAVERI: Do not call me an impostor in front of my daughters.

DEVRAJ: Then provide a valid identity document.

KAVERI: It was taken. My residency card, my electoral slip, everything. Check your old records.

MEERA: Sir, the old electoral register may have her photograph.

DEVRAJ: The register records a deceased voter. It cannot testify for a living stranger.

SFX: scattered claps from crowd

FARIDA: But a burnt body testified well enough to jail four men?

DEVRAJ: Farida, your courage is impressive. Your brother's workshop lease is still under police review, isn't it.

FARIDA: You already took the workshop. You cannot frighten us with its empty walls.

SFX: bare feet step forward

KAVERI: Inspector, I came for my children. Move aside.

SFX: metal railing gate unlatches

DEVRAJ: You came into a police station, interrupted an official ceremony and attempted to approach two minors. You will wait where complainants wait.

KAVERI: I am their mother.

DEVRAJ: That is your complaint. Stand behind the railing while we examine it.

SFX: metal railing gate rattles

CONSTABLE: Inside, madam.

SFX: cloth scuffle, railing gate slams

KAVERI: Don't touch me. Tara, Munni, I am here. I came back for you.

SFX: child runs two steps

MUNNI: Maa!

OMKAR: Hold her, Tara.

TARA: Munni, stop. Please... just stop.

KAVERI: Baba, tell them about the burn on my left wrist. Tell them how I got it in your kitchen.

HARILAL: Many women have burns.

KAVERI: Tell them what you called me when I was small.

HARILAL: My child is not standing behind that railing.

KAVERI: Then look into my face and say whose body you buried.

SFX: walking stick stops

HARILAL: We did not bury anyone.

KAVERI: What?

DEVRAJ: Harilal, there is no need to discuss evidence publicly.

SFX: canvas satchel opens

HARILAL: There is every need. This woman wants the village to doubt my daughter's death.

KAVERI: Baba, please. I am standing here.

SFX: official paper unfolds beside microphone

HARILAL: This is Kaveri's death certificate. I identified the body. I personally lit my daughter's funeral pyre.
