# Episode 5 — "The Pending List"

SFX: registry room, ledgers, distant typing

NARRATOR: To keep a fake court alive, you need one thing above everything else. You need to know which cases the real court has not got. And that list exists. In one room. In one cupboard. Guarded by one clerk who does not want money.
    [tension 0.80 slow | bed tension | SWELL]

SFX: chair pulled, door closed

ADVOCATE: Sit down, Mr Christian. I did not ask that question in front of my client for a reason.
    [neutral 0.62 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: Then ask it now.
    [tension 0.70 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: Is there an appointment order?
    [tension 0.75 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: There is a consent memorandum signed by both parties.
    [tension 0.72 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: That is not what I asked you.
    [hurt_anger 0.75 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: No. There is no prior appointment order.
    [sorrow 0.72 slow | bed sorrow]

ADVOCATE: Thirty-eight years I am practising. Do you know how many rooms like yours I have seen?
    [reflective 0.65 slow | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: How many?
    [hesitation 0.58 clipped | bed sorrow]

ADVOCATE: Four. All four closed within one year. Not because they were caught. Because one losing party went to a real court, and the real court said — which case? Which number? Which judge?
    [tension 0.78 normal | bed tension]

MORRIS: My orders are correct on the documents.
    [hurt_anger 0.72 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: Your orders are excellent. That is the tragedy. If you had a degree you would be sitting in that building, and I would be standing before you.
    [sorrow 0.70 slow | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: Is that a compliment or a warning?
    [sarcasm 0.68 normal | bed sorrow]

ADVOCATE: It is arithmetic. Today I consented because my client had already lost. The next lawyer will not consent. He will check first.
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed tension | STING]

NARRATOR: And that was the sentence that changed everything. Not a threat. Not a police complaint. One old man, being kind. He will check first.
    [tension 0.82 slow | bed tension]

SFX: registry corridor, footsteps

MORRIS: Pandit-ji. One minute.
    [neutral 0.58 normal | bed neutral]

PANDIT: Arre, Morris. Long time. They are saying you have started some office.
    [joy 0.60 normal | bed neutral]

MORRIS: A private forum. Small matters.
    [hesitation 0.62 clipped | bed tension]

PANDIT: Small matters. Above a tyre shop. With a board.
    [sarcasm 0.68 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: You have seen it.
    [fear 0.68 clipped | bed tension]

PANDIT: Everybody in this registry has seen it. What do you want?
    [neutral 0.62 clipped | bed tension]

MORRIS: The civil pending list.
    [tension 0.75 slow | bed tension]

PANDIT: The pending list.
    [fear 0.70 slow | bed fear]

MORRIS: Only to see which disputes are already filed here. I do not want to touch a matter that is before a real judge.
    [urgency 0.72 fast | bed fear]

PANDIT: You do not want conflict. Very responsible.
    [sarcasm 0.70 slow | bed fear]

MORRIS: It is thirty-two pages. One hour.
    [urgency 0.72 clipped | bed fear]

PANDIT: It is thirty-two pages of other people's misfortune, and you want to shop in it.
    [hurt_anger 0.78 normal | bed fear]

SFX: envelope taken from pocket

MORRIS: Name your price.
    [tension 0.72 clipped | bed tension]

PANDIT: Put that back inside.
    [hurt_anger 0.75 clipped | bed tension]

MORRIS: Two thousand.
    [tension 0.70 clipped | bed tension]

PANDIT: I said put it back. Cash is evidence. Cash is a number somebody can count in a court.
    [fear 0.78 fast | bed fear]

MORRIS: Then what do you want?
    [hesitation 0.68 slow | bed fear]

PANDIT: Come at one o'clock. Everybody goes for lunch at one.
    [tension 0.72 slow | bed tension]

SFX: empty room, photocopier hum

NARRATOR: At one o'clock, the registry emptied. And a thirty-two page list was left lying on the rear photocopier. Not handed over. Just left. So that nobody had given anything to anybody.
    [tension 0.82 slow | bed tension | DROP]

SFX: camera shutter, pages turning fast

MORRIS: Page eleven is missing.
    [urgency 0.75 fast | bed tension]

PANDIT: Page eleven is land acquisition. Government matters. You will not survive government matters.
    [neutral 0.68 clipped | bed tension]

MORRIS: You have read it before leaving it.
    [tension 0.75 slow | bed tension]

PANDIT: I have read every page in that cupboard for nineteen years. Nobody has ever asked me one question about any of them.
    [sorrow 0.75 slow | bed sorrow]

NARRATOR: Nineteen years. And Morris understood, standing in that empty room, that he was not the only man in that building who had been made invisible.
    [sorrow 0.78 slow | bed sorrow]

SFX: camera shutter final, papers squared

MORRIS: It is done. Thirty-two pages.
    [neutral 0.65 normal | bed tension]

PANDIT: Good. Now my price.
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: You said not money.
    [fear 0.72 clipped | bed fear]

PANDIT: It is not money.
    [tension 0.80 slow | bed fear]

PANDIT: One sealed envelope. Every month. On the last working day, same day you pay your clerk.
    [tension 0.82 slow | bed fear]

MORRIS: Containing what?
    [fear 0.78 clipped | bed fear]

PANDIT: You will open it first. And then you will do what is inside it.
    [tension 0.85 slow | bed fear | STING]

MORRIS: Pandit-ji—
    [fear 0.80 fast | bed fear]

PANDIT: No questions. That is the price. No questions, ever, about what is in the envelope or who put it there.
    [tension 0.85 slow | bed fear]

MORRIS: And if I refuse?
    [fear 0.78 slow | bed fear]

PANDIT: Then give back the photographs and we will both forget this lunch hour.
    [neutral 0.70 slow | bed fear]

NARRATOR: He had come to buy a list, so that he would never rule on a case somebody could check. He walked out owing a man twelve rulings he had not yet written.
    [tension 0.88 slow | bed tension | DROP]

NARRATOR: And the first envelope would arrive on the last working day of that same month.
    [tension 0.90 slow | bed tension | BUTTON]
