# Episode 4 — "Present, Sir"

SFX: A cycle chain that needs oil. Loose stones. A school bell, hand-swung, three strikes, from about two hundred metres away.

CHAITRA: (breathing hard, to herself) Two hundred and eighty-eight. In a hand I have never seen.

SFX: The cycle stopping. Being leaned against a compound wall. A gate with a chain that is never actually locked.

GIRIJA: (calling across a yard) Yaar? Oh — is that Master's daughter? Chaitra! Come, come. You've grown taller than your mother. Everybody was saying — first in the taluk, first in the taluk, and Master didn't tell us one word. Not one word. That man. Twenty-four years I am teaching in the next room, and he has never once told me a good thing about his own house.

CHAITRA: Where is he.

GIRIJA: Assembly. But you can't go in the middle of — arey, listen, today is not a normal day, today the census people have come, from the block office, staff verification—

CHAITRA: Good.

GIRIJA: (to the yard generally, delighted) See her face. Exactly the father. Exactly.

SFX: Four hundred children. Not shouting — the specific low roar of four hundred children standing in lines being told to stop moving. A microphone that pops when it is switched on.

SHANKARAPPA: (amplified, flat) Silence. Fifth standard, third row, silence. Today the assembly will take longer. As per procedure.

SFX: A folding table being opened on the veranda. A plastic case unclipped. The whine of a small machine warming up.

NARRATOR: The department had issued a circular. Staff strength across the district was to be verified physically, name by name, in the presence of the school, with a thumb impression on a machine, so that nobody would be drawing a salary who was not standing in front of the children he was paid to teach. Somebody in an office two hundred kilometres away had thought this was a formality.

SFX: Chaitra's footsteps on gravel, at the back of the assembly, stopping.

CHAITRA: (low, to herself) Appa.

SFX: A chair scraping on the veranda.

PUTTARAJU: (amplified, bored) Headmaster, you read the names. Not me. My office only supplies the machine. Each teacher comes forward, says present, gives thumb. If the thumb does not take, we do it again. If it does not take second time, we write remarks. I don't want any remarks in this school.

SHANKARAPPA: As per procedure, sir.

PUTTARAJU: As per procedure only.

SFX: A long cloth-bound register opening on the table. Pages.

SHANKARAPPA: (amplified) Staff of Government Higher Primary School, Nerlige. Sanctioned strength eleven, working strength nine. Names as per service register.

SFX: Four hundred children going quiet, in one movement, the way children do when they sense that adults are being tested for once.

SHANKARAPPA: Girija, assistant teacher.

GIRIJA: Present, sir!

SFX: A beep. A machine accepting.

SHANKARAPPA: Nagarathnamma, assistant teacher.

VOICE: Present, sir.

SFX: Beep.

SHANKARAPPA: Yogesh, physical education instructor.

VOICE: Present sir.

SFX: Beep.

SHANKARAPPA: Nirmala, assistant teacher.

VOICE: Present, sir.

SFX: A machine trying. Trying again. A flat two-tone refusal.

PUTTARAJU: (amplified, without interest) Thumb is worn. Cooking at home, no? Ladies' thumbs go first. Wet the finger and press slower. Not harder. Slower.

SFX: The machine. Then the beep.

PUTTARAJU: See. Nobody has ever failed this machine twice. Twenty-two schools I have done this month, four hundred and eleven teachers, four hundred and eleven thumbs. Not one remark.

GIRIJA: (off-mic, to somebody beside her, and it carries the way a teacher's whisper carries) Four hundred and eleven and he wants us to clap.

SFX: Somebody shushing her. Four hundred children who have heard every word and are pretending not to have.

SFX: A page turning. And then not turning. The microphone carrying the sound of a man breathing near it.

PUTTARAJU: Next.

SFX: Nothing. Long. Four hundred children waiting.

CHAITRA: (from the back, not loudly, but it carries the way a voice carries across a school yard in the morning) Read it, sir.

SFX: Every head turning at once. That sound — four hundred collars.

GIRIJA: (hissing) Chaitra. Chaitra!

PUTTARAJU: (amplified, irritated) Who is that? This is a departmental verification, not a public function. Headmaster—

CHAITRA: I am a candidate for assistant teacher, sir. My verification was rejected on Tuesday. In front of ninety people, at counter four, they read out my father's name and they read out the date he died. Nineteen ninety-four. So I have come to hear his name read out by somebody who has known him for nineteen years, and I would like it read at the same volume.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

NARRATOR: She was twenty-two, she had not slept, and she had made one mistake. She had assumed that a man cannot lie in front of four hundred children.

SHANKARAPPA: (amplified, and there is a sigh in it, audible, the sigh of a man with nineteen months to retirement) Chaitra amma, sit down at the side. Please.

CHAITRA: Read the name, sir.

SFX: A pen being set down on a table. Deliberate.

PUTTARAJU: Headmaster. The machine is on battery.

SHANKARAPPA: Sir.

PUTTARAJU: Read the name.

SFX: The register page, flattened with a palm.

SHANKARAPPA: (amplified) Lokanath. Assistant teacher. Appointed nineteen ninety-four. Continuous service twenty-four years in this district, eleven years in this school.

SFX: Nothing. The fan of a small machine. A crow on the tin roof, walking.

CHAITRA: (and her voice has gone somewhere else) Appa. Don't.

SFX: A chair, on the veranda, pushed back. One pair of chappals on cement. Six steps. The specific quiet of four hundred children who have understood that something is happening and do not know what.

CHAITRA: Appa. There is a machine on that table. Don't put your hand on it. Appa — look at me — the buffalo, the notebooks, forty-one notebooks, you said forty-one notebooks—

MALLESHA: (not amplified; the microphone catches him anyway, and his voice is completely level) The fourth standard has arithmetic at nine forty. Multiplication of three digits by two. They have been weak in carrying over. That is all.

CHAITRA: Appa!

SFX: Nothing. Long.

SHANKARAPPA: (amplified, tired) Once more, for the record, as per procedure. Lokanath. Assistant teacher.

SFX: Four hundred children. And one hand going up — cloth moving, a shirt sleeve, nothing more than that, and every person in that yard hears it because they are all listening for it.

MALLESHA: Present, sir.
