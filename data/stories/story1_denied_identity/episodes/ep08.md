# Episode 8 — "Counter Four Again"

SFX: A shamiana flapping. A generator behind it. A brass band that has been playing the same eleven bars for forty minutes. Plastic chairs on dry mud.

INCHARA: (low, fast) Front row is sarpanch and donors. Second row is department. He'll come at ten forty, he'll cut the ribbon at ten forty-two, he will be in that chair for exactly the length of one welcome song and then he goes to the car, and between the chair and the car there is eleven metres of open ground with no peon on it. That is your eleven metres. Don't waste it on hello.

CHAITRA: Why are you helping me.

INCHARA: Because if you get thrown out I'll write that too. Now stand at the water drum, everybody stands at the water drum, nobody looks at the water drum.

SFX: A microphone whining. Applause that starts before it is earned.

VOICE (compere): — and now the new toilet block of Government Higher Primary School, Hosakere, will be inaugurated by the respected deputy director of public instruction of our district—

SFX: Scissors. A ribbon. A conch, badly blown. More applause. A brass band starting the same eleven bars.

NARRATOR: Eleven metres. She had rehearsed it in her head all night in eleven sentences, because that is the number she now counts everything in.

SFX: Footsteps on mud. Chairs. A voice saying yes-yes-yes to three people at once.

CHAITRA: Sir. Roll number four one nine two. Taluk first, written exam, assistant teacher recruitment.

REVANNA: (moving, not stopping) Verification is at the district office, amma, not at a toilet block.

CHAITRA: My verification was rejected because my father is dead, sir.

SFX: Footsteps stopping. Mud.

REVANNA: (carefully) That is a condolence, not a rejection ground.

CHAITRA: He dropped me at the bus stand that morning, sir. He is a serving teacher of this department with twenty-four years, and on Tuesday your counter four read out his death date to ninety people, and my file was stamped in violet.

SFX: A crowd noticing. That change in the pitch of a crowd.

REVANNA: (lower) Who is the clerk?

CHAITRA: Basavaraj, sir. He was not wrong. That is the difficulty. He read exactly what the register says.

SFX: Nothing. Long. The band stopping between songs, the way bands do, into silence.

REVANNA: (and it is the voice of a man doing arithmetic) In two thousand and nineteen a complaint came to my office from this taluk. Anonymous. About a service register. I marked it to the block office for report and the report came back — no irregularity, register in order, signed by the block education officer.

CHAITRA: Puttaraju sir.

REVANNA: (flatly) I have been the deputy director for four years and I have never once been shown a register in this taluk that was not in order.

SFX: A car door being opened by someone else. Not closed.

REVANNA: What do you want from me? Say it in one line, the car is here.

CHAITRA: Reopen my file, sir. Not his. Mine. Let me stand at counter four one more time.

REVANNA: (to somebody else) Where is the pad. (writing) File of candidate roll number four one nine two is reopened for fresh verification, register the reason as departmental discrepancy in the father's service record, and the reason is to be read out at the counter — read out, not written in the margin — because it was read out on Tuesday. (to her) Is that symmetrical enough for you, amma?

CHAITRA: Yes sir.

REVANNA: Then don't come to any more toilet blocks.

SFX: A car door. An Innova pulling away over dry mud. The band starting again immediately, as if nothing has happened.

INCHARA: (arriving at a run) He wrote. He actually wrote. What did he write, what were the words, exact words—

CHAITRA: He said read it out, not in the margin.

INCHARA: (a breath) That's a quote. That's my quote. Chaitra, that is a quote from a deputy director on paper.

SFX: A bus, a long one, gears. A district road. Then: a corridor with four ceiling fans, and ninety people breathing.

NARRATOR: Twenty-one days after they stamped her study certificate in violet, she came back through the same door, and the corridor was the same corridor, and the fan was still turning too slow to matter.

SFX: The rubber stamp coming down, twice, on wood.

BASAVARAJ: Next. Counter four. Original and two copies, don't hand me photostats of photo—

SFX: The stamp stopping mid-stroke.

BASAVARAJ: You.

CHAITRA: Four one nine two.

BASAVARAJ: (uncomfortable, quieter) Amma, that file is closed, and there is a queue, and Tuesday was not personal—

CHAITRA: Rule fourteen, sub-clause three. Verification is by seniority of receipt.

BASAVARAJ: (a pause) Yes.

CHAITRA: My receipt is twenty-one days old, sir. There is nobody in this line senior to me.

SFX: Nothing. Long. Ninety people going quiet in one movement.

BASAVARAJ: (a drawer; an envelope; official paper unfolded) There is an order. It came by fax yesterday evening at six and by hand this morning at nine.

CHAITRA: Read it, sir.

BASAVARAJ: Amma—

CHAITRA: The order says read it. You read Tuesday's out. Read this one at the same volume.

SFX: Nothing. A man clearing his throat. A microphone that isn't there, so he does it with his chest, the way clerks do.

BASAVARAJ: (loud, formal, to the corridor) By order of the deputy director of public instruction. The verification file of candidate roll number four one nine two is reopened. The rejection of the fourth is set aside. Reason to be recorded: departmental discrepancy in the father's service record — the discrepancy being in the department's register and not in the candidate's documents.

SFX: Ninety people. A murmur that moves down a corridor like water.

BASAVARAJ: (and then, still loud, because he has already committed to the volume) Fresh verification, counter four. Madam, please give the six originals.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

NARRATOR: He had called her amma all week. Amma is for a girl at a counter. Madam is for a file that has an order behind it. Nine days, one word, ninety witnesses. She stood at that counter and she did not trust her own face, so she looked at the wood.

CHAITRA: (quietly) Thank you, sir.

BASAVARAJ: Don't thank me. (stamping, briskly) Marks card. Degree. Caste and income. Study certificate — this one is stamped rejected in violet, we will need a fresh certified copy, that is forty rupees and eleven days, nothing I can do. Birth certificate—

SFX: The stamp stopping.

BASAVARAJ: Where is the birth certificate.

CHAITRA: An application for correction of entry is pending at the block office. Form fifteen. Inward entry made on the nineteenth.

BASAVARAJ: (and something in his voice moves — a clerk hearing a thing that clerks hear) Nineteenth. Block office. Establishment section.

CHAITRA: Yes.

SFX: A register. Pages, fast, a man looking for something he already suspects is there.

BASAVARAJ: Madam. Your father's service file was requisitioned from this office at nine twenty this morning. Requisition slip is here in the movement register. Purpose column says pre-audit scrutiny.

CHAITRA: Requisitioned by who?

SFX: A finger stopping on a line.

BASAVARAJ: Thammanna. Establishment section, block office. Signed in his own hand, one hour and forty minutes before you walked in.
