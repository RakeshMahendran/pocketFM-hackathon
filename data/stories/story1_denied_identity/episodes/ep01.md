# Episode 1 — "Counter Four"

SFX: A ceiling fan turning too slow to matter. Ninety people breathing in a corridor. A rubber stamp coming down, twice, on wood.

BASAVARAJ: Next. Counter four. Original and two copies, don't hand me photostats of photostats.

SFX: Paper sliding across a laminate counter.

CHAITRA: Marks card, degree, caste and income, birth certificate, study certificate for seven years. Six documents. All originals.

BASAVARAJ: Roll number.

CHAITRA: Four one nine two. I stood first in the taluk.

BASAVARAJ: Everybody in this line stood first in something.

SFX: Pages turning. A pen tapping.

CHAITRA: Sir, my verification slot was ten fifteen. It is now half past one.

BASAVARAJ: And the list is the list. Rule fourteen, sub-clause three — verification is by seniority of receipt, not by appointment. Sit down.

CHAITRA: I'll stand.

SFX: More pages. The tapping stops.

BASAVARAJ: Father's name?

CHAITRA: It is written there.

BASAVARAJ: Say it.

CHAITRA: Lokanath. Master Lokanath. Government Higher Primary School, Nerlige. Twenty-four years in the department, sir, so if there is a query about the father's employment—

BASAVARAJ: There is a query about the father.

SFX: A drawer opening. A register — the big kind, cloth-bound — dropping flat on the counter.

CHAITRA: What query.

BASAVARAJ: (not to her) Puttaraju sir, one minute. Establishment matter.

CHAITRA: Sir. What query.

NARRATOR: There were ninety people in that corridor. Afterwards, every one of them remembered where they were standing.

SFX: The register's pages, thick, turning slowly. A finger stopping on a line.

BASAVARAJ: This name is flagged. Deceased-record cross-check, this is a standing instruction, madam, it is not my personal decision.

CHAITRA: My father dropped me at the bus stand this morning.

BASAVARAJ: Then your father is not this name.

CHAITRA: (fast) Read it properly.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

BASAVARAJ: I am reading it properly.

SFX: The rubber stamp. Once. REJECTED, in violet, across her seven-year study certificate.

CHAITRA: Read it out.

BASAVARAJ: Madam, sit down—

CHAITRA: Ninety people are already listening. Read it out.

SFX: A chair scraping. The corridor going quiet in one movement, the way a corridor does.

BASAVARAJ: The man named as her father on this certificate died on the fourteenth of August, nineteen ninety-four.
