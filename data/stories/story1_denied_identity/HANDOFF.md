# Handoff — The Man Who Answers to a Dead Name

`evt_kadamballi_2022`. Output of steps 1–3 (hunt → season → episodes). Ready for
the canon store, character views and the spinoff writer.

Generated by Claude following `src/discovery/prompts/hunter.md`,
`src/scoring/prompts/expand.md` and `src/generation/prompts/episode.md`.

## Files

| File | What it is |

## `beats.json` — the contract

```json
{"beat_id": "b023", "ep": 8, "seq": 2, "world_time": "...", "location": "...",
 "present": ["char_id"], "witnessed_by": ["char_id"], "hidden_from": ["char_id"],
 "what_happened": "one objective sentence",
 "state_changes": [{"entity": "...", "fact": "...", "valence": -5..5}],
 "source_ref": "t5" | "fictionalized", "tier": "core_canon"}
```

Verified before handoff:

- 46 beats, unique `beat_id`, all 14 episodes covered
- **Every id in `present` / `witnessed_by` / `hidden_from` exists in `dossier.cast`.** No strays
- `source_ref` is a `dossier.timeline` id or the literal `fictionalized` — 7 beats
  dramatise a sourced fact, 39 are invention
- `tier` is `core_canon` throughout. Nothing here may be mutated by a spinoff

Two beats carry a `note` field marking **deliberate open canon** — consequential
events with empty `present` and `witnessed_by`, which nobody in the cast can
reference. `b038` (the sale deed registered with nobody watching) is the season's
most consequential unwitnessed beat and the most obvious spinoff seed.

## Not in here — the next stage produces it

- **Character stubs.** `stub.voice_samples` needs verbatim lines, which only
  exist once prose is written. The scripts are in `episodes/`; the stubs are not
  extracted.
- **`branch_canon`.** Everything here is `core_canon`. Spinoff beats commit as
  `branch_canon` and default to `hidden_from` = all mainline characters.

## Clearance — read before generating anything

Status is **`fictionalize_first`**. The source event is recent (2022) and the
principals are living private Indian citizens.

- The four real individuals appear in `dossier.people` **by role only** — their
  names are deliberately not recorded anywhere in this output.
- Every generated name is fictional and lives in `dossier.cast`. Use `char_id`s.
  No real name may enter generated fiction.
- `dossier.never_narrate_as_fact` lists claims tagged `alleged`. A character may
  assert them; the narrator may not.
- The protagonist is invented outright — the record mentions no daughter. The
  point of view was moved off the accused deliberately.

## Known caveats

- One promise (`promises.json`) is `paid_late` rather than `paid` — settled after
  its `must_pay_by` episode.
- The cast was amended mid-run when writing surfaced three roles the plan had not
  anticipated. `beats.json` and `dossier.cast` agree now, but the pipeline has no
  rule permitting this and it should get one.
- `episode.md` never states the allowed `source_ref` values, so the generated
  values were wrong on all 46 beats and were rewritten before handoff. Fix the
  prompt before regenerating anything.
