# Episode 5 — The Name in the Ledger

# Episode 5 — "The Name in the Ledger"

SFX: metal latch rattling, fists striking wooden door

MEERA: This door. Break this door, and I will show you where they kept me.

LANDLORD: Nobody breaks my property. Show me a warrant or leave my courtyard.

KAVITA: Open the room. Refuse again, and I will seal the entire floor until a magistrate decides who enters it.

LANDLORD: You police people arrive after eighteen months and speak as though dust committed the crime.

MEERA: I slept behind that door. I counted the cracks above the window every night.

LANDLORD: I have never rented a room to any Meera.

DEVRAJ: Then we have travelled to Kota for another false memory.

MOHAN: She knew the staircase without anyone showing her.

TARA: That does not mean she lived here.

MEERA: There are seventeen steps. The sixth one is broken at the edge. The room has a green wall and no fan regulator.

LANDLORD: Every room upstairs has a green wall.

KAVITA: And the broken sixth step?

LANDLORD: People notice stairs while climbing them.

MEERA: There is a water stain shaped like a hook beside the window.

LANDLORD: Enough. The room was rented to another woman.

KAVITA: Name.

LANDLORD: Sarla.

MEERA: That is what Shahid called me in front of strangers.

LANDLORD: I do not know any Shahid.

KAVITA: You answered before I asked whether you knew him.

LANDLORD: Because she has repeated the name three times in my courtyard.

DEVRAJ: Madam, open the room. Either her details are correct, or we end this journey here.

MEERA: You are waiting for me to fail.

DEVRAJ: I am waiting for evidence that does not change whenever a new person arrives.

MOHAN: If she is wrong about one stain, will you take her away?

KAVITA: No. One room does not decide her identity.

LANDLORD: Good. Then decide it somewhere else.

KAVITA: Constable, call the local station and request a property seal.

LANDLORD: Wait. There is no need to spoil everyone's day over one empty room.

MEERA: It was not empty when you collected rent.

LANDLORD: I collected rent from Sarla's guardian.

KAVITA: Guardian? She was an adult tenant.

LANDLORD: That was the word he used.

KAVITA: Who was he?

LANDLORD: A transport man. I do not remember every customer.

MEERA: You remember enough to change the word landlord into guardian.

SFX: keys jangling, padlock opening

LANDLORD: I remember rent paid on time. That is all.

SFX: door hinges scraping

NARRATOR: The lock opened. Meera stepped inside before anyone invited her.

MEERA: The cot was against this wall.

KAVITA: It is against the other wall now.

MEERA: Move it. Please. There should be writing behind the headboard.

LANDLORD: There is no writing. I repaint between tenants.

MEERA: You did not repaint behind the cot.

SFX: wooden cot dragging across cement

KAVITA: Constable, move it carefully.

TARA: There are lines on the wall.

KAVITA: Thirty-four vertical marks. Some crossed in groups of seven.

MEERA: I marked the nights when he took the outside bolt off.

DEVRAJ: Anyone could make marks.

MEERA: The last group stops halfway because Shahid came early.

LANDLORD: She is inventing meaning for scratches.

KAVITA: Then tell me why this bedroom door has an outside bolt.

LANDLORD: For luggage security.

VOICE FROM COURTYARD: Luggage does not sleep in the room and cry at night.

LANDLORD: Who said that? Come upstairs and give your name.

SECOND VOICE FROM COURTYARD: We all heard someone. None of us wants our name in your ledger.

MEERA: They heard me.

KAVITA: They heard a person. I will take individual statements before deciding more.

MEERA: Look above the window. The hook-shaped stain.

TARA: It is there.

DEVRAJ: Stains survive tenants. It could be older than her claim.

MOHAN: Why do you make every correct answer smaller?

DEVRAJ: Because you are a child, and somebody must protect you from wanting too much.

MOHAN: I only want Ma.

TARA: You want this woman to be Ma.

MOHAN: She knows things Ma knew.

TARA: Things can be told.

MEERA: Tara is right to be afraid.

TARA: Do not tell me what I feel.

MEERA: Then I won't.

KAVITA: Meera, what else should be in this room? Something removable, not a wall or stain.

MEERA: A brass button under the window ledge. I pushed it into a crack.

LANDLORD: Now we will search for buttons in an old building?

KAVITA: Yes. Evidence is often smaller than the person hiding it.

TARA: Why a brass button?

MEERA: It came off Mohan's brown sweater. I found it in my blouse pocket after they brought me here.

MOHAN: The sweater with the bear?

MEERA: No. The one you hated. Brown wool, wooden train stitched near the collar.

MOHAN: It scratched my neck.

MEERA: You cried whenever I pulled it over your ears.

MOHAN: What did you sing?

TARA: Mohan, not here.

MOHAN: What did you sing when I would not put my head through?

MEERA: Little train, little train, wait beside the door. Mohan has one stubborn head, but Ma has counted four.

MOHAN: No. You missed the next part.

MEERA: I don't... I thought you had forgotten it.

MOHAN: Sing it properly.

MEERA: One for anger, one for tears, one that asks for sweets. The fourth comes out when morning starts, and kisses both my cheeks.

MOHAN: Ma.

TARA: Mohan—

MOHAN: She is my Ma. I am saying it where everybody can hear me. She is my Ma.

VOICE FROM COURTYARD: The boy has recognised his mother.

DEVRAJ: A lullaby proves memory, not identity.

MOHAN: Then my memory is better than yours.

SFX: metal scraping against plaster

KAVITA: I found the crack beneath the ledge.

KAVITA: There is no button. But the plaster has been disturbed recently.

LANDLORD: Repairs happen. You cannot arrest a wall.

MEERA: Where is the rental ledger?

LANDLORD: You have seen the room. Leave my records alone.

KAVITA: The room was rented under another name. The ledger is now part of the verification.

LANDLORD: Old books are downstairs. Some pages may be damaged.

KAVITA: If one page is missing, the entire ledger goes into evidence.

SFX: steel trunk lid opening, paper rustling

NARRATOR: Downstairs, the landlord opened a steel trunk and lifted out a red cloth book.

LANDLORD: Here. Sarla. Room twelve. Rent paid in cash.

KAVITA: The tenant column contains a different name, but this signature does not say Sarla.

MEERA: That is my name.

DEVRAJ: No. No, that date cannot be right.

KAVITA: Read it aloud.

LANDLORD: Twenty-first of June, twenty twenty-one.

DEVRAJ: By then, our family believed Meera had already been cremated.

KAVITA: And beside that date, the rental ledger contains Meera's signature.
