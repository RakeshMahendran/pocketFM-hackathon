# Episode 13 — "The Woman Under Meera’s Name"

SFX: sealed envelope slapped onto wooden table

LAB TECHNICIAN: Stop. Nobody touches this envelope until all four seals are read aloud.

JANKI: Read Kesar’s result first. I have waited long enough for everyone else’s name.

MEERA: And read mine in front of my children. They buried my name in public.

KAVITA: Both results will be read. Nobody leaves midway.

SFX: wax seals tapped one by one

LAB TECHNICIAN: Seal one, district forensic laboratory. Seal two, collection officer. Seal three, magistrate’s court. Seal four, receiving clerk.

DEVRAJ: The samples were not mixed? I need that stated clearly.

LAB TECHNICIAN: They were collected separately, coded separately and tested twice.

TARA: Then open it.

MOHAN: Ma, hold my hand.

MEERA: I am holding it.

SFX: paper knife slicing envelope

LAB TECHNICIAN: Reference sample A belongs to Devraj. Reference sample B belongs to the woman identifying herself as Meera.

DEVRAJ: She is Meera. You may read the report exactly, but she is my daughter.

JANKI: Let the paper say it too.

SFX: report sheet unfolded

LAB TECHNICIAN: The probability of biological parentage between sample A and sample B exceeds ninety-nine point nine nine percent.

MOHAN: What does that mean? Say it normally.

KAVITA: It means the laboratory confirms that Meera is Devraj’s biological daughter.

TARA: Say her full status too. She is alive.

KAVITA: Meera is alive, and this report scientifically confirms her identity.

DEVRAJ: I called my own daughter an impostor. This paper does not remove that shame.

MEERA: No. It does not.

DEVRAJ: But it removes the excuse of anyone who still calls you a stranger.

SFX: crowd murmuring beyond doorway

VOICE IN CORRIDOR: The dead woman’s sample is next.

JANKI: Do not call her that. Call her Kesar, if the report gives me that right.

SFX: second envelope lifted

LAB TECHNICIAN: The second comparison uses preserved tissue from the burned body and Janki’s reference sample.

JANKI: Her black thread had three knots. There was a copper bead between the second and third.

KAVITA: Those details supported the inquiry. DNA will decide the identification.

JANKI: I know. I am only... I am trying to remember her before you read it.

MEERA: Remember her.

SFX: paper sliding across table

LAB TECHNICIAN: The preserved tissue is not genetically related to Devraj.

DEVRAJ: Then the woman I signed for was never my daughter.

LAB TECHNICIAN: Correct.

JANKI: And me? Please, do not stop there.

LAB TECHNICIAN: The sibling analysis supports a full biological sister relationship between Janki and the deceased woman.

JANKI: How strongly? Give me the number.

LAB TECHNICIAN: The likelihood ratio exceeds twenty-eight thousand to one.

JANKI: So she was Kesar.

KAVITA: Yes. The woman cremated under Meera’s name was Kesar.

JANKI: They searched for Meera’s killers while my sister lay inside Meera’s death certificate.

MEERA: And while I was alive inside another woman’s name.

TARA: Mother, does this finish it? Can nobody take you away now?

MEERA: It finishes who I am. It does not finish what was done.

KAVITA: Correct. Identity is settled. Criminal responsibility is not.

JANKI: I want Kesar’s name returned today.

KAVITA: The civil registrar has been directed to prepare the corrected certificate.

JANKI: Directed is not delivered.

KAVITA: Then we will collect it ourselves.

SFX: metal service window rattling open

NARRATOR: One hour later, the laboratory report reached the district records counter before anyone could soften its language.

SFX: rubber stamps knocking in a drawer

BABULAL: I need the original death entry, the disputed seal order and both certified DNA pages.

JANKI: You already have them.

BABULAL: I have copies. Today, copies are not enough.

MEERA: They were enough when the office declared me dead.

BABULAL: Yes. That was wrong.

MEERA: Say the exact wrong.

BABULAL: The burned body was registered under Meera’s name without scientific identification.

DEVRAJ: And I signed the identification.

BABULAL: You did.

JANKI: After Meera’s name was already written on the form.

KAVITA: Which officer entered that name, Babulal? Promise nothing. Read the movement sheet.

SFX: file pages turned rapidly

BABULAL: The receiving duty officer wrote it. The entry says, identity supplied by telephone before family arrival.

MEERA: Whose telephone call?

BABULAL: The caller field contains one name.

KAVITA: Read it.

BABULAL: Raghav.

DEVRAJ: He gave them Meera’s name before I saw the body?

BABULAL: Twenty-three minutes before you signed.

MEERA: Then my father did not begin the lie.

DEVRAJ: I still completed it.

KAVITA: The duty officer physically wrote Meera’s name. The record says Raghav supplied it by telephone.

JANKI: He named my sister as Meera before anyone had identified her.

KAVITA: That is what the contemporaneous entry records.

SFX: staple pulled from paper

BABULAL: There is another attachment. The east depot complaint mentioned in the Kota register inquiry.

MEERA: The second woman.

KAVITA: Read only what the document establishes.

BABULAL: The complaint identifies the missing woman as Kesar. A depot camera note records Raghav’s former vehicle number.

JANKI: Kesar was the second woman linked to that alleged buyer?

BABULAL: Yes. The complaint gives her name, the vehicle number and the east depot location.

MEERA: His vehicle arrived with three occupants and left with two.

KAVITA: The depot timing places Kesar outside that vehicle before it departed Kota.

JANKI: He left her there.

KAVITA: The records show his vehicle departed after Kesar was dropped near the east depot. They do not yet prove what happened after that.

MEERA: But they prove why the third occupant was missing when the vehicle left.

KAVITA: Yes. Kesar was the third occupant. Raghav’s vehicle left with two after leaving her behind.

JANKI: And later she was found at the accident site under Meera’s name.

KAVITA: The DNA report establishes that the burned body at that site was Kesar.

BABULAL: I can now cancel the incorrect body-name entry and create Kesar’s lawful death record.

JANKI: Do it where I can see your hand.

SFX: fountain pen scratching on certificate

BABULAL: Cause and criminal responsibility remain subject to investigation. Identity is confirmed by DNA.

JANKI: Write her name without Meera’s name beside it.

BABULAL: Kesar. Female. Identity confirmed.

JANKI: Again.

BABULAL: Kesar.

MEERA: She waited inside my grave. I waited outside my house.

JANKI: Neither of you chose that.

SFX: official seal pressed hard

BABULAL: The corrected certificate is ready.

JANKI: Give it to me.

SFX: certificate passed through metal window

BABULAL: This certifies the death under Kesar’s own name. The previous certificate naming Meera remains suspended for cancellation by the court.

JANKI: For the first time since she vanished, an office has written my sister’s name correctly.

TARA: And Mother’s name?

KAVITA: Tomorrow, the court will hear the DNA report, the money trail and the false identification entry together.

MOHAN: Will the four men come too?

KAVITA: Yes. The court has ordered all four produced.

MEERA: Then let everyone who saw four murderers see four living men.

DEVRAJ: And everyone who saw a stranger will see my daughter.

JANKI: What does the final laboratory conclusion say? Not the certificate. The last paragraph.

SFX: DNA report lifted from counter

KAVITA: It says the remains match Janki’s biological family and do not match Devraj’s family.

MEERA: Read the consequence.

KAVITA: The DNA report places Kesar at the accident site and Meera nowhere near the death scene.
