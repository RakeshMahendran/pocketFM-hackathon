# Episode 2 — "The Wrong Hand"

SFX: crowd pounds on interview-room door

DEVRAJ: If that woman goes near Tara or Mohan, I will hold this station responsible for what it does to them.

SFX: door bolt slides shut

KAVITA: Your concern is noted. Now step away from the door.

MEERA: Do not send him away. Let him hear every answer.

KAVITA: No. Private details are useful only when the crowd cannot feed them to you.

DEVRAJ: She already knows enough to perform.

KAVITA: You will wait outside until I call you.

DEVRAJ: This is my daughter's case.

SFX: door opens, crowd swells

KAVITA: That is precisely why you do not conduct the test.

DEVRAJ: Very well. Test her. But do not mistake confidence for truth.

SFX: door shuts, crowd muffled

KAVITA: Close it.

MEERA: How long before I see my children?

KAVITA: When I can put you near them without turning two children into evidence.

MEERA: They are already evidence. Everyone outside is saying their mother's name.

SFX: metal chair scrapes

KAVITA: Sit. One fact at a time.

MEERA: I cannot sit quietly while Baba calls me an impostor.

KAVITA: Then sit angrily.

MEERA: You think I am lying.

KAVITA: I think the register says you are dead, you say you are alive, and four prisoners cannot both have murdered you and not murdered you.

MEERA: Ask me something.

SFX: notebook opens

KAVITA: What did Devraj break on the day Tara was born?

MEERA: Nothing. He claimed the tea glass broke by itself. Actually, he squeezed it when the nurse said the baby was a girl.

KAVITA: Where did the glass cut him?

MEERA: Between his thumb and first finger. He hid the bandage before entering my room.

KAVITA: What did he bring you?

MEERA: Two oranges and the wrong slippers. Both were for the right foot.

KAVITA: What colour?

MEERA: Green, with silver flowers. I wore them anyway because he looked so ashamed.

KAVITA: Mohan's first word.

MEERA: Not Ma. Everyone says Ma. It was wheel. He pointed at Baba's bicycle and said it until he slept.

KAVITA: What frightens Tara?

MEERA: People knowing she is frightened. She will stand near any snake if someone is watching. Alone, she cannot even touch a shed snakeskin.

KAVITA: That answer may matter later.

MEERA: It matters now. Bring her.

KAVITA: Not yet.

MEERA: You keep saying not yet. I heard that for months. Food, not yet. Outside, not yet. Home... not yet.

KAVITA: Who said that to you?

MEERA: I will give a full statement. First let my children see my face.

KAVITA: Your conditions do not control this inquiry.

MEERA: And my father's fear should not control it either.

KAVITA: Correct.

MEERA: Then why is he still the one deciding who I am?

SFX: door bolt snaps back

KAVITA: He is not.

SFX: door opens, crowd murmuring

KAVITA: Devraj. Come in alone.

DEVRAJ: Has she failed?

KAVITA: She answered six questions. Four match your earlier family statement exactly. Two were never in the file.

DEVRAJ: Then someone in the family spoke carelessly.

MEERA: Say my name once while looking at me.

DEVRAJ: I will not reward manipulation.

KAVITA: Did you break a tea glass when Tara was born?

DEVRAJ: I may have. That was many years ago.

KAVITA: Did you bring two right-foot slippers?

DEVRAJ: Small family stories travel. Evidence does not.

KAVITA: You said you brought evidence.

SFX: paper envelope placed on desk

DEVRAJ: I did.

DEVRAJ: The hospital photographed the burned body before identification. I identified the tattoo and the black thread tied at the wrist.

MEERA: My mother tied that thread after Mohan's fever. I never removed it.

DEVRAJ: The body had the same thread. The same tattoo. That is why I signed.

KAVITA: Describe your tattoo without looking at the photograph.

SFX: cloth sleeve pulled back

MEERA: Three dots above a small crooked leaf. The woman made the last line too deep. It is here, on my left hand.

DEVRAJ: A tattoo can be copied after the case becomes public.

MEERA: After? Look closely. The leaf has stretched with my skin. This is not new.

SFX: door opens

KAVITA: Babulal, bring the identification record.

SFX: file pages rustle

BABULAL: Madam, the hospital note says black thread at wrist and tattoo visible beside it.

KAVITA: Does it specify left or right?

BABULAL: No. Only the photograph shows the side.

DEVRAJ: I saw the mark. I had just lost my daughter. Do not examine my grief as though it were a crime.

SFX: magnifying glass taps photograph

KAVITA: I am examining the identification.

MEERA: Which hand?

DEVRAJ: The image may have been reversed during printing.

BABULAL: The hospital number is readable, not reversed.

MEERA: Which hand, madam? Please... say it.

KAVITA: The photograph shows the tattoo on the body is on the opposite hand from Meera's tattoo.
