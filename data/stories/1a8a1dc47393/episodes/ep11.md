# Episode 11 — Under the Neem Tree

# Episode 11 — "Under the Neem Tree"

SFX: garden hoe biting dry earth, children whispering

TARA: Do not let her follow us. If she is really my mother, she will know where to go.

MOHAN: She is Ma. You heard the lullaby.

TARA: You wanted her to be Ma before she opened her mouth.

MOHAN: And you wanted her to be a stranger.

SFX: small clay object scraping against metal

TARA: I wanted nobody to leave twice.

MOHAN: There. The bird still has one wing.

TARA: Put it back. Deep. Then cover the soil properly.

MOHAN: What if she does not remember?

TARA: Then I stop making excuses for her.

SFX: village courtyard crowd, neem leaves rustling

NARRATOR: Tara buried the clay bird again before breakfast. By noon, she had gathered half the village beneath the neem tree.

MEERA: Why are all these people here?

TARA: Because everybody watched when Grandfather called you a stranger. They can watch this too.

DEVRAJ: Tara, if this is another public test—

TARA: It is my test. You had yours. Police had theirs. Now I decide.

KAVITA: What are the rules?

TARA: Nobody answers for her. Nobody gives hints. Nobody tells me that fingerprints are enough.

MOHAN: Fingerprints are enough.

TARA: Mohan.

MOHAN: Fine. I will not give hints.

MEERA: Ask.

TARA: Not yet. First, tell everyone what you want from this house.

MEERA: You and Mohan.

TARA: That is not an answer. We are not objects kept in a cupboard.

MEERA: I want to live where my children do. I want to cook badly in my own kitchen. I want to know which schoolbook Mohan has lost this week.

MOHAN: Geography.

TARA: No hints.

MEERA: And I want Tara to stop standing near every doorway, ready to run if I say the wrong thing.

TARA: I do not do that.

SFX: crowd murmuring softly

MEERA: You are doing it now.

TARA: Maybe I like the doorway.

MEERA: You hated doorways. You said they made every room look unfinished.

TARA: That could be in a diary.

MEERA: You never kept a diary. You wrote one page, then tore it because Mohan drew a buffalo over your secret.

SFX: brief laughter from crowd

MOHAN: It looked better.

TARA: This is not the test.

KAVITA: Then begin it.

TARA: Before you disappeared, I made something. I hid it. Nobody from police found it. It is not in any statement.

DEVRAJ: What did you make?

TARA: I said nobody answers for her.

DEVRAJ: I was only— yes. Ask her.

TARA: What was it?

MEERA: You made many things. A clay cup that leaked. A horse with five legs.

TARA: So you do not know.

MEERA: I did not finish.

TARA: Then finish.

SFX: crowd goes quiet

MEERA: A bird.

MOHAN: She knows.

TARA: What colour?

MEERA: None. You refused paint because you said real sparrows did not wear festival colours.

TARA: How many wings?

MEERA: Two when you made it. One after you dropped it beside the stove.

TARA: Where did I hide it?

MEERA: You told me you threw it away.

TARA: Then you do not know where it is.

MEERA: I know you were lying.

TARA: That is not a place.

MEERA: You had soil beneath your fingernails. Red soil, not the grey dust near the wall.

TARA: There is red soil in three parts of the courtyard.

MEERA: You came inside with neem leaves caught in your braid.

TARA: There are neem leaves everywhere.

MEERA: Not in the kitchen. Not behind the water drum. Under this tree.

TARA: Where under the tree?

MEERA: Near the root shaped like a bent knee.

TARA: There are four roots.

MEERA: The eastern root. Three handspans from the trunk.

TARA: Why there?

SFX: crowd murmuring in surprise

MEERA: Because that was where I buried your first tooth after you asked whether a new one could grow from the ground.

TARA: Nobody knew about the tooth.

MEERA: You and I knew.

TARA: I never told Mohan.

MOHAN: You told me this morning.

TARA: About the bird. Not the tooth.

KAVITA: Has the location ever appeared in a police statement, school record or family testimony?

TARA: No.

KAVITA: Did anyone tell Meera before this test?

TARA: No. I buried it again myself.

MEERA: Again?

TARA: I checked that it was still there.

MEERA: You dug it up without me.

TARA: You were dead.

MEERA: I know what the register said.

TARA: No. You don't... you do not know what that did here. Every person brought food. Every person spoke softly. Then they went home.

MEERA: And you stayed.

TARA: I stayed with a brother who woke up calling for you. I stayed with Grandfather, who said questions were disrespectful.

DEVRAJ: Tara, I was ashamed and afraid.

TARA: I am not asking you.

MEERA: You stayed. I was not here. Both are true.

TARA: If I accept you, what happens when police call tomorrow? What happens when Kota calls? What happens when Raghav walks free?

MEERA: I answer the police. I go to Kota if the inquiry needs me. And every time, I tell you before I leave.

TARA: People promise that when they want entry.

MEERA: Then do not give me trust today. Give me one night.

TARA: One night?

MEERA: Let me sleep in my house. If you wake, I will be there. If Mohan calls, I will answer. Tomorrow, you decide again.

MOHAN: She can sleep beside us.

DEVRAJ: The house is hers too.

TARA: You do not decide that.

DEVRAJ: No. I don't. I have decided enough for all of you.

KAVITA: There is no police restriction preventing Meera from staying with her children. The identity certificate remains disputed, not cancelled.

TARA: So the choice is mine?

KAVITA: The personal choice is yours. The evidence has already made its own.

VOICE IN CROWD: The girl has tested her enough. Let the mother enter.

SECOND VOICE IN CROWD: We all watched her refuse the woman. We should hear her answer now.

TARA: Nobody speaks for me.

MEERA: They did that to you after I disappeared, didn't they?

TARA: Every day.

MEERA: Then take your time.

SFX: hoe dropped onto soil

TARA: Dig where you said.

MEERA: You want me to dig it up?

TARA: Three handspans. Eastern root.

MOHAN: I will help.

SFX: hands scraping dry soil

TARA: No. She knows the place.

MEERA: The earth is loose here.

TARA: Because I buried it again this morning.

SFX: clay clicking against a metal bangle

MEERA: I feel something.

MOHAN: Take it out, Ma.

MEERA: Your bird. One wing. And the beak is still too large.

TARA: You said it looked like a crow.

MEERA: You did not speak to me for two hours.

TARA: Three hours.

SFX: crowd laughing softly

MEERA: Then I owe you one hour.

TARA: You owe me more than that.

MEERA: I know.

TARA: Tonight, you sleep in the room with us. Not near the door. Mohan kicks when he dreams.

MOHAN: Only sometimes.

TARA: And if police call, you wake me before you go.

MEERA: I will.

TARA: Say it properly.

MEERA: I will not leave this house without telling you.

TARA: Everyone heard that?

VOICE IN CROWD: Everyone heard.

TARA: Good.

MEERA: Tara... may I touch you?

SFX: quick footsteps, tight embrace

TARA: You do not need to ask me that, Mother.
