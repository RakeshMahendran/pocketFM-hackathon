# Episode 10 — "Two Days Before"

SFX: police station gate clanging shut, crowded voices

NARRATOR: By the next morning, Devraj's apology had reached every courtyard. So had another fact. Kavita had found the five lakh rupees.

SFX: papers slapped onto wooden desk

RAGHAV: You froze my cousin's cooperative account without one question to me.

KAVITA: I am asking now. Explain the deposit dated the third of May, twenty twenty-one.

RAGHAV: It was for a tractor.

MEERA: You said the money was not paid to you. You did not say it was sitting with your cousin.

RAGHAV: Because it was not my account. That distinction is usually understood by adults.

SFX: crowd murmuring closer

DEVRAJ: Speak without insulting my daughter.

RAGHAV: Your family arrangements change very quickly, Devraj.

DEVRAJ: My mistake does not make your answer true.

SFX: chair dragged across concrete

KAVITA: Good. Everyone has finished performing. Sit down, Raghav.

RAGHAV: I prefer to stand.

KAVITA: Then stand beside the account statement, where everybody can see both of you.

SFX: ceiling fan rattling, low crowd murmur

NARRATOR: The public desk became a hearing without a judge. Villagers filled the doorway. Meera stood where she could see Raghav's face.

SFX: paper unfolded

KAVITA: Cooperative account ending four one nine two. Account holder, Raghav's cousin. Deposit, five lakh rupees. Date, third of May, twenty twenty-one.

RAGHAV: Correct. A commercial deposit is not a crime.

KAVITA: Who deposited it?

RAGHAV: A tractor buyer.

KAVITA: Name.

RAGHAV: The papers are old. I cannot remember every farmer who asks for machinery.

MEERA: But you remembered the date in front of the village. Eighteen months later. Before I told police the amount.

RAGHAV: I remembered because the accusation was repeated to me.

KAVITA: By whom?

RAGHAV: People talk.

KAVITA: Name one person.

RAGHAV: This is becoming theatrical.

SFX: one villager claps, then stops

MEERA: No. Theatre gives the audience a lie and tells them it is a lie. You asked us to call yours truth.

RAGHAV: I arranged a tractor inquiry. That is why the date stayed in my mind. There. Your mystery is finished.

KAVITA: You arranged it?

RAGHAV: I connected the buyer to my cousin. I did not receive the money.

KAVITA: So when you said you knew nothing about the payment, that was false.

RAGHAV: No. You are flattening a commercial transaction into a confession.

DEVRAJ: You knew the date because you arranged the deposit.

RAGHAV: I knew a tractor booking date. Meera later attached her story to it.

MEERA: My story? You watched Shahid count the notes twice. Bundles of five hundred. You took one bundle back because it was short.

RAGHAV: That is an allegation from a woman whose rental signature has already been called false.

KAVITA: Copied. The examiner called it copied, not false. Do not improve the evidence while quoting it.

SFX: crowd muttering angrily

RAGHAV: Then arrest the handwriting.

SFX: pen tapping paper

KAVITA: The account statement includes a deposit slip reference. Your cousin signed the slip. The depositor field contains initials.

RAGHAV: Initials are not a person.

KAVITA: They match the initials used by Shahid on two transport receipts.

RAGHAV: Many people share initials.

MEERA: Then bring Shahid here.

RAGHAV: You seem very eager to see the man you claim imprisoned you.

MEERA: I am eager for him to see that I came back.

DEVRAJ: And that she did not come back alone.

SFX: crowd erupts in overlapping protests

RAGHAV: Please. A father's courage, after signing his daughter dead, is not evidence.

SFX: wooden ruler striking desk

KAVITA: Quiet. Raghav, threats disguised as insults remain threats when twenty people hear them.

RAGHAV: I threatened nobody. I am concerned Devraj may damage his health by becoming emotional.

DEVRAJ: My health is not in your account.

KAVITA: The tractor. Manufacturer?

RAGHAV: A standard farm model.

KAVITA: Manufacturer?

RAGHAV: I don't... I did not handle delivery.

KAVITA: Model number?

RAGHAV: Ask my cousin.

KAVITA: We did. He said you chose the vehicle and brought the buyer.

RAGHAV: Then he is mistaken.

MEERA: Your cousin remembers you. You remember the date. Neither of you remembers the buyer.

KAVITA: Or the tractor.

RAGHAV: A booking can be cancelled.

KAVITA: Then there should be a refund.

RAGHAV: Perhaps there was.

SFX: paper turned sharply

KAVITA: There was not.

RAGHAV: Then the amount may have been adjusted against another vehicle.

KAVITA: Which vehicle?

RAGHAV: You have the account. You tell me.

KAVITA: I have every debit for the next six months. No tractor dealer received money from this account.

RAGHAV: Cash settlement.

KAVITA: Five lakh rupees in cash, with no receipt?

RAGHAV: Village commerce does not always ask police permission.

MEERA: Neither did you, when you took me to Kota.

RAGHAV: You went willingly.

MEERA: Then why did you remove my phone battery?

RAGHAV: I did not.

MEERA: Why did you tell Tara I had chosen another life?

RAGHAV: I did not speak to Tara.

DEVRAJ: You spoke to me. You said searching would shame the children.

RAGHAV: I offered practical advice to a distressed family.

MEERA: You offered us silence. My father accepted it. I will not.

KAVITA: Raghav, was the deposit connected to Meera?

RAGHAV: No.

KAVITA: Was it connected to Shahid?

RAGHAV: No.

KAVITA: Was there a tractor?

RAGHAV: Yes.

KAVITA: Then give me its chassis number by five this evening.

RAGHAV: That deadline is unreasonable.

KAVITA: You have remembered the transaction for eighteen months. Five hours is generous.

RAGHAV: And if an old office cannot find one number?

KAVITA: Then I ask the district transport office whether your tractor ever existed.

RAGHAV: You are treating clerical delay as criminal intent.

KAVITA: I am treating five lakh rupees as five lakh rupees.

SFX: rubber stamp striking paper, evening crowd murmuring

NARRATOR: At five, Raghav returned with no chassis number, no invoice and no buyer. Kavita returned with a certified search.

RAGHAV: A registration search proves nothing. Farm vehicles remain unregistered for months.

KAVITA: Not this dealer's vehicles. The dealer says no order was placed by you, your cousin or this account holder.

RAGHAV: Dealers lose records.

KAVITA: Banks do not lose the date stamped on a deposit.

MEERA: Say the dates together.

KAVITA: The money entered your cousin's account on the third of May, twenty twenty-one.

MEERA: And I disappeared?

KAVITA: On the fifth of May, twenty twenty-one.

RAGHAV: Coincidence is not evidence.

KAVITA: No. The account is evidence. The dates are evidence. Your changing answers are evidence.

DEVRAJ: And the tractor?

SFX: certified report placed on desk

KAVITA: The account received the five lakh rupees two days before Meera disappeared.

KAVITA: But the tractor was never registered.
