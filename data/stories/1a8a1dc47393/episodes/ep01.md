# Episode 1 — Dead in the Register

# Episode 1 — "Dead in the Register"

SFX: police station gate clangs, crowd murmuring

MEERA: Tara! Mohan! Please, call my children. Tell them their mother has come back.

SFX: wooden desk struck once

KAVITA: Stop there. Do not cross the desk until I ask you.

MEERA: Then ask quickly. I have waited... I have waited long enough.

KAVITA: Your name.

MEERA: Meera. Devraj's daughter. Tara and Mohan's mother.

SFX: crowd presses closer

VOICE IN CROWD: She says she is the dead woman.

SECOND VOICE IN CROWD: Call her father. Let him see her face.

MEERA: He will know me. Bring him here. Please.

SFX: metal gate slams

KAVITA: Constable, shut the gate. Nobody leaves, and nobody touches her.

SFX: walking stick taps across concrete

DEVRAJ: Move aside. I will settle this.

MEERA: Baba...

DEVRAJ: Do not call me that.

MEERA: Look at me. Properly. The cut above my eyebrow, I got it on your grain scale. You carried me home.

DEVRAJ: Many people have scars.

MEERA: Your left knee hurts before you sit. You hide money inside the red account book. Baba, please... just hold my hand.

SFX: crowd falls quiet

DEVRAJ: Do not touch me.

MEERA: One touch. You will know.

DEVRAJ: I buried my daughter. I placed wood on her pyre with these hands. You are an impostor.

SFX: uneasy whispering

VOICE IN CROWD: Her own father will not touch her.

SECOND VOICE IN CROWD: Someone trained her. Why else would she come here?

MEERA: I came for my children. Where are Tara and Mohan?

DEVRAJ: My grandchildren have already suffered. They will not be displayed before a stranger.

MEERA: They are not your shield. Bring them.

DEVRAJ: Sub-inspector, remove this woman before Tara hears this filth in the market.

KAVITA: No. She entered a police station and made an identity claim. We record it before anyone removes anyone.

DEVRAJ: The police recorded her death. Is your own record now a rumour?

KAVITA: One fact at a time. Meera, when did you last see your children?

MEERA: The morning I disappeared. Tara would not drink her milk. Mohan had fever... no, the fever began the night before.

DEVRAJ: All of that was discussed after the funeral. Anyone in Dharmapur could repeat it.

MEERA: Not this. Mohan called fever medicine bitter rain. Tara drew a moustache on the bottle so he would laugh.

DEVRAJ: Enough.

MEERA: You know that happened.

DEVRAJ: I know grief makes foolish people generous with information.

SFX: keys rattle at record-room door

KAVITA: Babulal, bring the death entry and the original identification file.

BABULAL: Madam, that file is sealed with the murder case.

KAVITA: Then open the cupboard, not the seal.

SFX: steel cupboard creaks open

BABULAL: If the woman is genuine, every connected page will have to be reviewed.

DEVRAJ: If. Please remember that word.

MEERA: Show me what you wrote about me.

SFX: thick register lands on desk

BABULAL: There is a death certificate, cremation acknowledgement, family identification and a station register entry.

MEERA: And my children were made to live under those pages.

SFX: register pages turning

KAVITA: Give me the date and status.

BABULAL: Fourteenth November, two thousand twenty-two. Meera, daughter of Devraj, recorded deceased.

MEERA: I am standing in front of you.

BABULAL: The station register still lists Meera as dead.

KAVITA: And four men are in jail for murdering her.
