# Episode 9 — My Daughter

# Episode 9 — "My Daughter"

SFX: village courtyard crowd and microphone feedback

MAN IN CROWD: Devraj, look at your daughter before you deny her again.

DEVRAJ: I will not answer a mob.

MEERA: Then answer me. Touch my hand and say who I am.

DEVRAJ: This public spectacle proves nothing.

MOHAN: It proved enough when you called Ma a stranger in front of everyone.

TARA: Mohan, stand beside me.

SFX: microphone stand adjusted

KAVITA: Nobody will touch or surround Devraj. He is here to correct his identification statement.

WOMAN IN CROWD: He sent four men to jail with that statement.

SFX: crowd voices overlap

MAN IN CROWD: And he would not even touch Meera at the station.

SFX: police whistle, single blast

KAVITA: Silence. Public humiliation will not repair public error.

MEERA: No. But public truth may begin to repair it.

DEVRAJ: You asked me to attend a formal verification. Conduct it formally.

KAVITA: I intend to. Stand at the microphone.

DEVRAJ: I can speak from here.

KAVITA: Your original identification was accepted publicly. Your correction will be heard publicly.

DEVRAJ: You have already marked the certificate disputed.

KAVITA: A red seal corrected a document. It did not correct your words.

SFX: slow footsteps toward microphone

NARRATOR: Devraj crossed the same courtyard where his refusal had made Meera a stranger.

SFX: microphone hum

DEVRAJ: The microphone is unnecessary.

KAVITA: The people at the back disagree.

JANKI: So does the family of the woman you identified.

DEVRAJ: The body has not been proved to be Kesar.

JANKI: No. But the hospital number proved my complaint was ignored.

KAVITA: Today's question is narrower. What did Devraj actually see?

DEVRAJ: I saw the body police brought from the accident site.

KAVITA: Could you identify the face?

DEVRAJ: The face was... it was severely burned.

KAVITA: That was not the question.

SFX: crowd gasps

DEVRAJ: No. I could not identify the face.

WOMAN IN CROWD: He never saw her face, yet he called it Meera!

KAVITA: One more interruption, and I will end the verification.

MEERA: Do not end it. I waited eighteen months for this answer.

KAVITA: Devraj, what feature did you rely upon first?

DEVRAJ: A dark mark on the hand.

KAVITA: You described it as Meera's tattoo.

DEVRAJ: That is what I believed it was.

KAVITA: Meera's tattoo is on the opposite hand.

DEVRAJ: I know that now.

MEERA: You knew which hand when I was a child. You paid for the medicine after it became infected.

DEVRAJ: The body was covered. The room smelled of smoke and chemicals.

KAVITA: What other feature did you rely upon?

DEVRAJ: The black thread around the wrist.

JANKI: Kesar wore one with three knots and a copper bead.

DEVRAJ: I did not see a bead.

KAVITA: Did Meera always wear a black thread?

DEVRAJ: She wore one during illness and after temple visits.

MEERA: Not every day. I had removed mine before I disappeared.

TARA: It was tied to the bedpost. I found it after she was gone.

DEVRAJ: You never told me that.

TARA: You never asked me. You told me Ma was dead.

MOHAN: Then you burned someone and said she was Ma.

DEVRAJ: Mohan, you are too young to understand that day.

MOHAN: I understood the day you would not touch her.

KAVITA: Devraj, did any doctor certify a facial identification before you signed?

DEVRAJ: No.

KAVITA: Did police have fingerprints or DNA linking the body to Meera?

DEVRAJ: Not then.

KAVITA: Did you see clothing you personally recognised?

DEVRAJ: Only burned cloth. Nothing certain.

KAVITA: Then state why you signed the identification.

DEVRAJ: Police said the accident matched the time she vanished.

DEVRAJ: They said the four men near the site had changed their stories.

DEVRAJ: They showed me the hand, the thread, and her name already written on a form.

MEERA: My name was already written?

DEVRAJ: In pencil, at the top. I thought they knew more than they told me.

KAVITA: That detail is not in your original statement.

DEVRAJ: Nobody asked who wrote the name first.

JANKI: They gave you a name, and you gave them certainty.

DEVRAJ: They asked whether it could be my daughter.

MEERA: And you turned could be into yes.

DEVRAJ: I was afraid.

MEERA: Of what?

DEVRAJ: Of taking an unclaimed body home. Of refusing, then learning I had abandoned you twice.

TARA: So you accepted the first answer that ended the waiting.

DEVRAJ: Yes.

KAVITA: State it without explanation. Did you identify the face?

DEVRAJ: No.

KAVITA: Was your identification based on certainty?

DEVRAJ: No. It was based on fear and grief.

SFX: crowd murmurs spread

WOMAN IN CROWD: Then Meera was shamed because her father was frightened.

DEVRAJ: Yes. She was shamed because I was frightened.

MEERA: At the station, I asked you to touch my hand.

DEVRAJ: I remember.

MEERA: You called me an impostor where my children could hear later.

DEVRAJ: I was trying to protect the decision I had already made.

MEERA: Not me. The decision.

DEVRAJ: Yes.

MOHAN: Touch her hand now.

TARA: No. Let Ma choose whether he may.

MEERA: You may touch my hand after you answer one question.

DEVRAJ: Ask it.

MEERA: Who am I? Say it so the whole village hears you.

DEVRAJ: Meera is my daughter. I was wrong to call her a stranger.
