# Episode 14 — Alive in the Register

# Episode 14 — "Alive in the Register"

SFX: courtroom handcuff chain dropping onto rail

COURT OFFICER: Remove their handcuffs. The murder charge has just lost its dead woman.

NARESH: Do not say that unless the judge has signed it.

SALIM: We want the order. All four names. Read every name.

SFX: wooden gavel, two strikes

COURT OFFICER: Stand quietly. The court is sitting.

JUDGE: This is the reopened matter concerning Meera’s identity, Kesar’s remains and the prosecution of four accused men.

MEERA: I request permission to stand with my children.

JUDGE: Granted.

MOHAN: Ma, they really took the chains off.

TARA: Not all of them. Their ankles are still locked.

JUDGE: Court officer, remove every restraint unless you have a separate lawful warrant.

COURT OFFICER: There is no separate warrant.

PINTU: Then use the key.

IRFAN: Wait... wait until the order. We have been promised doors before.

SFX: ankle lock clicking open

SALIM: He is right. Read it first.

SFX: thick case file opened

JUDGE: The DNA report confirms that Meera is alive and identifies the burned remains as Kesar.

JUDGE: The factual basis for alleging that these four men murdered Meera no longer exists.

NARESH: Then we leave today?

JUDGE: You will hear the full order without interruption.

NARESH: Yes, Your Honour.

JUDGE: The prosecution relied upon a body identification now disproved by DNA, a missing statement and inconsistent case records.

JUDGE: No lawful purpose is served by preserving a murder case for a person who stands alive before this court.

SFX: gallery murmurs rising

VOICE IN GALLERY: They spent all that time in jail for nothing.

SALIM: Say it louder. The village called us murderers louder than that.

SFX: gavel, single strike

JUDGE: Silence in court.

JUDGE: The murder proceedings against Salim, Naresh, Pintu and Irfan are quashed.

PINTU: Quashed means finished? Not bail?

KAVITA: Finished. The murder case cannot keep you in custody.

IRFAN: All four of us?

JUDGE: All four.

NARESH: No reporting every week? No police permission to leave Dharmapur?

JUDGE: Not under this quashed case.

SALIM: And my missing five-page statement?

JUDGE: Its disappearance will remain part of the departmental and criminal inquiry.

PINTU: We entered this building as four murderers.

SALIM: Then let the officer say how we leave.

COURT OFFICER: You leave as free men. There is no subsisting custody order against any of you.

SFX: gallery benches creaking

VOICE IN GALLERY: Salim and the others are innocent of Meera’s murder.

NARESH: There was no murder of Meera.

MEERA: I am sorry my stolen name became your prison.

SALIM: You came back alive. You did not put us there.

IRFAN: Will my brother receive protection now? I gave the court what I knew.

KAVITA: The protection direction remains active. Your release does not cancel it.

IRFAN: Good. Then open the gate.

SFX: another file placed before judge

JUDGE: Before release processing, the court will address the remaining evidence concerning Raghav.

RAGHAV: I came voluntarily. I have answered every proper question.

KAVITA: You changed your Kota statement after the travel register was requested.

RAGHAV: I corrected an error. Correction is not concealment.

KAVITA: Your former vehicle entered Kota with three occupants and left with two.

RAGHAV: A vehicle number does not identify passengers.

KAVITA: The east depot record identifies Kesar as the third occupant left near the depot.

RAGHAV: That is an interpretation.

KAVITA: The DNA report identifies Kesar as the woman later found burned at the accident site.

RAGHAV: DNA cannot place me at an accident I did not witness.

JUDGE: The court has not said it can.

RAGHAV: Then there is no ground to detain me.

KAVITA: There is also the five-lakh-rupee deposit made two days before Meera disappeared.

RAGHAV: A tractor payment.

KAVITA: No tractor was registered. No purchase invoice exists. No delivery occurred.

RAGHAV: Registration can be delayed.

KAVITA: For eighteen months?

RAGHAV: The buyer may have abandoned the transaction.

KAVITA: Then why was the money never returned?

RAGHAV: Ask the account holder.

KAVITA: We did. Your cousin states that you controlled the passbook and withdrawal slips.

RAGHAV: He is protecting himself.

KAVITA: The first withdrawal occurred forty minutes after your vehicle entered Kota.

RAGHAV: Coincidence is still legal.

MEERA: You knew the five-lakh date before I said the amount.

RAGHAV: You have repeated that performance enough.

TARA: Do not call my mother a performance.

SFX: angry gallery murmurs

VOICE IN GALLERY: He called her an impostor too.

DEVRAJ: No. I called her that. I was wrong, and I said so before this village.

DEVRAJ: Raghav supplied her name for the burned body before I reached the hospital.

RAGHAV: You signed the form, Devraj. Be careful where you move your shame.

DEVRAJ: I am not moving it. I am naming the man who placed the lie in front of me.

JUDGE: The movement sheet records that a duty officer wrote Meera’s name after receiving it by telephone from Raghav.

RAGHAV: That entry proves only that someone used my name.

KAVITA: The call originated from the number registered to your transport office.

RAGHAV: Many people used that office.

JUDGE: You may contest the evidence during investigation and trial. Today, the court considers preservation of evidence and risk of interference.

RAGHAV: I have remained in Dharmapur throughout.

KAVITA: You filed a changed statement, denied Kota and described the travel register as fabricated.

BABULAL: The register predates this inquiry. Its paper, ink and binding were verified.

RAGHAV: A clerk defending a neglected register is not an expert.

BABULAL: No. The forensic document examiner is.

JUDGE: The application for custodial arrest is allowed concerning the money trail, false identification information and connected disappearance inquiry.

RAGHAV: On suspicion?

JUDGE: On recorded financial evidence, contradictory statements and a documented risk of interference.

RAGHAV: Meera’s allegation remains an allegation.

KAVITA: And it will be investigated as one. Turn around.

RAGHAV: You are arresting the man who brought her to safety.

MEERA: You brought me to Kota. You did not bring me home.

RAGHAV: This village will tire of your story.

SFX: handcuffs ratcheting shut

KAVITA: Your hands, please. The court has been courteous enough to provide an order.

SFX: gallery erupting

VOICE IN GALLERY: Raghav is leaving in chains while the four men walk free.

SALIM: Look at me, Raghav.

RAGHAV: I do not take instructions from you.

SALIM: You sent us to that road. Your name stayed outside the file. Ours went inside a murder charge.

RAGHAV: Then enjoy your release carefully. Dharmapur has a long memory.

KAVITA: So does a recorded threat made inside a courtroom.

RAGHAV: It was advice.

SFX: side gate unlocking

JUDGE: Remove him.

RAGHAV: Meera, this does not prove your entire story.

SFX: courtroom side gate slamming

MEERA: No. It proves enough to stop you walking away today.

SFX: large register dropped onto public counter

NARRATOR: By noon, the hearing moved from the courtroom to the public records desk, where the first wrong had been written.

BABULAL: The court order directs immediate cancellation of Meera’s death entry.

MEERA: Not another disputed stamp. Cancellation.

BABULAL: Cancellation. The red line will cross the full entry, and the order number will be written beside it.

TARA: Let us see every word.

MOHAN: Can I stand on the chair? I cannot see.

SFX: wooden chair scraping

MEERA: Come here. I will lift you.

JANKI: And Kesar’s certificate?

BABULAL: Entered under her own name, with the DNA confirmation attached.

JANKI: Show the seal.

SFX: certificate unfolded

BABULAL: Here.

JANKI: Kesar. Not unidentified. Not Meera.

BABULAL: Kesar.

DEVRAJ: The same villagers watched me refuse my daughter’s hand.

SFX: crowd pressing closer

VOICE IN CROWD: We watched.

DEVRAJ: Then watch this too.

DEVRAJ: Meera is my daughter. I identified the wrong body, and I was wrong to call her a stranger.

MEERA: Father.

DEVRAJ: I will not ask you to erase what I did.

MEERA: Good. Stand beside me while they erase what the register did.

SFX: four papers stamped in sequence

SALIM: Our release orders are stamped.

NARESH: Four orders. I counted twice.

PINTU: The outside gate is open.

IRFAN: My brother is waiting beyond it.

KAVITA: Walk through together. The crowd must see all four of you leave without restraints.

SALIM: Meera, come with us to the gate.

MEERA: First, I need to watch my death end.

SFX: pen dragging slowly across register page

BABULAL: Red line through the old entry.

MOHAN: It is gone?

BABULAL: Legally cancelled.

TARA: Write the new entry clearly.

BABULAL: The court has supplied the final wording.

JANKI: Read it before you write.

BABULAL: One living person restored. One deceased person correctly identified. Four accused released from a case without a murdered Meera.

SALIM: Write it.

SFX: crowd parting, footsteps beginning

VOICE IN CROWD: Make room. Let all four men reach the gate.

NARESH: No chains.

PINTU: No guards holding our arms.

IRFAN: No murder case behind us.

MOHAN: Ma, they are free.

MEERA: Yes.

TARA: And you are not dead.

MEERA: Not in this house. Not in this village. Not on that page.

JANKI: My sister has her name.

DEVRAJ: My daughter has hers.

SFX: register page pressed flat

BABULAL: Final entry complete. I will read it exactly once.

BABULAL: The official register now records one final fact: Meera is alive, Kesar was the woman cremated, and all four accused are free.
