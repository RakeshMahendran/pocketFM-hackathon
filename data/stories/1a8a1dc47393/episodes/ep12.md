# Episode 12 — The Open Entry

# Episode 12 — "The Open Entry"

SFX: records office door banging open, ledger pages fluttering

RAGHAV: Remove Kota from my statement. Today. Before the court receives another copy.

BABULAL: You cannot withdraw a place as if it is a spelling mistake.

SFX: file dropped onto counter

RAGHAV: I can correct a statement taken under public pressure.

BABULAL: You signed every page.

RAGHAV: Then give me a fresh page.

BABULAL: This is a records office, not a washing stone.

RAGHAV: Your shop is on estate land, isn't it? Paperwork can become tiring when boundaries are reviewed.

BABULAL: My shop has nothing to do with this file.

RAGHAV: Of course not. I was expressing concern for your workload.

SFX: police boots entering room

KAVITA: Express it from the other side of the counter.

RAGHAV: Sub-inspector. Excellent. Tell your clerk that citizens may amend inaccurate statements.

KAVITA: He is not my clerk. And an amendment does not erase the first statement.

RAGHAV: Then attach my correction. Meera was never in Kota with me.

SFX: crowd gathering at doorway

MEERA: You stood before the committee and said I travelled there willingly.

RAGHAV: I repeated what I had heard. The crowd was shouting. Questions overlapped.

KAVITA: Nobody asked whether you had been to Kota. You volunteered details about a transport fee and the payment date.

RAGHAV: I was confused by the accusation.

MEERA: You were clear enough to call me a woman who abandoned her children.

RAGHAV: I withdraw that too.

MEERA: You cannot return eighteen months with one sentence.

RAGHAV: I am correcting an error. You should be pleased.

MEERA: I will be pleased when you say where you left me.

RAGHAV: I left you nowhere, because I did not take you anywhere.

SFX: recorder button clicking

KAVITA: Your correction will be recorded as a changed account. State the complete version.

RAGHAV: I met Meera near Dharmapur. I did not transport her to Kota. I did not introduce her to Shahid.

KAVITA: How do you know Shahid's name?

RAGHAV: It has been shouted through the village for days.

MEERA: Not in front of you.

RAGHAV: Villages have ears even when police pretend they do not.

KAVITA: Continue.

RAGHAV: I know nothing about a room in Kota. I know nothing about confinement. The five lakh rupees were a tractor deposit unrelated to Meera.

BABULAL: You did not bring the tractor record.

RAGHAV: The transport search was incomplete.

KAVITA: It covered every district registration for twelve months after the deposit.

RAGHAV: Then perhaps the buyer changed his mind.

MEERA: Yesterday, the tractor existed. Today, the buyer changed his mind. Tomorrow, will the account disappear?

RAGHAV: Your confidence is impressive for somebody asking the state to restore her name.

MEERA: The state marked me dead. I learned not to borrow confidence from paper.

BABULAL: Paper also marked the certificate disputed.

RAGHAV: After you interfered with it.

BABULAL: After police ordered a correction.

RAGHAV: You are very brave behind that counter.

BABULAL: I am... I am careful behind it. That is different.

KAVITA: And today he will remain careful while you sign your changed statement.

SFX: pen scratching across paper

RAGHAV: Gladly.

KAVITA: Read the last line aloud.

RAGHAV: I state that Meera was never in Kota with me, and any earlier statement suggesting otherwise was made under confusion.

KAVITA: Date and time.

RAGHAV: The twenty-fourth of November, twenty twenty-two. Ten forty in the morning.

SFX: pen scratching, rubber stamp striking paper

KAVITA: Sign.

RAGHAV: Finished.

SFX: metal file clasp snapping shut

BABULAL: No. Now I attach it to your first statement.

RAGHAV: Do that. The latest statement controls.

KAVITA: The court decides what controls.

SFX: chair pushed back

RAGHAV: Then I have nothing further to say.

BABULAL: Wait.

RAGHAV: For what?

BABULAL: You said you were never in Kota with Meera.

RAGHAV: That is what you just stamped.

BABULAL: Your vehicle number. Does it remain D H seven, C, eight two six four?

RAGHAV: Why?

BABULAL: Yes or no.

RAGHAV: That vehicle was sold.

KAVITA: After the dates under inquiry. Was that its number in May twenty twenty-one?

RAGHAV: Yes. Hundreds of people knew it.

BABULAL: I saw the number this morning.

RAGHAV: In which file?

BABULAL: Not a police file. An old travel register sent from Kota after a transport office audit.

RAGHAV: Travel registers contain copying errors.

BABULAL: You have not seen this one.

RAGHAV: I know how roadside clerks write.

KAVITA: Bring the register.

BABULAL: It is in the lower records room.

RAGHAV: Before you produce some mouldy book, remember that a vehicle number does not identify its driver.

MEERA: You recognised the number before he opened the book.

RAGHAV: He spoke my old vehicle number aloud.

MEERA: And you became frightened.

RAGHAV: Do not mistake irritation for fear.

KAVITA: Stay here.

RAGHAV: Am I detained?

KAVITA: No. You may leave. Your statement and vehicle number will remain.

RAGHAV: A courteous threat is still a threat.

KAVITA: Then you recognised the courtesy.

SFX: heavy footsteps, thick ledger thudding onto counter

NARRATOR: Babulal returned carrying a register so large he held it against his chest with both arms.

SFX: dry pages turning

BABULAL: Kota commercial travel register. Entry gate, east depot. The binding was never closed after the final page.

KAVITA: Why was it sent to Dharmapur?

BABULAL: Vehicle numbers were being compared with local permits. The packet arrived seven months ago. Nobody indexed it.

RAGHAV: So it sat unattended for seven months. Excellent chain of custody.

KAVITA: It is an administrative register, not seized evidence. We will verify it with the Kota office.

BABULAL: The page carries their seal and the attendant's initials.

KAVITA: Find the vehicle.

BABULAL: There are two entries. Arrival on the fifth of May, twenty twenty-one, at nine twenty in the evening.

MEERA: The day I disappeared.

RAGHAV: She knows the date. Anyone could have written the number later.

KAVITA: The ink crosses the page ruling. It was written before the next entry. What name is beside it?

BABULAL: No driver's name. Only vehicle number, arrival time and passenger count.

KAVITA: Passenger count?

BABULAL: Three.

RAGHAV: A commercial vehicle carries passengers. Astonishing.

MEERA: Raghav drove. I sat in the back. Another man sat beside him.

KAVITA: That is Meera's assertion. The register independently records three occupants.

RAGHAV: It records a number somebody copied from a passing vehicle.

BABULAL: The entry is open.

KAVITA: Meaning?

BABULAL: Arrival was recorded, but there is no departure on the same line. That is why I noticed it.

RAGHAV: Then it proves the vehicle never left.

SFX: pages turning rapidly

BABULAL: No. The departure was entered four pages later, after the attendant changed shifts.

KAVITA: Read it.

BABULAL: Vehicle D H seven, C, eight two six four. Departure, two fourteen in the morning. Passenger count, two.

MEERA: It arrived with three and left with two.

RAGHAV: Passenger counts are guesses.

KAVITA: Why is the departure line marked in red?

BABULAL: The vehicle was linked to an unclosed inquiry. A woman reported missing from the same depot corridor.

RAGHAV: Which woman?

KAVITA: You said this register was a copying error. Why do you need her name?

RAGHAV: Because you are building another performance around a red line.

BABULAL: There is a complaint reference in the margin.

KAVITA: Whom did the complaint concern?

BABULAL: A second woman connected in the complaint to an alleged buyer. She was last seen near the east depot.

MEERA: The same place Raghav said he never took me.

RAGHAV: This proves no person was inside my vehicle.

KAVITA: No. It proves your vehicle entered and departed Kota while you now deny being there.

RAGHAV: On different dates, perhaps.

BABULAL: The date is written on both pages.

KAVITA: Read the departure date.

BABULAL: The sixth of May, twenty twenty-one.

MEERA: And the missing woman?

BABULAL: Her complaint gives the same night.

SFX: ledger page pressed flat

KAVITA: The register shows Raghav's vehicle left Kota that night.

KAVITA: The same night the second alleged buyer disappeared.
