# Episode 7 — "The Missing Statement"

SFX: court corridor, handcuffs and crowd murmurs

COURT GUARD: Back. All four of you, back against the wall.

SALIM: Let me speak to the judge. Two minutes. That woman is alive.

COURT GUARD: You will speak when your case number is called.

NARESH: Our case number belongs to a dead woman. Bring her inside.

SFX: crowd surges against wooden barrier

WOMAN IN CROWD: Eighteen months in jail, and now the murdered woman walks into court.

SFX: wooden baton strikes barrier

COURT GUARD: Quiet. This is a court corridor, not the village square.

SFX: footsteps approach through crowd

MEERA: They were jailed in my name. I want to hear why.

SALIM: Meera... you are really standing here.

MEERA: Yes. Now tell me how my life became your murder charge.

IRFAN: Salim, stop. You don't know who is listening.

SALIM: Everyone listened when they called us murderers.

PINTU: And everyone watched when police dragged us from our houses.

SFX: file folder snaps shut

KAVITA: You will each get an opportunity. Do not coordinate your accounts here.

NARESH: Coordinate? We have shared one cell and one accusation for eighteen months.

KAVITA: Then eighteen months should have taught you to answer separately.

SALIM: My answer was separate. I gave it on the first night.

KAVITA: What answer?

SALIM: That Raghav sent us to the accident site.

SFX: handcuff chain jerks sharply

IRFAN: Salim!

COURT GUARD: No shouting between prisoners.

MEERA: Say it again. Clearly.

SALIM: Raghav told us where to go, before the police arrived there.

KAVITA: Do not add another word in this corridor.

SALIM: Madam, I already said every word. Police wrote it down.

SFX: courtroom bell, single strike

COURT CLERK: Case number forty-seven. State versus Salim, Naresh, Pintu and Irfan.

SFX: iron gate opens

COURT GUARD: Move.

SFX: chains and footsteps enter high-ceilinged courtroom

NARRATOR: Four men entered together. For the first time, the woman they were accused of murdering entered behind them.

SFX: gavel, two strikes

JUDGE: Who authorised this crowd? Clear the centre aisle.

COURT CLERK: The villagers are witnesses to the identity dispute, Your Honour.

JUDGE: They are spectators until summoned. Spectators remain silent.

WOMAN IN GALLERY: They called Salim a butcher in front of his mother.

JUDGE: One more interruption, and the gallery will be emptied.

SFX: papers slide across desk

KAVITA: The police request permission to place a new development on record.

JUDGE: Proceed.

KAVITA: Meera's death certificate was formally marked disputed yesterday.

SFX: handcuffs shake

NARESH: Then open these cuffs.

JUDGE: You will not instruct the court.

NARESH: No, sir. I... I only mean, there cannot be her murder if she is here.

JUDGE: A disputed certificate is not an acquittal. It is a reason to examine the record.

MEERA: Examine the part where they used my name without asking whether I was alive.

JUDGE: You will be heard after the case history is read.

SFX: case file pages turn

COURT CLERK: The prosecution alleged that the four accused killed Meera and burned her body near the east quarry road.

COURT CLERK: They were detained after being located near the accident site.

PINTU: Because somebody called us there.

JUDGE: Who called you?

PINTU: Salim said there was a crashed vehicle. He said people might need help.

JUDGE: Salim, where did you get that information?

IRFAN: Sir, may I speak privately before he answers?

JUDGE: No. The question was not addressed to you.

SALIM: Raghav came to the repair shed that morning.

SALIM: He said a vehicle had gone off the east quarry road.

SALIM: He told us to reach it before the traffic police took everything away.

KAVITA: What time?

SALIM: A little after six. The tea stall had just opened.

JUDGE: Was Raghav with you at the site?

SALIM: No. He gave the location, then left in his white pickup.

MEERA: Why did all four of you go?

SALIM: Pintu knew the road. Naresh had a tow chain. Irfan came because I asked.

NARESH: We found a burned vehicle, one body, and no living passenger.

PINTU: Police arrived within twelve minutes. Too quickly for that road.

JUDGE: How were the accused connected to the alleged victim?

SFX: pages flip rapidly

COURT CLERK: The original case diary says they were found beside the body and gave inconsistent accounts.

NARESH: We were questioned separately after being awake all night.

IRFAN: They kept asking which one of us lit the fire.

SALIM: I kept saying Raghav sent us.

KAVITA: Did you sign that statement?

SALIM: Every page. My thumbprint is below the last line.

JUDGE: Sub-inspector, locate the first-night statement.

SFX: heavy file lands on desk

KAVITA: Court clerk, give me volume one.

SFX: paper rifling

COURT CLERK: Arrest memos, seizure list, site sketch and medical forms.

KAVITA: Where are the individual statements?

COURT CLERK: After page eighty-six, according to the index.

KAVITA: Page eighty-six is followed by page ninety-three.

JUDGE: Check the supplementary volume.

SFX: second file opens

COURT CLERK: Supplementary volume begins with laboratory correspondence.

MEERA: Ask who removed seven pages.

JUDGE: The court will ask the questions.

SALIM: My statement was five pages. The others signed one page each.

PINTU: Seven pages.

IRFAN: Don't... don't say anything else, Salim.

SALIM: You stayed quiet for your brother. I stayed quiet because I trusted the file.

KAVITA: Clerk, read the index entry aloud.

COURT CLERK: Accused statements, pages eighty-seven through ninety-three, recorded on the night of arrest.

JUDGE: And those pages are not in either volume?

SFX: paper movement stops

COURT CLERK: No, Your Honour.

KAVITA: The original case diary contains the index, but Salim's statement is missing.
