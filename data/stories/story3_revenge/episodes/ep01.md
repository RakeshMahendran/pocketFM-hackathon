# Episode 1 — "Call Your Own House"

SFX: Bricks going down in stacks, four at a time, close. Beyond it, forty people breathing in a yard and nobody talking.

DEVLAL: Line up by house. Not by family — by house. Sanichari, you are one house even if you sleep in three.

SANICHARI: In the year the well fell in, I was two houses.

DEVLAL: In the year the well fell in there was no book. Move.

SFX: A wooden chair dragged over dry ground. It has one short leg and it knocks.

NARRATOR: Pay-out at teghra more happens once a season, in the open, so that everyone can see it happen. That is the point of doing it in the open. Remember that.

DHANIRAM: Birju. Come here, beta. Sit.

BIRJU: Sir, I can stand.

DHANIRAM: The munshi sits. That is what the chair is for. Take the book.

SFX: A cloth-bound ledger set down on a knee. Board covers. The spine cracks like a small dry stick.

BIRJU: (reading) This is — sir, this is blank. Page one is blank.

DHANIRAM: Page one is where you start. Read the houses out. Loud, so they hear it from their own man and not from mine. They have been asking for that for years. Today I am giving it to them.

SFX: Forty people going very quiet at once. A crow somewhere over the chimney.

LAKHAN: Let him read.

BIRJU: (steadying) Sanichari. Carry-forward, sixty-three thousand, two hundred.

SANICHARI: Say it again.

BIRJU: Sixty-three thousand, two hundred.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

DEVLAL: Next house.

BIRJU: Nepal Manjhi's house. Forty-one thousand, six hundred. Chhotu's house — fourteen thousand, and a fine of ten thousand entered in Chait for a hen.

SFX: A woman laughing once, wrongly, and stopping.

DHANIRAM: Keep going, beta. You are doing it very well. You have a good hand and a good voice. Now call your own house.

BIRJU: Sir.

DHANIRAM: Call your own house. Everyone else's was called.

SFX: Pages turning. Slower than they need to.

PHULWA: (from the back, quiet) Birju. Have you eaten?

BIRJU: Amma, not now.

PHULWA: You have not eaten.

SFX: One page. Then the sound of a boy trying to breathe normally and being heard doing it.

NARRATOR: He was twenty-one years old and he was the first person born in bharwara to finish class ten, and he had spent two months believing that this book was a job.

BIRJU: Phulwa's house. Carry-forward — eighty-nine thousand, four hundred.

SFX: Forty people not moving.

BIRJU: (smaller) Eighty-nine thousand, four hundred.

NARRATOR: Above the figure, on the line before it, there is a thumbprint. It is his mother's. The date beside it is 2009, and in 2009 Birju was twelve years old and could not yet write.
