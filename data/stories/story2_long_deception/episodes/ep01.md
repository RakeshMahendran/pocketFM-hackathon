# EPISODE 1 — "The List"

SFX: A loudspeaker with a dead cone. Two hundred pairs of rubber slippers on dry ground. A metal chair being dragged.

KISHORE: Roll one-four-one. Roll one-four-two. Come forward when your number is called and do not crowd the table.

SFX: Paper. The rustle of a big sheet held in wind.

RUPA: Sir. One-nine-six.

KISHORE: Wait.

RUPA: Sir, I ran the eight hundred in three minutes twelve. It is written on my card. Three minutes twelve seconds.

KISHORE: Next.

SFX: A man pushing forward through bodies. Slippers. Somebody's tiffin box falling.

HARIYA: Sir — sir, listen — she is my daughter, she has come four times, four times from Chandpura, the bus is forty rupees each way and it was only—

KISHORE: Stand behind the rope.

HARIYA: It was only that the form went by post, sir, and the post here is—

KISHORE: Behind the rope.

NARRATOR: Two hundred candidates. One list. The district had advertised eleven posts. Everybody standing on that ground had done the arithmetic on the bus and decided not to say it out loud.

SFX: The loudspeaker crackles. A name. Another name. Cheering somewhere at the back, one voice only.

KISHORE: Final list. Selected candidates report to the district office on Monday with original certificates. Everybody else, the ground is needed at four o'clock.

RUPA: Sir. My number was not read.

KISHORE: Then your number is not on it.

RUPA: Read it again.

SFX: Silence spreading outward. Two hundred people turning.

KISHORE: You want me to read the whole list again, for you, in front of everyone?

RUPA: Yes.

NARRATOR: She was ashamed the moment she said it. She said it anyway.

SFX: Chair legs. The sheet flattened on a table.

KISHORE: One-nine-six. One-nine-six is not here. One-nine-five, one-nine-seven.

RUPA: That is my number.

KISHORE: It is nobody's number. Girl, half this ground has paid somebody. Every year. They stand here with their chest measurements and their three minutes twelve and somewhere there is a man with their money in his shirt. Go home and ask him for it back. Ask your father. He is the one shouting.

SFX: Laughter. Not all of it. Enough of it. Somebody near the rope repeating the line for the people at the back who did not hear.

HARIYA: Rupa. Rupa, come. Come now.

RUPA: I want to see where my number went.

KISHORE: You want to see. Fine. Come here. Look. Struck. There — pencil line, initialled, dated in the margin.

SFX: A fingernail tapping paper.

RUPA: Read the date.

KISHORE: Eleventh of the eighth. Last year.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

NARRATOR: Eleven months ago. Her father had handed over the ninety thousand rupees in March.
