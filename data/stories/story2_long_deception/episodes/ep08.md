# EPISODE 8 — "The Storeroom"

SFX: The district office corridor at nine in the morning. A very different sound from the first floor — stone, height, a hundred pairs of feet, a bell somewhere.

RUPA: I want to ask about a temporary wing.

CLERK: Application?

RUPA: Not an application. A question. Under which department does an assisting station come? For confirmation of service.

CLERK: Assisting station of what?

RUPA: Of police.

CLERK: Then police.

RUPA: But which section handles the confirmation? Establishment, or—

CLERK: Madam, there are eleven counters. This is counter four. Counter four is arms licence renewal. Somebody has sent you to the wrong counter and the wrong counter will always answer you, that is the tragedy of this building. Go to counter nine.

SFX: Feet. A hundred of them.

CLERK 2: Counter nine is closed till the fourteenth.

SFX: Nothing. Long. Then the bell.

NARRATOR: She had taken half a day's leave to stand in a corridor and be sent from four to nine and find nine closed. She went back and took the fourteenth off as well, and wrote it in the leave book, and the leave book is a detail nobody in that building thought about for another six months.

SFX: The first floor. A storeroom — no fan, a door with a spring hinge, cardboard, the smell you can hear in how flat the sound is.

KAJRI: Why are we in here?

RUPA: Because you keep looking at the door.

KAJRI: I do not.

RUPA: Kajri. There is no Sahayak Thana on any list in that building. Counter four had never heard of it and counter nine is shut till the fourteenth, and on the fourteenth I am going back, and I am going to stand there till somebody tells me which department we belong to.

KAJRI: (no laugh, for once) Say that again.

RUPA: I am going to counter nine on the fourteenth and I am going to ask which department pays us.

SFX: The spring hinge. Not opening. Being held.

KAJRI: Rupa. Do not.

RUPA: Why?

KAJRI: Because it is a good eighteen thousand and you have a sister and a fifth of the month, and because — because it is very hard to get an office to admit you exist, that is all, it is a slow building, that is all.

RUPA: That is the third time you have said "that is all" in one minute.

SFX: A shutter of some kind. A crate moved. Feet leaving fast.

SFX: The bench. A full one. Somebody counting notes, and the counting stopping abruptly.

NAGESH: How much.

MAN'S VOICE: Six thousand. Sahab, I gave it to madam last Thursday also, four thousand, and she said the Sonbarsa matters are being settled directly and there is no receipt in Sonbarsa matters because—

NAGESH: Sit, sit. You are Bansi's brother.

MAN'S VOICE: Yes.

NAGESH: And on Thursday, madam said the Sonbarsa matters are settled directly.

MAN'S VOICE: Yes.

NAGESH: And no receipt.

MAN'S VOICE: She said in Sonbarsa there is a different arrangement because of the boundary with the—

NAGESH: Bhai sahab. Go downstairs. Have a tea. It is on the office. Bishnu will not charge you, tell him I said.

SFX: Steps going down the iron stairs. The fan.

NAGESH: Kajri.

KAJRI: Sahab—

NAGESH: How long.

KAJRI: (laughing, badly) Sahab, it is only Sonbarsa, it is my own village, they come to me because they will not open their mouth to—

NAGESH: How long.

KAJRI: Four months.

NAGESH: How much.

KAJRI: I do not have the—

NAGESH: You have the number. Everybody has the number. Nobody in the history of this district has ever not had the number.

KAJRI: Thirty-one thousand.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

NAGESH: Beti. Sit, sit. Do you know what the difficulty is? The difficulty is not thirty-one thousand. Thirty-one thousand is a scooter. The difficulty is that on Thursday a man in Sonbarsa said to another man in Sonbarsa: the office takes money without a receipt. And that second man told it in a tea shop. And you have heard me say what a tea shop is.

KAJRI: I will give it back. All of it, from my—

NAGESH: You will not give anything back, because giving it back is an admission and an admission is worse than the thing. You will simply stop, and the Sonbarsa people will be told, kindly, that the arrangement has been discontinued on the instruction of the office, which will make them feel that there is an office, which is a benefit.

KAJRI: Yes, Sahab.

NAGESH: And the last thing.

KAJRI: Sahab?

NAGESH: You have been in this room eleven months and you have never once brought me anything. Pushpa brings me something every week. Ratan brings me nothing, but Ratan is five hundred a day and nobody asks a five hundred a day man for anything. You are not five hundred a day.

SFX: A chair. The fan. Somebody breathing badly.

KAJRI: Sahab. Not today.

NAGESH: Beti, it is exactly today. That is what today is for.

SFX: Nothing. Long. Longer.

KAJRI: The Kachhwara girl.

SFX: Evening. The bench gone. A broom somewhere below. The fan being switched off — and the room without it, which is the loudest thing in the episode.

NAGESH: Beti. Sit, sit.

RUPA: I will stand.

NAGESH: Forty-four in nine days. I said forty and I got forty-four and I said nothing, and you were annoyed with me for saying nothing, exactly as I predicted. Now sit.

SFX: A chair. Wood on concrete.

NAGESH: Two days of leave. The seventh and the fourteenth.

RUPA: It is in the book.

NAGESH: It is in the book. That is what I like about you, everything is in the book. Beti — the district office. Counter four is arms licence, which everybody in this town learns the hard way, and counter nine is shut till the fourteenth, which is also true, and you will find on the fourteenth that it opens at eleven and the sir who signs sits till twelve. So go at eleven. Not at nine. At nine you will stand in a corridor for two hours with the arms licence people and you will come back angry and you will write only thirty files that week.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

RUPA: Who told you where I was?

NAGESH: Beti, this is a small town.

RUPA: Who told you.

NAGESH: You want a name. Names are a bad habit; once you start collecting them you have to keep them somewhere. Let me instead say it back to you, and you tell me whether it is worth a name.

SFX: A file being closed. The spring hinge of the storeroom door, far off, catching in a draught.

NAGESH: "I am going to counter nine on the fourteenth and I am going to ask which department pays us."

SFX: Nothing.

NAGESH: Have I got a word wrong?

SFX: Nothing. Long.

RUPA: I said that in the storeroom. The door was shut. There was one person in the room with me.
