# Episode 9 — The Newest Ink

SFX: Auction-room doors are bolted. Ewan pounds on them from inside.
EWAN: Nobody leaves with that ledger. Pippa, give me the loose schedule.
MACRAE: Mr Kerr, an accused treasurer does not command the room.
PIPPA: He does if the accusation was printed yesterday.

SFX: Paper is smoothed across the auction table.

BELL: Choose your next words carefully, Miss Shaw. Your wages are not a shield.
PIPPA: No. The ink is.
LUCIEN: This sale has already lost an hour.
EWAN: Families may be boarding your ship now.
LUCIEN: Then your English dispute should become concise.

SFX: Pippa uncaps an ink bottle.

PIPPA: Bell’s old ledgers use iron-black ink. This withdrawal entry is blue-black.
BELL: Ink changes with storage.
PIPPA: Not into the exact colour on Ross’s newest certificates.
MACRAE: Similarity is not proof.
EWAN: Ross mixed the last batch himself. Send for him.
MACRAE: The printer is not accountable to a clerk.
BELL: The investors will not accept uncertainty over a treasury theft. Bring Ross.

SFX: Macrae’s cane stops tapping.

NARRATOR: For once, Bell’s fear of public doubt was useful to Ewan.

SFX: Bolts draw back. After a short rush of corridor noise, a printer’s case is set heavily on the table.

ROSS: I was told a certificate had failed.
PIPPA: Your ink did not.
ROSS: That sounds less like praise than usual.
EWAN: Examine the withdrawal entry, the security schedule and your newest Ravenshore certificate.

SFX: Glass stopper lifts. Paper edges rub. Ross sniffs the ink.

ROSS: Same blue-black.
MACRAE: Many printers use blue-black.
ROSS: Not this mixture. Two measures oak gall, one copper salt, gum reduced because the engraved borders smear.
PIPPA: When did you mix it?
ROSS: Three days ago.
EWAN: The ledger dates the withdrawal in October.

SFX: Investors erupt in angry voices.

INVESTOR: Then the entry is false!
MILES: Uncle, you said Ewan emptied the fund months ago.
BELL: Silence.
ROSS: There is more. This sheet carries powdered chalk from my drying rack. It has not been inside a ledger since October.
PIPPA: It was inserted recently.
ROSS: Yes.
MACRAE: You are a printer, Ross, not a magistrate.
ROSS: A magistrate may know law. I know what leaves my press.

SFX: Pippa places the loose schedule beside a new certificate.

PIPPA: The cut edges match.
EWAN: Same paper stock?
ROSS: Same mould lines. Same batch.
EWAN: Supplied for the issue naming me Treasurer.
ROSS: Yes.

SFX: Ewan turns pages quickly.

EWAN: The theft page was made from the newest bond paper, written in ink mixed three days ago, and inserted into an older relief ledger.
BELL: That does not identify who inserted it.
EWAN: No. It proves I did not authorize a withdrawal in October on paper that did not exist until this week.

SFX: Several palms strike the auction tables.

INVESTOR: Withdraw the accusation.
SECOND INVESTOR: And tell us where the money is.
MILES: Uncle, say it plainly.
BELL: My nephew will not direct this bank.
PIPPA: Then the figures will. Show us the cash account supporting the relief ledger.
BELL: That account is subject to reconciliation.
EWAN: I reconciled it yesterday. There was no deposit.
BELL: There were expected revenues.
EWAN: Expected from what?
BELL: Land subscriptions.
EWAN: Amount received?
BELL: The treasury operated across several accounts.
PIPPA: Name one holding cash.
BELL: Miss Shaw—
PIPPA: Name one.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

LUCIEN: Bell, my investors have been locked in a room while your clerks discuss ink. Give them a treasury balance.
BELL: Cross-Channel subscriptions are not yet posted.
LUCIEN: I sent the subscription total two days ago.
BELL: Less commissions, printing, passage and agency charges.
EWAN: Balance?
BELL: There are liabilities in transit.
MILES: Balance, Uncle.

SFX: Bell shuts the ledger.

BELL: The Ravenshore treasury presently holds no available cash.

SFX: A wave of voices crashes through the room.

INVESTOR: Empty?
SECOND INVESTOR: You sold us government bonds backed by an empty treasury!
MILES: You accused Ewan to hide that?
BELL: I relied upon documents submitted under sovereign authority.
MACRAE: Take care, Bell.
BELL: I am taking care. The planted withdrawal does not belong to my bank’s hand.
MACRAE: A courteous man does not abandon his partners because a clerk recognizes ink.
BELL: A sovereign man does not require his banker to forge a clerk’s seal.

SFX: Macrae’s cane strikes the table.

MACRAE: The seal was required because Mr Kerr refused the dignity of his appointment.
EWAN: You ordered it forged.
MACRAE: I ordered treasury documents completed.
PIPPA: With his mother’s house as collateral?
MACRAE: A treasurer must offer confidence.
EWAN: My mother never pledged her house.
BELL: Then the security schedule is invalid.
EWAN: Say the accusation was false.
BELL: The evidence against you was manufactured.
EWAN: In front of everyone.

SFX: Investors quieten.

BELL: Ewan Kerr did not withdraw four thousand eight hundred pounds from the Ravenshore relief account. The account contained no such money. The ledger entry accusing him was planted.

SFX: A single investor begins clapping. Others join, not in celebration but demand.

INVESTOR: Return our subscriptions.
BELL: The remaining certificates will be reviewed.
LUCIEN: Your review does not concern my completed sales.
EWAN: Your ship must be stopped.
LUCIEN: Bell signed an undertaking, not I.
BELL: My undertaking covers treasury authorization. No ship can lawfully depart under Ravenshore papers.
EWAN: Then send the port order now.
BELL: Miles, bring the dispatch book.

SFX: Miles opens a narrow book. Pages flick.

MILES: The cancellation order is not here.
BELL: I signed it at Vey’s office.
EWAN: You signed a promise. Did anyone send the order?
BELL: My secretary was instructed.
PIPPA: By whom?
BELL: By me.
PIPPA: There is no dispatch number.

SFX: Distant pounding at the outer doors.

MESSENGER: Open for a port telegram!

SFX: Bolts wrench back. A messenger runs in, paper shaking.

MESSENGER: Cross-Channel sailing confirmation for Mr D’Arcy.
LUCIEN: Give it here.
EWAN: Read it aloud.
LUCIEN: It is private commercial correspondence.
AGNES: There are families on that ship.

SFX: Agnes enters, boots hard on the floor, and tears the telegram from Lucien.

AGNES: Departure recorded at eight-ten this morning. Passengers and land purchasers aboard.
EWAN: How many?
AGNES: More settlers than the notice listed.
BELL: That cannot be correct. I signed that no further expedition would leave.
PIPPA: The port stamp is correct.
EWAN: When did it sail?
AGNES: The second ship already sailed from France this morning.
PIPPA: It left despite Bell’s signed promise that no further expedition would depart.
