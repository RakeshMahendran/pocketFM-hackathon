# Episode 9 — The Green Cross

SFX: [en] Printing presses thunder. Street sellers shout over a London crowd.
STREET SELLER: [en] Second Briarhaven bond issue! New administration confirmed! Traitor clerk exposed!
EWAN: [en] Give me that sheet.
STREET SELLER: [en] One penny.
EWAN: [en] It carries my name.
STREET SELLER: [en] Then you should have bought the first copy.
SFX: [en] Coin clicks. Newspaper snaps open.
INES: [en] Read the official words.
EWAN: [en] “The rumours brought from Crownwater by a dismissed junior clerk have no bearing upon the lawful government of Briarhaven.”
INES: [en] Dismissed.
EWAN: [en] Gregor refused to accept my resignation. Now he has invented one.
INES: [en] Keep reading.
EWAN: [en] “A second issue of bonds will finance the capital’s restoration following storm damage.” Restoration. There was never a capital.
STREET SELLER: [en] The hall opens at twelve. Banker speaking. Soldier speaking. New lady official speaking.
EWAN: [en] What lady official?
STREET SELLER: [en] Buy tomorrow’s sheet.
SFX: [en] Ewan seizes the seller’s bundle. Papers scatter.
STREET SELLER: [en] Thief!
INES: [en] Ewan, here. Bottom of the announcement.
EWAN: [en] “Acting Superintendent of State Printing—Ada Wren.”
SFX: [en] The printing presses continue behind a sudden silence.
INES: [en] Is that the woman who gave you the plate?
EWAN: [en] Yes.
INES: [en] You said she vanished.
EWAN: [en] She did.
INES: [en] Then either they found her or she found them.
EWAN: [en] Ada proved every institution came from one workshop. She would never validate another issue.
INES: [en] You knew her before she disappeared. You do not know what happened after.
EWAN: [en] I am going inside.
STREET SELLER: [en] Not dressed like that.
EWAN: [en] I was invited by the people whose names Gregor printed.
SFX: [en] A folded petition opens.
INES: [en] One hundred and fifty-nine survivor signatures.
NARRATOR: [en] Six weeks after the Crownwater gate opened, the survivors’ testimony reached London. Gregor answered it with engraved invitations, a balcony and another two hundred thousand pounds of paper.
SFX: [en] Carriage doors slam outside a packed subscription hall. Coins and conversation fill the room.
USHER: [en] Invitation.
EWAN: [en] Petition from the surviving Briarhaven settlers.
USHER: [en] Petitions enter through the rear office.
EWAN: [en] The sale is taking place in this room.
USHER: [en] Subscribers enter through this door. Complaints enter tomorrow.
INES: [en] Fourteen people cannot complain tomorrow.
USHER: [en] Madam, I am certain your observation is sincerely intended.
SILAS: [en] Let them pass.
EWAN: [en] Silas.
SILAS: [en] Ewan. You have acquired a tan and a very expensive reputation.
EWAN: [en] Stop the issue.
SILAS: [en] I move paper. I do not stop weather.
EWAN: [en] Gregor is calling the absence of a city storm damage.
SILAS: [en] Buyers prefer repair costs to existential questions.
EWAN: [en] How many bonds have moved?
SILAS: [en] Eight hundred reserved before breakfast. Ninety pounds issue price.
INES: [en] The first issue was eighty.
SILAS: [en] Disaster has increased confidence.
EWAN: [en] You heard the Crownwater testimony.
SILAS: [en] I read a clerk’s allegation and an administrator’s cautious record.
EWAN: [en] One hundred and fifty-nine survivors signed this.
SILAS: [en] And several thousand investors did not.
EWAN: [en] The papers are forged.
SILAS: [en] Then you should not have made them so handsome.
SFX: [en] A ceremonial staff strikes the floor three times.
COMPERE: [en] Gentlemen, subscribers and friends of Briarhaven, please welcome the Cazique, Gregor Vale!
SFX: [en] Applause swells.
GREGOR: [en] My friends, I am told that a storm has made our young country controversial.
VOICES: [en] Shame on the liars! Long live Briarhaven!
GREGOR: [en] Storms are not unusual. Ambition is. Britain did not build her commerce by trusting every frightened clerk who returned from a difficult journey.
EWAN: [en] Fourteen settlers are dead!
SFX: [en] The crowd reacts. A gavel pounds.
GREGOR: [en] Ewan Pell. How welcome.
EWAN: [en] Say their number.
GREGOR: [en] You have interrupted a financial presentation.
EWAN: [en] One hundred and seventy-three sailed. One hundred and fifty-nine reached Crownwater. Say how many died in the country you sold them.
GREGOR: [en] The tropics are demanding.
EWAN: [en] There is no capital, harbour, hospital or government.
GREGOR: [en] You abandoned your assigned post before the administration could complete its preparations.
EWAN: [en] I stood on the mapped site. There was one ruined shelter.
INES: [en] I guided him there. I know that coast.
GREGOR: [en] And you are?
INES: [en] The person your guidebooks forgot to invent.
VOICES: [en] Let her speak! Remove them! What happened to the loan?
CYRUS: [en] Subscribers, the bank’s validation remains in force. The first loan was assessed upon the instruments submitted, not upon theatrical reports from unidentified traders.
EWAN: [en] Then identify one physical asset securing it.
CYRUS: [en] The territorial grant.
EWAN: [en] The boundary marker predates Gregor’s claimed grant by three years.
GREGOR: [en] According to whom?
EWAN: [en] According to the date cut into the stone.
GREGOR: [en] Stones are often reused.
EWAN: [en] So are your banknote numbers.
SFX: [en] Laughter and angry shouting divide the hall.
SILAS: [en] Gentlemen, subscriptions are still being accepted at the side tables.
EWAN: [en] Close those tables.
SILAS: [en] That authority does not belong to you.
EWAN: [en] It does not belong to any person in this room.
GREGOR: [en] There you are mistaken. Briarhaven’s civil administration has continued throughout this unfortunate disturbance.
EWAN: [en] Name one official who has governed from Briarhaven.
GREGOR: [en] Administration is not confined by geography.
EWAN: [en] Then show us the official.
GREGOR: [en] With pleasure.
SFX: [en] Gregor rings a small silver bell.
GREGOR: [en] You accused me of hiding the machinery of government. Today, you may inspect it.
EWAN: [en] Ada is not your machinery.
GREGOR: [en] Curious. I had not yet said her name.
SFX: [en] The crowd murmurs. Camera powder flares with a muffled pop.
EWAN: [en] Where is she?
GREGOR: [en] Mr Pell, your concern arrives after a long voyage. She remained in Britain and accepted responsibility.
EWAN: [en] Her father’s shop was stripped bare.
GREGOR: [en] Businesses fail. Talented daughters improve their circumstances.
EWAN: [en] Let me speak to her alone.
GREGOR: [en] An official of her rank cannot be privately examined by a fugitive clerk.
INES: [en] If she is free, ask her to come down.
GREGOR: [en] Threats are unnecessary. Ada has requested to address the subscribers herself.
EWAN: [en] Requested?
GREGOR: [en] You may find her choice disappointing.
SFX: [en] Balcony curtains draw apart above the hall.
VOICES: [en] There she is! The Green Cross! Make way!
EWAN: [en] Ada!
SFX: [en] Nothing. Long.
COMPERE: [en] Ada Wren, Acting Superintendent of State Printing, wearing the Green Cross of Briarhaven.
ADA: [en] Ewan Pell is the traitor who abandoned Briarhaven.
