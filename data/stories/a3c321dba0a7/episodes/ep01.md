# Episode 1 — The Wrong Number

SFX: METAL SEAL STRIKES PAPER. INVESTORS MURMUR. PRINTING PRESS CLATTERS BEYOND A THIN WALL.

EDWIN: [language=en] Change this number.

IAIN: [language=en] I cannot.

EDWIN: [language=en] I paid four hundred pounds for land, clerk. I will not hold certificate number three hundred and twelve when the gentleman beside me holds another three hundred and twelve.

IAIN: [language=en] That is precisely why I cannot change it.

SFX: PAPER SNATCHED FROM A DESK.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Let us be practical. Mr Bell, prepare the surgeon a clean certificate with a private serial.

IAIN: [language=en] A private serial will not appear in the register.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Then improve the register.

IAIN: [language=en] The register records copies issued from this office. The certificates are not independent grants.

SFX: THE INVESTOR QUEUE GOES QUIET. THE PRESS SLOWS, THEN STOPS.

ELSPETH: [language=en] Mr Bell, families are waiting. Must you discuss office language before subscribers?

IAIN: [language=en] They are buying the language.

EDWIN: [language=en] Answer cleanly. Do I own four hundred pounds of land or a copy of a London paper?

CASSIAN: [language=en] You own an estate recognised by the government of Poyais. Mr Bell is a junior copyist who has confused a printer’s serial with sovereign title.

IAIN: [language=en] Then show me the sovereign register.

TOMAS: [language=en] A printer prints what is supplied.

IAIN: [language=en] You supplied this run, Tomas. Three hundred certificates. One plate. Repeated numbers.

TOMAS: [language=en] I was supplied with approved instructions.

CASSIAN: [language=en] There. Even the printer understands his station.

VOICE IN QUEUE: [language=en] The clerk does not.

SFX: SEVERAL INVESTORS LAUGH.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Mr Bell failed in uniform and now fails at a desk. I apologise to every subscriber delayed by his incompetence.

ELSPETH: [language=en] There are recruiting agents from Edinburgh in this room.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Then they may report that I dismissed an incompetent clerk before he damaged another honest investment.

IAIN: [language=en] Dismissed?

CASSIAN: [language=en] Publicly and without ambiguity.

IAIN: [language=en] Good. I shall take my coat.

SFX: CHAIR SCRAPES. QUICK FOOTSTEPS. A KEY TURNS IN THE OFFICE DOOR.

IAIN: [language=en] Unlock it.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Confidential government papers are kept in this room, are they not.

IAIN: [language=en] Papers I copied under your orders.

CASSIAN: [language=en] And if any disappear after your departure, your creditors will hear that a former soldier stole from a foreign government. Your sister may find that difficult at the family door.

IAIN: [language=en] Leave Agnes out of it.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Certainly. Remain here until every sheet is counted, and I shall have no reason to mention her.

EDWIN: [language=en] You said he was dismissed.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Dismissal and departure are different administrative stages.

EDWIN: [language=en] I want my corrected certificate.

CASSIAN: [language=en] And you shall have it tomorrow, Doctor. With a number worthy of your confidence.

SFX: FOOTSTEPS AND LOW CONVERSATION RECEDE. THE OUTER DOOR CLOSES. PRINTING PRESS RESUMES.

IAIN: [language=en] Three hundred and twelve.

SFX: LEDGER PAGES TURN RAPIDLY.

IAIN: [language=en] Three hundred and twelve. Three hundred and twelve. Six grants, one number.

NARRATOR: [language=en] Iain had wanted respectability. Cassian gave him a locked room, a theft accusation waiting outside it, and six buyers who owned the same piece of land.

SFX: A FAINT KNOCK AT THE STREET-FACING WINDOW.

IAIN: [language=en] Who is there?

MESSENGER: [language=en] Letter for the Poyais office. From the coast.

IAIN: [language=en] The door is locked. Push it through the gap.

SFX: WINDOW RATTLES. AN ENVELOPE SCRAPES ACROSS THE FLOOR. WATER DRIPS FROM IT.

IAIN: [language=en] The paper is wet.

MESSENGER: [language=en] Salt water. The packet vessel nearly lost the mail.

SFX: MESSENGER’S FOOTSTEPS DEPART. IAIN BREAKS WAX. PAPER CRACKLES.

NORA: [language=en] To whoever still sells passages to Poyais: stop them.

NORA: [language=en] The first settlers found no capital.

NORA: [language=en] There was no port authority.

NORA: [language=en] There was no government at all.
