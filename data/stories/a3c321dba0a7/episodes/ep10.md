# Episode 10 — The Name Before the Man

SFX: [en] Subscription hall erupting—boots, angry shouts, reporters calling questions.
EWAN: [en] Ada! Look at me. Tell them who emptied your shop.
ADA: [en] Keep away from me, traitor.
GREGOR: [en] Mr Pell, the lady has answered you before witnesses.

SFX: [en] A gavel hammered against wood.
COMPERE: [en] The second Briarhaven subscription remains open. Eighty pounds at issue.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] Is she truly the state printer?
ADA: [en] I hold the commission and the Green Cross.
EWAN: [en] You taught me that a printer names the type, the stock and the press. Name them now.
ADA: [en] I do not submit to examinations from deserters.
EWAN: [en] Which press printed the bonds?
GREGOR: [en] Mr Pell, must your humiliation become repetitive?
INES: [en] Ewan, she is frightened.
EWAN: [en] She is precise when she is frightened.
ADA: [en] Ask my father’s account where the missing letters went.
SFX: [en] A metal printer’s sort drops and rings on the floor.
GREGOR: [en] Enough. Escort Miss Wren upstairs. Gently, gentlemen. She is an officer of state.
ADA: [en] The wrong letters always leave an impression.
SFX: [en] A door slams above; a key turns.

SFX: [en] Crowd thinning, coins counted into wooden trays.
EWAN: [en] Let me speak with her alone.
GREGOR: [en] No.
EWAN: [en] Then produce her commission register and her father’s account.
GREGOR: [en] You crossed an ocean to accuse me of lacking registers. Now you demand two more. Persistence can resemble character from a distance.
EWAN: [en] You bought her father’s debt.
GREGOR: [en] Debt is not bought, Mr Pell. It is relieved by a more patient creditor.
EWAN: [en] Where is her father?
GREGOR: [en] At liberty, provided his daughter continues to behave with professional dignity.
EWAN: [en] And the print shop?
GREGOR: [en] Consolidated. Idle machinery is a sorrowful thing.
INES: [en] Let her leave with us.
GREGOR: [en] Madam, London rewards those who recognise its doors. That one is not yours.
EWAN: [en] Ada was taken because you held her father’s debts.
GREGOR: [en] Miss Wren accepted an appointment. If you insist on using uglier words, perhaps ask why her father is not in a debtor’s cell.
EWAN: [en] I am asking her.
GREGOR: [en] Tomorrow, if she signs the third printing order, she may answer any appropriate question in my presence.
EWAN: [en] There will be no third order.
GREGOR: [en] A charming ambition. Your mother’s account has been discharged, I hear. That makes courage much cheaper.
SFX: [en] Gregor’s cane taps away across the boards.

SFX: [en] Narrow stairwell—pipes knocking, a guarded door rattling overhead.
ADA: [en] Ewan?
EWAN: [en] One minute. Tell me the truth.
ADA: [en] They purchased every note against my father. Rent, paper, ink, the Albion press. One hundred and twelve pounds.
EWAN: [en] Who purchased them?
ADA: [en] A private account controlled by Gregor. The men came before opening, emptied the shop and brought the plates here. They said my father would be arrested before noon unless I came with them.
EWAN: [en] Why accuse me today?
ADA: [en] Each public appearance removes ten pounds from his account. Each refusal restores twenty.
EWAN: [en] Come down now.
ADA: [en] There are two guards and my father’s signed confession is in Gregor’s desk.
EWAN: [en] Confession to what?
ADA: [en] Counterfeiting every Briarhaven instrument. If I run, they file it.
EWAN: [en] I can take the plates.
ADA: [en] Plates prove one workshop. The master ledger proves who ordered every sheet and received every payment.
EWAN: [en] Where?
ADA: [en] Records room. Iron cupboard. Lower lock sticks after the second turn.
EWAN: [en] The key?
ADA: [en] They searched me.
SFX: [en] Fingernail scraping wax from beneath a stair rail.
ADA: [en] I pressed it here when they took my bag. The wrong letters leave an impression.
GUARD: [en] Who is below?
ADA: [en] A traitor begging forgiveness.
INES: [en] Then let us disappoint him elsewhere.
SFX: [en] Ewan and Ines hurry down the stairs as the guard’s bolt draws back.

NARRATOR: [en] The subscription hall closed at eleven. Gregor left by the front entrance with three investors and two reporters. Ewan returned through the coal hatch.

SFX: [en] Coal scraping; iron hatch lowering softly.
INES: [en] You said you knew this building.
EWAN: [en] I know the documents. Buildings are less orderly.
INES: [en] The guard turns every seven minutes. We have five.
EWAN: [en] I need the ledger and Ada’s father’s confession.
INES: [en] You may carry one book quietly. Choose before the guard chooses for you.
SFX: [en] Footsteps overhead; a distant key ring.
EWAN: [en] Records room should be beyond the certificate press.
INES: [en] Should be?
EWAN: [en] The floor plan was printed in a Briarhaven prospectus.
INES: [en] Then it may contain a cathedral.
SFX: [en] A latch lifts; door opens.

SFX: [en] Printing workshop—rollers ticking as they cool, paper rustling in a draught.
EWAN: [en] Ada’s press.
INES: [en] And the shop’s type cases.
EWAN: [en] Constitution headings. Bank charter border. Military commissions. All here.
INES: [en] Do not prove what we already proved. Find the cupboard.
SFX: [en] Heavy footsteps approaching beyond the room.
EWAN: [en] Iron door, left wall.
SFX: [en] Wax mould pressed around a key blank; lock resists.
EWAN: [en] First turn.
SFX: [en] Lock clicks, jams.
EWAN: [en] Second turn. It is stuck.
INES: [en] Ada said after the second turn.
EWAN: [en] I heard her.
INES: [en] Your shame is loud enough without speaking it.
SFX: [en] Ewan forces the key backward a fraction; the cupboard opens.
EWAN: [en] Master subscription ledger. Land grants, bonds, recruiting receipts—indexed by purchaser.
INES: [en] Take it.
EWAN: [en] Wait. A leaf has been cut out near the front.
INES: [en] We have forty seconds.
EWAN: [en] Ada’s father’s confession is not here.
INES: [en] Then Gregor keeps it closer.
SFX: [en] Door handle tested from outside.
GUARD: [en] Records room?
INES: [en] Now, Ewan.
SFX: [en] Ledger shoved beneath a coat; cupboard closes.

SFX: [en] Coal hatch bursts open; street cart rattling past.
GUARD: [en] Stop there!
SFX: [en] Running footsteps, whistle blasts, cart wheels splashing through a gutter.
INES: [en] Into the alley!
EWAN: [en] The book is tearing my coat.
INES: [en] Better the coat than your neck.
SFX: [en] They turn sharply; pursuing boots continue past.

SFX: [en] Cellar door barred; ledger dropped onto a packing crate.
INES: [en] Read enough to know we stole the right one.
EWAN: [en] First bond issue. Seven hundred subscriptions. Elspeth’s recruiting payments. Cyrus’s validation fee.
INES: [en] And Gregor?
EWAN: [en] Private withdrawals beside every institutional payment.
SFX: [en] Pages turning faster, then stopping.
INES: [en] What is it?
EWAN: [en] The ledger contains a payment to me dated two weeks before I was supposedly hired.
