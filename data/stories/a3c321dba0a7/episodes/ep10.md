# Episode 10 — The Man Who Came Back

SFX: Heavy doors crash open. Wet boots cross a marble floor. A wooden cane strikes once.

THOMAS: Which one of you is Ewan?

EWAN: I am. Thomas—how many came back?

THOMAS: Not enough. Bring me Bell.

SFX: A crowded registry chamber erupts in whispers.

BELL: This is a petition hearing, not a dockside spectacle.

THOMAS: I crossed an ocean to make it a spectacle.

VEY: State your name and your business for the record.

THOMAS: Thomas. Surveyor. Settler. Survivor of Ravenshore.

SFX: Chairs scrape. Several people stand.

AGNES: Thomas!

THOMAS: Agnes. You reached London.

AGNES: With eleven others. Where are the rest?

THOMAS: Some are in coastal villages. Some took passage north. Twenty-three died before help reached us.

SFX: The chamber falls silent.

VEY: Those deaths will be entered beside the existing complaint.

BELL: Sir, before such figures are repeated publicly, they should be verified.

THOMAS: I buried six myself. Shall I fetch them for verification?

VOICE IN GALLERY: Answer him, Bell!

ANOTHER VOICE: Twenty-three people!

VEY: Order. Thomas, tell this chamber what you found at the advertised capital.

THOMAS: No capital. No harbour. No government house. No bank. No streets. Mud, jungle and paper money that no one on the coast would accept.

BELL: The settlement may have been established at another coordinate.

THOMAS: Then your maps sent us sixty miles from it.

MACRAE: New countries suffer difficult beginnings. You were engaged as a surveyor, were you not? Difficult ground was your opportunity.

THOMAS: I was engaged to survey farms outside a city. There was no city.

MACRAE: A city is built by men who do not surrender to discomfort.

THOMAS: Fever is not discomfort. An empty medicine chest is not discomfort. A child dying under a printed proclamation is not discomfort.

SFX: Angry voices rise throughout the gallery.

VEY: Thomas, did any person in Bell’s office warn you that the government’s possessions had not been verified?

THOMAS: One man asked questions.

BELL: Many questions were asked. My office conducted extensive—

THOMAS: Not you.

SFX: Thomas’s cane crosses the floor and stops before Ewan.

THOMAS: This clerk asked where the farms were surveyed. He asked what wages had been deposited. He asked whether there was a harbour, a hospital or one completed government building.

EWAN: And then I wrote your deposit into the ledger.

THOMAS: Because they put you beside Mara and called you a government clerk.

EWAN: I should have refused.

THOMAS: You were ashamed then. Be useful now.

VEY: Useful in what capacity?

THOMAS: As the office witness for the survivors’ petition.

BELL: Ewan is a copying clerk. He has no standing to represent investors, settlers or this institution.

THOMAS: He has more standing than the men who sold us a country they never saw.

VOICE IN GALLERY: Let the clerk speak!

MILES: Uncle, everyone can hear you trying to silence him.

BELL: Miles, kindly remember whose name brought you into this room.

MILES: I remember whose bond carried a duplicate number too.

SFX: A wave of shocked murmurs.

VEY: Ewan, step forward.

EWAN: I prepared maps, ledgers, land grants and bonds. I found duplicated serials. I found the expedition was sent without office-supplied food, medical chests, axes or saws. I found the relief fund never contained the four thousand eight hundred pounds written in its ledger.

THOMAS: Put his name on our petition.

AGNES: Put it above Bell’s.

VEY: The order of names is not a matter for the gallery.

AGNES: My family’s shop was taken in the order Bell preferred.

VEY: That matter is before us as well.

SFX: Papers are unfolded across a long desk.

PIPPA: Sir, these are the auction transfers. The investor proceeds entered Bell’s subscription account, then moved into an account controlled by Macrae before the certificates were delivered.

MACRAE: Those funds met sovereign expenses.

PIPPA: Printing, commissions and passage charges consumed part. The remaining recoverable balance is two thousand one hundred and forty pounds.

BELL: Recoverable from whom?

PIPPA: From you. The receiving account was opened on your introduction and cleared through your bank.

BELL: That is an accounting interpretation.

EWAN: It is your signature.

VEY: Let us avoid an extended inquiry into how a banker’s signature reached a sovereign account. Bell, you will fund a settlement in the amount identified by your clerk.

BELL: My clerk appears unusually ambitious today.

PIPPA: Two thousand one hundred and forty pounds, sir. I checked it three times.

VEY: The settlement will first redeem property deeds pledged by settler families. The remainder will fund survivor relief.

BELL: And if I decline?

VEY: Your bank premises are on a licence reviewed annually, are they not?

SFX: Nothing. Long.

BELL: I shall not permit innocent families to suffer over a misunderstanding. Prepare the draft.

VOICE IN GALLERY: He is paying!

ANOTHER VOICE: Say why!

VEY: For the record, Bell has agreed to fund the settlement.

SFX: A pen scratches. A metal cash box opens.

NARRATOR: Before the chamber emptied, Bell’s money did what Ravenshore money never had. It purchased something real.

SFX: A thick document slides across wood.

VEY: Agnes, the settlement has redeemed the deed to your family’s shop. No Ravenshore creditor retains a claim over it.

AGNES: Read the address.

VEY: Must I?

AGNES: They read it aloud when they took the deed. Read it aloud when you return it.

VEY: The Calderwick shop and rooms above it are restored to Agnes and her family, free of the Ravenshore pledge.

SFX: The gallery applauds.

AGNES: Ewan, take it.

EWAN: It belongs to you.

AGNES: I want the man who wrote away my seventeen pounds to hand back what survived.

SFX: Paper passes from Vey to Ewan, then to Agnes.

EWAN: Your shop is yours again.

AGNES: And Thomas’s names?

VEY: The survivors’ petition will be accepted with Thomas as principal witness and Ewan as the office witness.

THOMAS: Say it plainly.

VEY: Ewan is the only worker from the Ravenshore office who attempted to establish what the country actually possessed before the settlers departed.

SFX: Applause grows louder.

BELL: This performance will not alter the legal character of the bonds.

PIPPA: It altered the room.

SFX: The rear doors open. A staff strikes the floor twice.

THOMAS: Tawis. You made the crossing.

TAWIS: I have arrived with the coastal record. It shows that no Ravenshore government has ever been recognized on the land Macrae claims.
