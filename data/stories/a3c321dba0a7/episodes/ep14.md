# Episode 14 — The Cancellation Register

SFX: A registry stamp crashes onto paper three times.

REGISTRY CLERK: Bond register. Land-grant register. Creditor’s writ. Which one do I close first?

EWAN: All of them.

MACRAE: You have no authority to close any.

VEY: He has the authority you printed for him.

SFX: The crowded public gallery breaks into sharp laughter.

NARRATOR: On the twenty-fourth of April 1835, Ewan returned to the registry carrying four things: a grant, a duplicate register, a survivor’s testimony and a coastal record.

VEY: This is the final sitting. Mr Kerr, establish the chain without speeches.

EWAN: The land claim came first. The printed government made it appear lawful. Bell’s bank made the bonds appear secure. The bond sale paid the ship. The ship carried settlers to a capital that did not exist.

MACRAE: A clerk’s arrangement of papers is not evidence.

EWAN: Then we will take each paper separately.

SFX: A heavy ledger opens.

EWAN: Pippa, serial R-4187.

PIPPA: Entered once for an investor who paid in full, then entered again eleven days later for Miles Orrock.

MILES: I was told the damaged number was a copying error.

EWAN: I was blamed for that error in front of you.

MILES: Yes.

EWAN: Did you see me print either certificate?

MILES: No.

EWAN: Did you see me initial yours?

MILES: No.

MILES: I laughed when Bell called you an incompetent provincial clerk. I was wrong.

SFX: Murmurs of approval.

MACRAE: One duplicated number cannot void a country.

EWAN: No. But it proves the register was used twice. Now the grant.

SFX: A wax seal cracks.

EWAN: Sixty acres, issued in April 1834 under Macrae’s authority. It belongs to the final series retained by Ross.

ROSS: Same paper mould, same engraved border, same seal plate and same numbered run. Sixty certificates were printed.

VEY: How many were issued?

ROSS: My invoice records all sixty as delivered.

MACRAE: Delivery to an office is not sale.

BELL: My sealed packet contained one completed grant. My later subscription list contains forty-three purchaser entries tied to that run.

MACRAE: You are reading an internal proposal as a transaction.

BELL: Seventeen deposits cleared through my bank. I will provide the vouchers.

VOICE IN GALLERY: Seventeen more families?

ANOTHER VOICE: After the deaths were known?

MACRAE: Investments continued because informed men rejected the hysteria surrounding one difficult expedition.

AGNES: My children slept in mud while your office sold another sixty acres at a time.

THOMAS: Twenty-three graves were not hysteria.

SFX: The gallery pounds walking sticks against the floor.

VEY: Order.

MACRAE: Sir Alistair, if this becomes a theatre, respectable institutions may find their own conduct examined beside mine.

VEY: That is an unusually courteous warning.

BELL: Examine mine. I have already admitted it.

VEY: Mr Hale, give the registry the result of the first expedition.

THOMAS: We reached the advertised coordinates on the ninth of December 1822. There was no capital, harbour, government house, bank, street or storehouse. The office had supplied government papers, notes and uniforms. It had supplied no axes, saws, medical chest or food stores.

MACRAE: Conditions changed during the crossing.

THOMAS: A city did not disappear during our crossing. It had never been built.

AGNES: Enter the names.

VEY: They remain preserved in the public complaint book, including the twenty-three reported dead.

AGNES: Then enter what happened to the second ship as well. We were told it sailed after Bell promised none would leave.

VEY: Tawis, your supplemental coastal return.

SFX: A bound record is placed on the desk.

TAWIS: The second ship was refused entry to the claimed territory. Coastal officers directed it to a licensed harbour. Its passengers were removed, provisioned and returned across the Atlantic. No settlement from that ship was established in Ravenshore.

AGNES: So they came home?

TAWIS: The passenger return records every settler from that ship as repatriated.

SFX: Agnes releases a long, shaking breath.

EWAN: And Macrae’s authority over the land?

TAWIS: The coastal record contains no recognized Ravenshore government, no recognized Cazique, no cession to Macrae and no authority for his agents to sell or settle the claimed land.

MACRAE: Recognition by neighbouring authorities is not the source of sovereignty.

TAWIS: Nor is an Edinburgh printing press.

SFX: Public applause fills the chamber.

VEY: The coastal record is admitted. The registry finds no recognized territorial authority supporting the bonds or grants.

EWAN: Then the April 1834 issue fails.

VEY: Unless its treasury witness authenticates Macrae’s authority under the clause Macrae approved.

MACRAE: Mr Kerr accepted wages from my office for years. His refusal now is self-serving.

EWAN: I copied papers because my mother depended on the money I sent home. You used that shame to keep me quiet.

MACRAE: And now you would make her homeless by destroying the security attached to your office debts.

EWAN: The collateral entry naming her house was planted in a false relief ledger.

BELL: My bank has withdrawn every claim arising from that entry.

VEY: Registry clerk, mark the collateral against Elspeth Kerr’s house discharged. No creditor may enforce it.

SFX: A stamp falls.

REGISTRY CLERK: Collateral discharged.

EWAN: And the writ naming me co-director?

CREDITOR’S COUNSEL: The writ relies on initials appearing on the eight hundred certificates.

PIPPA: Those initials were applied before Ewan received or inspected the auction shipment.

ROSS: They are transfer copies from a specimen signature, not initials written by hand.

BELL: Ewan did not authorize them. I permitted the certificates to be sold regardless.

VEY: The creditor’s claim against Ewan Kerr is dismissed. The registry will record that his initials were forged and that he was neither director nor treasurer of a valid Ravenshore government.

SFX: The gallery rises in loud applause.

MILES: Mr Kerr, I bought the first duplicated bond. I believed the banker, the uniform and the seal. I did not believe the clerk who checked the number.

EWAN: Believe the register next time.

MILES: I shall.

AGNES: What of the shop deed and the investors’ money?

BELL: Your family’s shop remains yours, free of the Ravenshore pledge. The recoverable funds are already in the settlement account.

PIPPA: Two thousand one hundred and forty pounds, less nothing for commission.

BELL: Less nothing.

NARRATOR: Before the registry closed, every approved payment voucher cleared. The recovered funds went to the local investors and survivor settlement, not to Bell’s commissions.

SFX: The hearing-room clock strikes four. Ledgers close throughout the chamber.

VEY: One matter remains. These documents cannot be destroyed. The duplicate register, the survivor deposit lists, the coastal return and every cancelled certificate will form a permanent fraud archive.

MACRAE: An archive maintained by the government that tolerated the sale.

VEY: Precisely. We shall not improve our appearance by losing the evidence.

BELL: Name Ewan as keeper.

EWAN: I was the clerk whose writing made the papers look complete.

THOMAS: You were also the clerk who asked what the country possessed.

AGNES: And the one who wrote our names down when everyone else preferred totals.

PIPPA: You found the first duplicate. Finish the entry.

VEY: Ewan Kerr is publicly cleared of theft, false collateral, treasury direction and liability for the forged certificates. He is appointed keeper of the Ravenshore fraud archive.

SFX: Sustained applause. A registry book is carried to Ewan.

VEY: Mr Kerr, the final issue requires its named witness. Do you affirm that Macrae held lawful authority over the claimed land?

EWAN: No.

VEY: Do you authenticate the Ravenshore treasury, register or sovereign seal?

EWAN: No.

VEY: Do you affirm that any Ravenshore bond or land grant in this registry remains valid?

EWAN: No.

SFX: Ewan signs. The registry clerk turns the book toward the public.

REGISTRY CLERK: The public registry records every Ravenshore bond and land grant as void.

VEY: Ewan Kerr’s name is entered as the official witness to their cancellation.
