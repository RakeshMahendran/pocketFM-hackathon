# Episode 14 — Keeper of the Evidence

SFX: [en] The hearing doors burst open. Hundreds of claim papers crackle in raised hands.

VOICE AMONG SETTLERS: [en] Read every name!

ANOTHER VOICE: [en] Read every pound!

HEARING CHAIR: [en] Ewan Pell, the creditors demand the complete ledger.

EWAN: [en] Then remove Gregor’s guard from Ada and I will read it complete.

GREGOR: [en] She remains an officer of Briarhaven by her own public declaration.

ADA: [en] I withdraw that declaration.

SFX: [en] A Green Cross medal tears from cloth and strikes the floor.

NARRATOR: [en] Before the doors opened that morning, an official clerk had prepared a sealed draft. It suspended every authority derived from Briarhaven until the hearing determined whether Briarhaven existed. No banker, settler or self-styled ruler had yet seen it. Remember that.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] Ada, were you held through a document concerning your father?

ADA: [en] Gregor held his signed confession to counterfeiting. If I refused the title, he promised to give it to the authorities without the printing orders attached.

GREGOR: [en] I promised nothing. I merely explained what an honest prosecution might require.

EWAN: [en] The orders are in the master ledger.

ADA: [en] Not all of them.

SFX: [en] Cloth lining rips. A folded sheet is drawn from behind the medal.

EWAN: [en] The missing front leaf.

ADA: [en] I removed it before your entry into the records room. I hid it inside the Green Cross because Gregor searched my pockets, not his own decoration.

GREGOR: [en] Theft confessed in public. How useful.

ADA: [en] Very useful.

SFX: [en] The leaf unfolds.

ADA: [en] It contains the first manufacturing order. Constitution, bank charter, military commissions, seals and numbered notes. All ordered by Gregor. My father signed only as printer receiving instructions.

EWAN: [en] And the first payment column?

ADA: [en] Names were assigned before the workers were hired. Future clerks were entered as recipients so private withdrawals could later be blamed upon them.

EWAN: [en] That is why the ledger records a payment to me two weeks before I began work.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] Is the order signed?

ADA: [en] By Gregor, with Cyrus approving use of the bank’s rooms for delivery.

CYRUS: [en] I approved delivery. I did not approve counterfeiting.

ADA: [en] You approved delivery of five hundred banknotes carrying the same serial number.

SFX: [en] The crowd shouts. A gavel strikes repeatedly.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] Officers, remove the guard from Ada. Her father’s confession and this order will be preserved together. No prosecution founded upon one may conceal the other.

SFX: [en] A sword belt and keys are taken away. Ada exhales.

ADA: [en] Ewan, read the ledger.

SFX: [en] A thick book is set upon the lectern.

EWAN: [en] Land account. Edinburgh. Sixty acres sold to one family for forty pounds. The same sixty acres sold again in Glasgow for fifty-two.

SETTLER: [en] That is my certificate!

EWAN: [en] Your name is here. It will enter the loss register.

EWAN: [en] Recruiting account. Elspeth, commissions on thirty-eight family deposits.

ELSPETH: [en] I took the payments.

EWAN: [en] Did you see an overseas issuing register?

ELSPETH: [en] No. I told families the register was at the capital because Gregor told me to say it.

EWAN: [en] Say what the capital was.

ELSPETH: [en] A room, a map and our promises.

EWAN: [en] Appointments account. Jonas, civil administration commission.

JONAS: [en] I paid for a title over offices that did not exist.

EWAN: [en] You watched Gregor call me an incompetent clerk in Edinburgh.

JONAS: [en] I did. Before the same witnesses, I say this plainly: you were the only clerk in that hall who asked for lawful authority. I was incompetent because I did not listen.

SFX: [en] Settlers murmur agreement.

EWAN: [en] Publication account. Thomas, guidebook payment.

THOMAS: [en] The roads, cathedral, mines and parliament were supplied to me in written descriptions. I never saw Briarhaven.

INES: [en] I saw the place. Your cathedral was jungle. Your harbour was open coast. Your government was starving families asking where the officials had gone.

THOMAS: [en] Then enter my fee against their claims.

EWAN: [en] Bond account. Seven hundred certificates subscribed or reserved before the promised loan papers existed. Fifty-six thousand pounds committed.

SILAS: [en] Correct.

EWAN: [en] Second commission account. Payments from the bank and Gregor’s private account to Cyrus.

CYRUS: [en] Those entries remain disputed.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] Your public admission yesterday does not.

SFX: [en] The sealed draft is broken open.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] The hearing finds that no parliament, bank, royal office or civil administration existed independently of the London and Edinburgh workshops that produced their papers. Gregor possesses no authority to issue land, offices or currency in Briarhaven.

GREGOR: [en] This hearing cannot strip a sovereign title.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] It cannot strip what never existed. Your claimed authority is rejected. Every seal, commission and certificate submitted by you is impounded.

SFX: [en] Officers collect metal seals and document boxes.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] Cyrus may not validate, market or present any further Briarhaven issue. His commissions are attached to the creditors’ claims pending recovery.

VOICE AMONG CREDITORS: [en] Say the bond price!

SILAS: [en] There is no price. There is no buyer.

SFX: [en] A cheer rises, mixed with weeping.

MARA: [en] Ewan.

EWAN: [en] Mother, your debt was discharged from my Crownwater witness allowance. Sixteen pounds and eight shillings. No forged certificate paid it.

MARA: [en] Then lift your head when you read.

EWAN: [en] Survivor account. One hundred and seventy-three departed. One hundred and fifty-nine reached Crownwater Colony. Every surviving name will be entered. Every death reported to the hearing will be entered. Every land payment, bond subscription and recruiting fee will be entered as an actual loss, not as an interest in Briarhaven.

EDWIN: [en] My examination record confirms the survivor list and the absence of any functioning government at the settlement site.

ROBERT: [en] The Colonial Office accepts the register as public evidence. The interception dispatch naming Ewan as solely liable is withdrawn from operation and preserved as an exhibit, not a judgment.

EWAN: [en] Preserve my signature too. I certified the first grant.

ADA: [en] And preserve the plate that manufactured its authority.

INES: [en] Preserve the marker sent across the ocean before the land was supposedly received.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] All three will remain with the ledger.

SFX: [en] Pages turn as names are read. One by one, settlers answer present.

NARRATOR: [en] The reading continued until every person who had arrived with a title heard it reduced to a payment, a date and a claim. No promised acre was restored. But no loss was hidden behind a flag again.

SFX: [en] A pen scratches across the final page. The room becomes still.

REGISTER CLERK: [en] On the final page of the eighteen thirty-four register, Briarhaven is entered as a fictional state.

HEARING CHAIR: [en] And Ewan Pell is appointed keeper of the surviving settlers’ evidence.
