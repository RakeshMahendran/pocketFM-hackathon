# Episode 3 — The Authoritative Ledger

SFX: BRASS RUBBING SCRAPES ACROSS PAPER. A BOND-OFFICE CROWD POUNDS ON THE LOCKED DOORS.

IAIN: [language=en] One nick above the crown. Hold the doors until we match every seal.

CASSIAN: [language=en] You have no authority to hold anyone.

AGNES: [language=en] Neither did you when you locked my brother in this office.

CASSIAN: [language=en] A family grievance is not a government inquiry.

IAIN: [language=en] Tomas, put the land grant beside the dispatch.

TOMAS: [language=en] A printer prints what is supplied.

IAIN: [language=en] Today he also says who supplied it.

SFX: PAPERS LAID DOWN ONE BY ONE.

NORA: [language=en] That dispatch says we were received at the capital. I stood on that coast. No official received us.

EDWIN: [language=en] You are Nora Keel?

NORA: [language=en] Ship’s steward. Twenty-seven settlers sick before we found shelter. Two dead before I secured passage out.

ELSPETH: [language=en] Two?

NORA: [language=en] You recruited them. You may hear the number.

SFX: CROWD MUTTERS ANGRILY.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Illness proves climate, not fraud. New settlements suffer setbacks.

NORA: [language=en] There was no settlement.

IAIN: [language=en] Read the number on your land grant, Edwin.

EDWIN: [language=en] Three hundred and twelve.

IAIN: [language=en] Here is another grant numbered three hundred and twelve. Both carry this seal.

AGNES: [language=en] Say the figure.

IAIN: [language=en] Six grants with that number. Two thousand four hundred pounds collected against them.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Replacement copies are routine.

IAIN: [language=en] Then why were all six buyers charged as original grantees?

SFX: OFFICE DOOR SHAKES UNDER ANOTHER BLOW.

MARA: [language=en] Open the doors. Subscribers must witness this.

CASSIAN: [language=en] You advised discretion yesterday.

MARA: [language=en] Yesterday my commission was attached to a valid loan.

IAIN: [language=en] And today?

MARA: [language=en] Today validity is the question.

SFX: BOLTS DRAW BACK. DOORS OPEN. INVESTORS SURGE IN.

VOICE IN CROWD: [language=en] There is the clerk!

SECOND VOICE: [language=en] Let him read the books!

IAIN: [language=en] Rowan, the next ship’s authorization.

ROWAN: [language=en] I write what sailed, and I keep what is offered for sailing.

SFX: STIFF DOCUMENT UNFOLDS.

ROWAN: [language=en] This authorizes the Prosperity to carry one hundred and fourteen passengers to Poyais. Departure is set for this afternoon.

NORA: [language=en] One hundred and fourteen more people will land without shelter.

ROWAN: [language=en] Not if this paper is false.

IAIN: [language=en] Place it beside the grant, the banknote and Cassian’s dispatch.

SFX: FOUR SHEETS SLIDE INTO A ROW.

IAIN: [language=en] Land office. National bank. Foreign ministry. Harbour authority. Four branches of government.

MARA: [language=en] All bearing the same nick.

IAIN: [language=en] All struck from one brass die kept in this London office.

CASSIAN: [language=en] A young state uses a central seal until separate departments are established. Let us be practical.

IAIN: [language=en] Then explain the paper.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Government stationery.

IAIN: [language=en] Tomas?

TOMAS: [language=en] The sheets came from my shop.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Naturally. Governments contract printers.

IAIN: [language=en] The ministerial letterhead?

TOMAS: [language=en] My type.

IAIN: [language=en] The banknotes?

TOMAS: [language=en] My plates.

IAIN: [language=en] The coast map showing a capital and harbour?

TOMAS: [language=en] My press.

CASSIAN: [language=en] And whose approved instructions?

TOMAS: [language=en] Yours.

SFX: THE CROWD EXPLODES IN SHOUTS.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Take care, Tomas. Your shop is solvent because respectable clients trust your silence.

TOMAS: [language=en] It will not remain solvent when one hundred and fourteen passengers die carrying my paperwork.

IAIN: [language=en] Was any original ministry document sent from Poyais?

TOMAS: [language=en] No. I received wording from this office. Every so-called department was printed in London.

EDWIN: [language=en] Four departments, one room.

IAIN: [language=en] One room, one die, one chain of invented authority.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Invented is Mr Bell’s accusation.

NORA: [language=en] No. I brought an official warning from the coast.

SFX: SEA-DAMAGED CASE OPENS.

NORA: [language=en] Osric Dane inspected the landing. His letter orders harbour authorities to prevent further departures until food, shelter and lawful administration can be verified.

ROWAN: [language=en] His office can authorize a hold.

IAIN: [language=en] Take it to the harbour. Take the false authorization with it.

ROWAN: [language=en] I will need a witness.

EDWIN: [language=en] You have one.

SFX: ROWAN AND EDWIN RUN OUT. CROWD PARTS FOR THEM.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Anyone obstructing that vessel may answer for its entire commercial loss.

NORA: [language=en] Better a commercial loss than another grave.

IAIN: [language=en] Mara, what were you paid?

MARA: [language=en] That is confidential.

IAIN: [language=en] You validated the papers. Name the commission before the people who relied upon you.

VOICE IN CROWD: [language=en] Name it!

MARA: [language=en] Two and one-half percent of subscriptions placed through my firm.

IAIN: [language=en] On two hundred thousand pounds?

MARA: [language=en] Five thousand pounds, if the issue sold in full.

SFX: ANGRY VOICES CLOSE AROUND MARA.

MARA: [language=en] I have received eight hundred and seventy-five pounds. I validated Cassian’s papers without independent confirmation.

IAIN: [language=en] Elspeth?

ELSPETH: [language=en] I brought families into this room because I believed there would be land.

NORA: [language=en] Name the commission.

ELSPETH: [language=en] Three pounds for each adult passage and one pound for each child.

VOICE IN CROWD: [language=en] You were paid for our children?

ELSPETH: [language=en] Yes.

IAIN: [language=en] How many passengers on the next ship came through you?

ELSPETH: [language=en] Sixty-three adults. Twenty-one children.

NORA: [language=en] Then help stop them.

ELSPETH: [language=en] I will give Rowan every recruiting list.

SFX: A SIDE DOOR CLICKS SOFTLY.

AGNES: [language=en] Iain. Cassian is moving.

IAIN: [language=en] Leave the door and guard the documents.

SFX: IAIN TAKES TWO STEPS, THEN STOPS. SIDE DOOR CLOSES. OUTSIDE, CARRIAGE WHEELS RACE AWAY.

AGNES: [language=en] He has gone.

IAIN: [language=en] The papers have not.

NARRATOR: [language=en] Cassian escaped the office before anyone could place the theft or the forged dispatch in his hands. Iain let him go rather than abandon the only evidence that could stop the ship.

SFX: CLOCK TICKS. CROWD BREATHES. DISTANT BELLS MARK THE PASSAGE OF AN HOUR.

MARA: [language=en] Your ledger lists every subscriber?

IAIN: [language=en] Every payment, certificate, passage and promised office.

AGNES: [language=en] Including what you owe?

IAIN: [language=en] Including that.

SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS APPROACH. ROWAN BURSTS THROUGH THE DOORS.

ROWAN: [language=en] Harbour control accepted Osric’s warning. The Prosperity has been taken off the departure board.

SFX: CHEERS COLLIDE WITH SOBBING. A STAFF STRIKES THE FLOOR.

MAGISTRATE’S CLERK: [language=en] By order of the examining magistrate, no bond, certificate or subscription record is to leave these premises.

SFX: METAL CHAIN PASSES THROUGH THE BOND-OFFICE DOOR HANDLES. A LOCK SNAPS SHUT.

MAGISTRATE’S CLERK: [language=en] The Poyais bond office is sealed.

ROWAN: [language=en] The next ship is cancelled. None of its one hundred and fourteen passengers will sail.

MARA: [language=en] I will read Iain Bell’s ledger as the authoritative list of every buyer and every forged promise.
