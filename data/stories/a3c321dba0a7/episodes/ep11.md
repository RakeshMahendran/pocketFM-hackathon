# Episode 11 — A Better Name

SFX: A coastal record slams onto the registry table. Seals and cords rattle.

TAWIS: Enter it beside the survivor petition.

VEY: I require a translation, certification and diplomatic review.

EWAN: I require ten minutes with it.

MACRAE: You require rest, Ewan. Come with me.

EWAN: No.

MACRAE: That was not an order. It was a courtesy.

SFX: The gallery presses closer. Constables knock their staffs against the floor.

VEY: The record will remain under registry seal until examined.

THOMAS: We crossed an ocean to bring it here. Do not bury it under procedure.

VEY: Procedure is what prevents every loud man with a seal from becoming a government.

TAWIS: Your procedure did not prevent this one.

VOICE IN GALLERY: Let Tawis read it!

BELL: This chamber has heard enough accusations for one morning.

AGNES: You heard my children’s names when you sold us land. You can hear Tawis now.

MACRAE: Ewan, outside. Unless you prefer your future decided by a shouting gallery.

EWAN: My future has been decided in private rooms before.

MACRAE: Then let us improve the result.

SFX: A side door opens. The gallery noise dulls as it closes.

NARRATOR: Macrae chose a room with two chairs and placed Ewan in the better one. Remember that.

SFX: A decanter stopper lifts. Liquid pours into one glass.

MACRAE: You have made yourself important.

EWAN: Thomas made the survivors audible.

MACRAE: Thomas brought graves. You brought columns, signatures and dates. London understands your language.

EWAN: London understood your uniforms.

MACRAE: Yes. Because I understood London.

SFX: A leather portfolio opens.

MACRAE: A partnership agreement. One quarter of the next land issue. No salary. Profit.

EWAN: There should be no next issue.

MACRAE: There is always a next issue. Different acreage. Different subscribers. Better safeguards.

EWAN: The land is not yours.

MACRAE: Tawis possesses a coastal record. I possess grants, treaties, a constitution, a bank charter and years of public recognition.

EWAN: You possess paper.

MACRAE: So does every bank in this city.

EWAN: What do you want me to sign?

MACRAE: A statement that Thomas’s account is an act of commercial sabotage encouraged by rival interests.

EWAN: He buried six settlers.

MACRAE: Grief does not make a man accurate.

EWAN: Neither does a uniform.

SFX: Macrae sets down the glass without drinking.

MACRAE: Your father left debts. Your mother’s house has already appeared in one unfortunate ledger. Respectability has not protected either of you.

EWAN: That entry was forged.

MACRAE: Certainly. Yet ink travels farther than correction.

EWAN: Is that your threat?

MACRAE: It is advice from a man offering you shelter.

SFX: Another document slides across the table.

MACRAE: A new name. Ewan Kerr disappears from the certificates. Ewan Carrick becomes partner and land commissioner. No provincial debt. No copying desk. No accusation attached to your mother’s door.

EWAN: You would change one letter and call me clean.

MACRAE: London has accepted countries built with less.

EWAN: And my share?

MACRAE: Twelve thousand acres reserved on the next issue. Your quarter of subscription profit should exceed three thousand pounds.

EWAN: Enough to buy my mother’s house twice.

MACRAE: Enough to make the men who mocked you request appointments.

EWAN: In exchange for calling Thomas a liar.

MACRAE: In exchange for recognizing that nations are made by those who continue.

EWAN: Twenty-three settlers did not continue.

MACRAE: Men die in old countries too.

SFX: Ewan tears the unsigned statement once.

MACRAE: Careful. That sheet carried my seal.

EWAN: Then now it carries the truth about your authority.

MACRAE: You think applause in a registry chamber has cleared you.

EWAN: Bell admitted the theft ledger was planted.

MACRAE: He admitted an entry was planted. He did not admit who authorized eight hundred certificates bearing your initials.

EWAN: They were forged.

MACRAE: Publicly, repeatedly and very neatly. A court may admire consistency.

EWAN: Ross can identify the printing dates.

MACRAE: Ross can identify ink. Bell can identify accounts. Pippa can identify transfers. Thomas can identify mud. Only you appear on every part of the chain.

EWAN: Because you put me there.

MACRAE: Because you wanted a name respectable people would trust.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

MACRAE: I am offering you one.

EWAN: I will keep mine.

MACRAE: As you wish. A man should be permitted to choose the name printed on his difficulties.

SFX: The side door opens. Gallery noise rushes back.

THOMAS: Ewan. What did he offer?

EWAN: Money, land and one changed letter.

AGNES: For what?

EWAN: My statement that Thomas sabotaged Ravenshore.

VOICE IN GALLERY: He tried to buy the witness!

ANOTHER VOICE: Say the amount!

EWAN: Twelve thousand acres and a quarter of the next land issue.

SFX: Public outrage breaks across the chamber.

MACRAE: An exploratory commercial proposal has been deliberately mischaracterized.

TAWIS: You offered land while my record disputing your authority lies on that table.

MACRAE: I offered an interest subject to lawful confirmation.

BELL: There is no bank treasury available for another issue.

MACRAE: Banks are replaceable.

BELL: Some introductions are not.

PIPPA: Nor are the ledgers. I retained copies.

VEY: Enough. Ewan, did you sign Macrae’s statement?

EWAN: No.

VEY: Did you accept land or partnership?

EWAN: No.

THOMAS: Put that in the petition too.

VEY: It will be entered.

SFX: Clerk’s pen scratches rapidly.

MILES: Ewan, if you refused three thousand pounds, you are either honest or completely mad.

PIPPA: Those are not opposites in this office.

SFX: The main doors open. Measured official footsteps approach.

PROCESS SERVER: Ewan Kerr?

EWAN: Yes.

PROCESS SERVER: I have a creditor’s writ for personal service.

VEY: On what claim?

PROCESS SERVER: Eight hundred recent Ravenshore certificates bear his initials beneath the title of treasurer.

EWAN: Those initials were forged.

PROCESS SERVER: The writ names Ewan Kerr as co-director of the Ravenshore treasury.
