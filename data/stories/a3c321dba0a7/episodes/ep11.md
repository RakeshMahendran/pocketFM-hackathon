# Episode 11 — The Price Before the Paper

SFX: [en] Exchange bell clanging; brokers shouting prices over one another.
EWAN: [en] Silas! Before you sell another bond, explain why Gregor paid me before he hired me.
SILAS: [en] Not here.
EWAN: [en] Here. Before the exchange crowd.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] The traitor has brought an account book!

SFX: [en] Brokers crowding closer, pencils scratching.
SILAS: [en] Close that ledger, Ewan. Paper survives men who speak carelessly beside it.
EWAN: [en] Entry dated before my appointment. Twenty-five pounds under my name. I never received it.
SILAS: [en] Then you have saved twenty-five pounds by doing nothing. Many investors envy you.
EWAN: [en] Who entered it?
SILAS: [en] A clerk.
EWAN: [en] Which clerk?
SILAS: [en] One with excellent handwriting and poor prospects.
EWAN: [en] Gregor placed advance commissions under the names of clerks he intended to hire, did he not?
SILAS: [en] I move instruments. I do not baptise them.
EWAN: [en] He entered money under my name before I arrived so every later fraud would appear to begin with my paid consent.
SILAS: [en] You have crossed the ocean and returned with a taste for conclusions.
EWAN: [en] Deny it.
SILAS: [en] I decline to improve your performance.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] Did Pell take the money or not?
SECOND VOICE: [en] Ask him about the dead settlers!
EWAN: [en] I took no payment before my employment. Silas knows the entry was planted as an advance commission against a clerk Gregor had not yet recruited.
SILAS: [en] Keep your voice down.
EWAN: [en] That is the first honest instruction you have given me.

SFX: [en] A hand snatches at the ledger; leather cover slams against a railing.
SILAS: [en] Give me that book.
EWAN: [en] No.
SILAS: [en] It belongs to the Briarhaven administration.
EWAN: [en] Then name the lawful officer who will claim it.
SILAS: [en] Do not play a clerk’s game with me.
EWAN: [en] Entries, dates and signatures are the clerk’s game. You only profited because no one asked the clerk to read them aloud.
SFX: [en] Exchange bell strikes again.
COMPERE: [en] Briarhaven second issue offered at eighty-two!
BROKER: [en] Eighty-one!
ANOTHER BROKER: [en] Seventy-nine!
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] Let him read!

EWAN: [en] On the fourteenth of April, the loan was publicly validated at two hundred thousand pounds.
SILAS: [en] Everyone here heard it.
EWAN: [en] You told me that day that seven hundred certificates had already been subscribed or reserved.
SILAS: [en] Demand was considerable.
EWAN: [en] You also said the unprinted bonds issued at eighty pounds were trading at eighty-six.
SILAS: [en] A strong market is not a crime.
EWAN: [en] How were bonds traded before the certificates existed?
SILAS: [en] By subscription receipt.
EWAN: [en] Secured by what?
SILAS: [en] The forthcoming loan instruments.
EWAN: [en] Forthcoming. Not existing.
SILAS: [en] Timing is not falsehood.
EWAN: [en] When did Cyrus first receive the loan certificates he claimed to validate?
SILAS: [en] Ask Cyrus.
EWAN: [en] I am asking the broker who sold them.
SILAS: [en] And I am refusing the unemployed clerk who stole a private ledger.
EWAN: [en] Then refuse me under oath.
SFX: [en] Crowd reacts; several canes knock against the floor.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] Yes! Under oath!
SECOND VOICE: [en] Answer the date!

SFX: [en] A walking stick taps twice; the crowd quiets unevenly.
CYRUS: [en] This exchange is not a courtroom. Mr Pell’s distress is understandable after his unfortunate expedition, but an account book removed at night proves only theft.
EWAN: [en] Did you independently validate the first bond issue?
CYRUS: [en] My bank examined the instruments submitted to it.
EWAN: [en] Did those instruments exist before Silas sold subscriptions against them?
CYRUS: [en] Commercial arrangements frequently precede engrossment.
EWAN: [en] Seven hundred certificates were subscribed or reserved. At eighty pounds each, that is fifty-six thousand pounds committed before the paper existed.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] Fifty-six thousand?
CYRUS: [en] Committed is not paid, and a reservation is not a sale.
EWAN: [en] Silas called them sold when the price rose to eighty-six.
SILAS: [en] I said they were trading.
EWAN: [en] Then tell these men what they traded.
SILAS: [en] Expectation.
EWAN: [en] Whose expectation?
SILAS: [en] The market’s.
EWAN: [en] Built on Cyrus’s validation.
SILAS: [en] Yes.
EWAN: [en] Given before the certificates existed.
SILAS: [en] No.
EWAN: [en] Then state the date you delivered the certificates to his bank.
SFX: [en] Nothing. Long.
EWAN: [en] State it.

CYRUS: [en] Silas, there is no obligation to indulge him.
SILAS: [en] You said the file was complete.
CYRUS: [en] It was complete for the purpose required.
SILAS: [en] You said your signature covered the interval.
CYRUS: [en] This is neither the place nor the audience for private recollection.
EWAN: [en] The date, Silas.
SILAS: [en] The certificates reached the bank on the sixteenth.
EWAN: [en] Two days after the validation.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] He validated blank paper!
ANOTHER VOICE: [en] We bought before the bank received anything!
CYRUS: [en] You bought subscription rights supported by a sovereign undertaking.
EWAN: [en] There was no recognised sovereign government. I stood at the supposed capital. There was no harbour, no parliament and no resident official.
CYRUS: [en] Your travel account remains contested.
EWAN: [en] Silas’s date is not.
SILAS: [en] I did not say the paper was blank.
EWAN: [en] You said the certificates arrived two days after Cyrus validated them. Say the rest plainly.
SILAS: [en] Why?
EWAN: [en] Because men in this room paid real money for paper you had not printed.
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] My family bought four!
SECOND VOICE: [en] I paid three hundred and twenty pounds!
EWAN: [en] Say it before them.
SILAS: [en] The first Briarhaven bonds were sold before the promised loan certificates existed.
SFX: [en] The exchange erupts—angry shouts, desks struck, the quoted price called downward.
BROKER: [en] Briarhaven at sixty-eight!
ANOTHER BROKER: [en] No buyer at sixty-eight!
VOICE IN CROWD: [en] Perring, answer him!

SFX: [en] Cyrus’s stick cracks against a desk.
CYRUS: [en] Mr Lowe has confused printing with legal creation. His commission depended upon volume, and excitement has made him imprecise.
SILAS: [en] My commission depended upon money reaching both of you.
CYRUS: [en] Think carefully.
SILAS: [en] I have. Markets forgive greed. They do not forgive the last man left holding it.
EWAN: [en] What else do you have?
SILAS: [en] Protection.
EWAN: [en] From whom?
SILAS: [en] Whoever loses first.
SFX: [en] Small keys pulled from a chain; a dispatch box opens beneath a broker’s desk.
CYRUS: [en] Close that box.
SILAS: [en] You called my recollection private. Let us improve it with figures.
SFX: [en] A second ledger lands open on the exchange desk.
EWAN: [en] Whose accounts are these?
SILAS: [en] Cyrus’s commissions. This ledger shows he received money from both the bank and Gregor’s private account.
