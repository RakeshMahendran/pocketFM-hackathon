# Episode 8 — The Clerk of the Survivors

SFX: [en] Wagon wheels grind over a wooden bridge. A sentry’s bell rings twice.
SENTRY: [en] Halt the convoy! No one enters Crownwater without a receiving order.
EWAN: [en] There are one hundred and fifty-nine survivors behind me. Open the gate.
SENTRY: [en] I said halt.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Two died while your flag was in sight.
INES: [en] Send for Edwin Codd. Tell him the people sold Briarhaven land have reached his boundary.
SENTRY: [en] Briarhaven officials report through the diplomatic office.
EWAN: [en] There are no Briarhaven officials.
SFX: [en] Survivors murmur. A child coughs violently.
EDWIN: [en] Who said that?
SFX: [en] Measured boots approach along the bridge.
EWAN: [en] I did. Ewan Pell, formerly a clerk in Gregor Vale’s office.
EDWIN: [en] Formerly?
EWAN: [en] If I am still employed, you may hang me after the children have eaten.
EDWIN: [en] This colony does not hang men to improve their grammar. How many sick?
INES: [en] Fifty-three cannot walk. Seventeen have fever. Six children are fading.
EDWIN: [en] Names?
EWAN: [en] Recorded here.
SFX: [en] A damp ledger is handed over.
EDWIN: [en] This says one hundred and seventy-three landed.
EWAN: [en] Fourteen are dead.
EDWIN: [en] And the remaining one hundred and fifty-nine followed you here?
SETTLER: [en] We followed Ines. We tolerated him.
EDWIN: [en] An important administrative distinction.
EWAN: [en] Will you admit them?
EDWIN: [en] Not as citizens of Briarhaven.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] We do not care what you call us.
EDWIN: [en] You will when a merchant charges your food to a government that does not exist. I need a lawful category.
EWAN: [en] Shipwrecked British subjects.
EDWIN: [en] Their ship did not sink.
INES: [en] Their country did.
SFX: [en] Nothing. Brief.
EDWIN: [en] Sentry, enter them as distressed passengers pending individual examination. Open the gate.
SFX: [en] Gate chains run. Heavy doors swing inward.
VOICES: [en] Thank God. Water. Make room. Carry the boy first.
EDWIN: [en] Medical station, thirty-eight children. Kitchens, prepare one hundred and fifty-nine portions. No merchant is to take a note or land certificate as payment.
SETTLER: [en] We have nothing else.
EDWIN: [en] Today, you do not pay.
SFX: [en] The survivors break into exhausted applause and sobbing.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Say it again, sir. Let the clerk hear.
EDWIN: [en] The survivors are admitted. Food, shelter and treatment will be provided under the Crownwater distress account.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Fourteen did not reach it.
EDWIN: [en] Their names will be entered too.
NARRATOR: [en] The first meal was served before noon. For the first time since the ship grounded, no Briarhaven paper changed hands.
SFX: [en] Tin bowls and spoons. A ladle strikes a pot.
CHILD: [en] Is this from our farm?
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] No, love.
CHILD: [en] When do we go to our sixty acres?
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Eat first.
EWAN: [en] I signed the grant for those sixty acres.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] I know.
EWAN: [en] There is no farm.
CHILD: [en] Then why is my name on the paper?
SFX: [en] Spoon drops into a bowl.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Go and help your brother.
SFX: [en] Small footsteps recede.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] You want forgiveness because you brought us through the marsh.
EWAN: [en] No.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] What do you want?
EWAN: [en] Every certificate, note and appointment they sold you.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] They are all we have left.
EWAN: [en] They are evidence.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Evidence belongs to courts. Those papers contain our names.
EWAN: [en] Then keep them. Let me record their numbers.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Why should we trust your record?
EDWIN: [en] Because it will be made in public, under my seal, with each owner standing beside him.
SFX: [en] Chair legs scrape over a stone floor. A crowded hall settles.
EDWIN: [en] This examination is open. The survivors may hear every question and every answer.
EWAN: [en] I ask that Ines remain beside the table.
EDWIN: [en] Granted.
INES: [en] I will correct him when he becomes convenient.
EDWIN: [en] Mr Pell, did a functioning government receive these settlers at the Briarhaven capital?
EWAN: [en] No.
EDWIN: [en] Did you find a harbour?
EWAN: [en] No.
EDWIN: [en] Government house?
EWAN: [en] No.
EDWIN: [en] Barracks, hospital or cathedral?
EWAN: [en] None.
EDWIN: [en] Any resident official empowered to recognise land claims?
EWAN: [en] None.
EDWIN: [en] What did exist?
EWAN: [en] An abandoned trading shelter, promotional maps and a boundary marker dated 1831.
INES: [en] I witnessed the marker and can identify its location.
EDWIN: [en] Recorded. Mr Pell, did you prepare Briarhaven documents?
EWAN: [en] Yes.
EDWIN: [en] Speak so the rear benches hear you.
EWAN: [en] I prepared land grants, commissions and bond certificates. I engraved the seal. I knew the banknotes could not be redeemed. I continued because Gregor Vale held my mother’s unpaid debt ledger and threatened her home.
VOICES: [en] Shame! He admits it! Fourteen dead!
SETTLER: [en] Put him outside the gate!
EDWIN: [en] Silence. Public testimony is not public revenge.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] His mother kept her home while ours were sold.
EWAN: [en] Her debt is sixteen pounds and eight shillings. I will not prepare another certificate to pay it.
EDWIN: [en] Clerk, enter a witness allowance of twenty pounds for Ewan Pell.
EWAN: [en] I do not want payment.
EDWIN: [en] That was not the question. The allowance is lawful compensation for guiding a distressed convoy and giving evidence.
EWAN: [en] Then issue sixteen pounds and eight shillings directly to my mother’s creditor in Edinburgh. Mark the debt discharged. Send the remainder to her.
EDWIN: [en] You understand that once dispatched, it cannot be reclaimed for your passage?
EWAN: [en] Dispatch it today.
SFX: [en] Pen scratches. Sand scatters across wet ink.
EDWIN: [en] Draft authorised. Mara Bell’s debt will be discharged without another Briarhaven certificate.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] That saves your mother.
EWAN: [en] It does not excuse me.
EDWIN: [en] Good. Excuses are difficult to index.
SFX: [en] Bundles of paper are placed on the table.
EDWIN: [en] First claimant.
SETTLER: [en] Forty acres, certificate C-208. I paid thirty sovereigns.
EWAN: [en] The signature is mine. The seal came from the Edinburgh plate.
EDWIN: [en] Second claimant.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Appointment as schoolmistress. Annual salary, seventy pounds.
EWAN: [en] Same paper stock as the bank charter. No office issued it.
EDWIN: [en] Third.
SETTLER: [en] Ten notes, serial B-417.
EWAN: [en] The number was duplicated five hundred times.
VOICES: [en] Five hundred? They sold me the same number! Mine too!
EDWIN: [en] Mr Pell, can any other witness identify these production marks?
EWAN: [en] Ada Wren can, if she is alive. Her print shop was emptied after she gave me a duplicate plate.
EDWIN: [en] Do you possess that plate?
SFX: [en] Metal plate lands heavily on the table.
EWAN: [en] Yes.
EDWIN: [en] Let the record state that the same damaged border appears on the land grants, banknotes and military commissions.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Then say what he is, sir. Not what he pretended to be.
EDWIN: [en] Ewan Pell is the clerk who can identify the forged Briarhaven papers, and this administration accepts his testimony on that basis.
SFX: [en] The hall erupts in argument, then settles under Edwin’s gavel.
EDWIN: [en] Look carefully at what you brought across the ocean.
EDWIN: [en] Ewan’s certificates are the only documents linking your names to land that does not exist.
