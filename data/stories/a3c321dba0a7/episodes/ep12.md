# Episode 12 — Protection in Confidence

SFX: [en] Government carriage door opens amid a hostile crowd outside the exchange.
ROBERT: [en] Mr Pell, enter the carriage if you prefer protection to arrest.
EWAN: [en] Protection from whom?
ROBERT: [en] That depends upon which door reaches you first.

SFX: [en] Crowd pounding the carriage panels; horses moving off.
VOICE OUTSIDE: [en] Pell! Where is our money?
ANOTHER VOICE: [en] Arrest Perring!
ROBERT: [en] You have produced an inconvenient morning.
EWAN: [en] Silas admitted the bonds were sold before the loan certificates existed.
ROBERT: [en] I heard. Half the exchange heard. By luncheon, the sentence will have acquired six versions and a death toll.
EWAN: [en] One hundred and fifty-nine survivors entered Crownwater Colony. The first ship carried one hundred and seventy-three settlers.
ROBERT: [en] Give me the master ledger, the duplicate plate and the boundary evidence. Quietly.
EWAN: [en] No.
ROBERT: [en] You misunderstand the offer.
EWAN: [en] You offered a closed door.
ROBERT: [en] I offered government protection, immunity from prosecution for document offences and secure lodging for your mother.
EWAN: [en] In exchange for silence.
ROBERT: [en] In exchange for orderly evidence. Silence is such an emotional word.
EWAN: [en] The families were sold a harbour that does not exist.
ROBERT: [en] And shouting that fact through London will not build one.
EWAN: [en] It may stop the next ship.
ROBERT: [en] That is already being considered.
EWAN: [en] Considered by whom?
ROBERT: [en] By men who do not announce policy from exchange railings.
SFX: [en] Carriage blinds snap shut.

NARRATOR: [en] The carriage did not stop at a police office. It entered a government courtyard where no creditor was permitted to follow.

SFX: [en] Iron gates closing; carriage wheels halt on stone.
ROBERT: [en] Bring the ledger. Leave the plate concealed unless asked.
EWAN: [en] You know I have it?
ROBERT: [en] Mr Pell, a clerk carrying nothing does not hold his coat with both hands.

SFX: [en] Office door opens; coal fire, papers shifted across a broad desk.
ROBERT: [en] Sit.
EWAN: [en] I will stand.
ROBERT: [en] Then we shall both pretend that grants you leverage.
SFX: [en] A document box unlocks.
ROBERT: [en] Crownwater transmitted an examination summary. No harbour. No public buildings. No resident officials. No functioning Briarhaven government.
EWAN: [en] Then you know the survivor testimony is true.
ROBERT: [en] I know an administrator recorded it.
EWAN: [en] Edwin fed the survivors and admitted them as distressed passengers.
ROBERT: [en] A careful classification. It avoids recognising the state they believed they had entered.
EWAN: [en] Because Briarhaven is not a state.
ROBERT: [en] Recognition is a tool, not a reward for honesty.
EWAN: [en] Gregor is preparing more certificates. Ada is confined above his subscription hall because he holds her father’s debts.
ROBERT: [en] Can she testify freely?
EWAN: [en] Not while he holds a signed confession over her father.
ROBERT: [en] Then she is presently a compromised witness.
EWAN: [en] She is a prisoner.
ROBERT: [en] Again, an emotional word where a useful one is available.
EWAN: [en] What useful word do you have for one hundred and seventy-three people sent to jungle and ruins?
ROBERT: [en] Exposure.
EWAN: [en] Whose?
ROBERT: [en] Ours, if handled badly.

SFX: [en] Master ledger placed on the desk.
EWAN: [en] Every bond, land grant and recruiting payment is indexed here. Cyrus took commission from his bank and from Gregor’s private account.
ROBERT: [en] This could collapse the second issue.
EWAN: [en] It should collapse the first one too.
ROBERT: [en] The first issue has already changed hands. If it is declared fraudulent today, innocent holders lose before the architects do.
EWAN: [en] They have already lost.
ROBERT: [en] They possess a negotiable claim.
EWAN: [en] Against what?
ROBERT: [en] That is the question your evidence makes dangerous.
EWAN: [en] Dangerous to investors or to the government?
ROBERT: [en] Must we choose?
EWAN: [en] Yes.
ROBERT: [en] Then I choose the order in which they are informed.

SFX: [en] Cabinet drawer slides open; sealed dispatches rustle.
EWAN: [en] What is that order?
ROBERT: [en] Future vessels will be intercepted before they reach the Sable Coast.
EWAN: [en] You have issued it already.
ROBERT: [en] A draft has advanced beyond discussion.
EWAN: [en] Where will the passengers be taken?
ROBERT: [en] Crownwater Colony where practical. Other safe ports where necessary.
EWAN: [en] When will you warn them in Britain?
ROBERT: [en] We will not announce the interception yet.
EWAN: [en] Then recruiting continues.
ROBERT: [en] Temporarily.
EWAN: [en] Families will still sell homes, buy land and board ships.
ROBERT: [en] Ships we intend to stop.
EWAN: [en] After the money is taken.
ROBERT: [en] A public warning would destroy the bonds before the government identifies the full exposure of British banks.
EWAN: [en] So investors receive silence and settlers receive interception.
ROBERT: [en] Settlers receive life. Investors retain the possibility of value while inquiries proceed.
EWAN: [en] There is no value. The capital has no harbour, buildings or officials.
ROBERT: [en] Your certainty is admirable. Government must survive certainty.
EWAN: [en] How many ships are expected?
ROBERT: [en] Three are under discussion. One may sail within days.
EWAN: [en] And you will let passengers pay until the gangway is raised.
ROBERT: [en] We will prevent them reaching the false settlement.
EWAN: [en] Without telling them why.
ROBERT: [en] Without causing a commercial panic that may consume firms entirely unconnected with Gregor.
EWAN: [en] Cyrus’s bank is connected.
ROBERT: [en] Your second ledger suggests that. A hearing must establish it.
EWAN: [en] Publicly.
ROBERT: [en] Privately first.
EWAN: [en] No.

SFX: [en] Chair pushed back sharply.
ROBERT: [en] Consider your position. You engraved a seal, certified land and prepared bonds. Gregor will say you designed the mechanism.
EWAN: [en] His ledger planted a payment under my name before I was hired.
ROBERT: [en] Which makes you appear involved earlier, not later.
EWAN: [en] Silas can explain the entry.
ROBERT: [en] Silas will explain whichever version preserves Silas.
EWAN: [en] The survivors know what I did at the coast.
ROBERT: [en] Survivors know hunger. Courts know signatures.
EWAN: [en] Is that the protection you offered?
ROBERT: [en] It is the danger from which protection would be useful.
EWAN: [en] I will testify in public. I will name the bonds, the plates, the missing government and every payment in that ledger.
ROBERT: [en] Then the Office cannot prevent interested parties from describing you as the author of the alarm.
EWAN: [en] I am the source of evidence, not the author of the fraud.
ROBERT: [en] Documents are less sentimental about distinctions.
EWAN: [en] Show me the interception order.
ROBERT: [en] That is not part of our arrangement.
EWAN: [en] There is no arrangement.
SFX: [en] Ewan snatches a dispatch; wax seal skids across the desk.
ROBERT: [en] Put that down.
EWAN: [en] It orders Crownwater and neighbouring ports to intercept every vessel carrying Briarhaven settlers.
ROBERT: [en] Continue to the authority line.
SFX: [en] Paper unfolds once more.
ROBERT: [en] The government dispatch names Ewan Pell as the official source, making him liable for every death and every lost investment.
