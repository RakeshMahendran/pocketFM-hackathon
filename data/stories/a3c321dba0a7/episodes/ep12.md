# Episode 12 — The Chain

SFX: A creditor’s writ crackles as Ewan spreads it over a counting table.

EWAN: Lock the door, Pippa. Bell leaves only after we rebuild the whole chain.

BELL: You are named as co-director and still giving orders in my bank.

EWAN: Then save your bank.

THOMAS: Or save Macrae. Choose where we can hear you.

SFX: The key turns in the lock.

BELL: This confinement is improper.

PIPPA: You once locked Ewan in a counting room over three hundred and twelve missing entries.

BELL: That was an internal employment matter.

TAWIS: This is an internal government matter. According to my record, there is no government.

SFX: Bundled ledgers land heavily on the table.

EWAN: Five links. Land claim. Printed authority. Bank subscription. Bond sale. Settler ship.

BELL: A dramatic arrangement does not establish liability.

EWAN: No. Signatures do.

PIPPA: First ledger. Bell introduced Macrae’s controlled account to the subscription account.

BELL: For the efficient transfer of legitimate sovereign funds.

EWAN: Second ledger. Eight hundred bonds sold before delivery. My initials appear on each certificate, although I did not write them.

BELL: That allegation requires expert evidence.

PIPPA: Ross already dated the ink used to plant the theft entry. His press invoices also place the certificates in production before Ewan was named treasurer.

BELL: Ross prints what clients supply.

THOMAS: Third link. Your sale financed our passage.

BELL: Passage was arranged by expedition agents.

THOMAS: Your office announced the payment in front of investors.

EWAN: Fourth link. The cargo ledger lists uniforms, proclamations, bank notes and government paper. No office-supplied food, medical chests, axes or saws.

BELL: I am a banker, not a quartermaster.

THOMAS: You sold shares in a country. You might have checked whether its people could cut wood.

TAWIS: Fifth link. The claimed territory was governed through coastal communities that did not recognize Macrae’s state, bank or land grants.

BELL: Your record may describe a local disagreement.

TAWIS: It records every recognized agreement affecting the claimed coast. Macrae’s government appears nowhere.

EWAN: Yet Bell’s bank accepted his bonds as sovereign paper.

BELL: Accepted for subscription, not guaranteed as sovereign obligations.

PIPPA: Your salon invitations called them government securities.

BELL: Promotional language.

THOMAS: Mara called mud a capital. Was that promotional too?

SFX: Bell shuts one ledger. Ewan opens it again.

EWAN: The writ says I directed the treasury because forged initials appear on the bonds. If initials create a director, your signature creates the bank behind every transfer.

BELL: You would implicate yourself to reach me?

EWAN: I am already named.

BELL: And Macrae?

EWAN: He receives the money, supplies the authority and orders the papers.

TAWIS: He also sells land he cannot govern.

BELL: Then your reconstruction needs the original grant.

EWAN: Which grant?

BELL: The document Macrae first used to persuade me that his claim had territorial standing.

PIPPA: It is not in the subscription archive.

BELL: No.

EWAN: Where is it?

BELL: Safe.

THOMAS: Twenty-three people were not.

BELL: Spare me the rehearsed injury. If I surrender the grant, Macrae will say I accepted it knowingly and built a bank issue around it.

EWAN: Did you?

BELL: I accepted what men with government introductions, military honours and proper seals accepted.

PIPPA: That is not an answer.

BELL: It is the answer by which respectable finance operates.

EWAN: Not anymore.

SFX: Tawis unties the cord around the coastal record.

TAWIS: Read the boundary description from Macrae’s prospectus.

PIPPA: Northern river, western ridge, Blackwater Coast, sixty miles south to the cape.

TAWIS: My record lists that same boundary under three coastal jurisdictions. None granted Macrae sovereign authority.

THOMAS: And the coordinates on his map placed us in uncleared ground.

EWAN: The map made the land claim visible. The seals made it official. Bell’s subscriptions made it valuable. The bond money paid the ship. The ship carried settlers to a capital that did not exist.

PIPPA: That is the chain.

BELL: It is a theory.

EWAN: Then break one link.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

BELL: The bank accepted the bonds because Macrae’s papers imitated the form expected of a government.

THOMAS: You knew they were imitation?

BELL: I knew they were new.

EWAN: Did you verify the territory?

BELL: No.

TAWIS: Did you ask any authority from the coast?

BELL: No.

PIPPA: Did you hold investor money until the certificates were delivered?

BELL: No.

EWAN: Did you allow Macrae to control it?

BELL: Yes.

SFX: Pippa’s pen stops.

PIPPA: Say whom you choose now.

BELL: I beg your pardon?

PIPPA: Protect Macrae, and this writ pulls your bank into the treasury. Give us the grant, and the chain reaches him before it closes around you.

BELL: You have become remarkably direct.

PIPPA: I learned from columns. They do not flatter.

THOMAS: Choose.

BELL: If I assist you, every investor will say I sold sovereign bonds without verifying a sovereign.

EWAN: That is what happened.

BELL: If I protect Macrae, creditors will seize the bank records and discover the same thing without my cooperation.

TAWIS: That is also what will happen.

SFX: Bell walks to a wall safe. Metal dials turn.

NARRATOR: For years, Bell had protected his reputation with locked doors. This time, opening one cost him more.

SFX: The safe opens. Documents shift inside.

BELL: There are older papers than the one I showed investors.

EWAN: How much older?

BELL: Some came before your employment. Others came after the survivors’ petition.

THOMAS: After London knew?

BELL: Macrae never regarded a complaint as a border.

SFX: Bell removes a thick sealed packet.

PIPPA: The wax is recent.

BELL: I received it with instructions for another land issue.

EWAN: When?

BELL: Last year.

THOMAS: He was still selling Ravenshore?

BELL: He was preparing to.

TAWIS: Open it.

BELL: Once opened, I cannot deny possession.

EWAN: You signed the account. You introduced the bonds. You funded the ship. Possession is the smallest thing left to deny.

SFX: Bell breaks the wax seal. Heavy parchment unfolds.

PIPPA: There is Macrae’s seal.

EWAN: Acreage?

BELL: Sixty acres.

TAWIS: Date of issue?

BELL: April 1834.

SFX: Bell places the sealed sixty-acre grant in Ewan’s hands.

BELL: Macrae’s paper country was still being sold thirteen years after the first settlers were abandoned.
