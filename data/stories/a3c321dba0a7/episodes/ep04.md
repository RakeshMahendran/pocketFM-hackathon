# Episode 4 — One Workshop

SFX: Printing press slams. Type rattles across a stone floor.
EWAN: Stop the press. Lock the door.
ADA: That costs money. Tell me why.
EWAN: Serial B-417 was sold in Glasgow. Tonight, a farmer gave me the same number in Edinburgh.
SFX: Press wheel slows to a groaning stop.
MASTER PRINTER: Miss Wren, your father gave no order to stop.
ADA: Then charge the lost sheet to me.
MASTER PRINTER: You do not own an account here.
ADA: One day I shall own the press. Leave us.
MASTER PRINTER: A woman owning a print room. Shall I set that in fairy type?
ADA: Set it in twelve-point and spell my name correctly.
SFX: Door closes. Key turns.
EWAN: I need the banknote plate.
ADA: No.
EWAN: Families are buying notes that cannot be redeemed.
ADA: And if my father finds you touching his plates, I lose my place before I ever own one.
EWAN: Gregor has my mother's debt ledger. I cannot walk away. I need proof strong enough to break his hold.
ADA: Proof will not pay your mother's arrears.
EWAN: Neither will a banknote printed twice under one serial number.
SFX: Paper unfolds against a worktable.
ADA: B-417. The numbering wheel should advance after every impression.
EWAN: Could it slip backwards?
ADA: Once, perhaps. Not from Glasgow to Edinburgh. Someone reset it.
EWAN: Why?
ADA: Because they printed another run and wanted the first buyer to think his note belonged to a limited issue.
EWAN: How many runs?
ADA: Bring me one of each document from the Briarhaven office.
EWAN: Gregor keeps the charter cabinet locked.
ADA: You engraved his seal in a morning. Do not become honest at the least useful moment.
SFX: Metal picks scrape inside a lock.
NARRATOR: Before midnight, Ewan returned with a constitution, a bank charter, a military commission and the note numbered B-417.
SFX: Print-room shutters knock. A lamp wick hisses.
ADA: Constitution first. Hold the lower edge.
EWAN: There is a crescent in the paper.
ADA: A flaw in the mould. Same crescent here on the bank charter.
EWAN: And on the commission.
ADA: Three institutions are buying from the same paper maker. That proves economy, not fraud.
EWAN: The ink cured on the same day.
ADA: Then we test the plates.
SFX: Drawer slides open. Heavy copper lands on wood.
EWAN: That is the Green Cross from the royal seal.
ADA: Listen.
SFX: Thin paper laid over copper. Charcoal rubs rapidly.
ADA: The left arm has a nick. Small as a pin bite.
EWAN: The constitution's royal seal has it.
ADA: So does the bank charter.
EWAN: The military commission too.
ADA: Separate offices could order the same emblem.
EWAN: Not the same damaged plate.
ADA: Correct. Now the border.
SFX: Second copper plate scrapes onto the table.
EWAN: Laurel leaves, crown, two ships.
ADA: Count the leaves beside the crown.
EWAN: Eleven.
ADA: The banknote has eleven. The land grant has eleven. The constitution has eleven.
EWAN: Parliament, bank and royal office.
ADA: All struck from plates cut in this room.
SFX: Nothing. Long.
EWAN: Say it plainly.
ADA: Briarhaven's government is a print order. Its parliament, bank and royal seal are physically manufactured in one Edinburgh workshop.
EWAN: Then the mystery is finished.
ADA: No. The mystery is why my father accepted the orders.
EWAN: Gregor paid him.
ADA: Gregor pays in promises. My father prefers coin.
EWAN: Who brought the designs?
ADA: A messenger. Gloves, military posture, no name. Every order was entered under a different customer.
EWAN: Show me the order book.
ADA: No.
EWAN: Ada—
ADA: The book belongs to my father. If you take it, Gregor knows where the evidence came from. If he knows, this shop closes.
EWAN: It is already producing false government papers.
ADA: It is also feeding six compositors, two apprentices and my father. Facts do not become simple because you are frightened.
EWAN: I am ashamed, not frightened.
ADA: You are both.
SFX: Numbering wheel clicks.
ADA: Here. B-417.
EWAN: The wheel has been fixed at that number.
ADA: It was not an accident. Every sheet in the second run carried B-417 until the operator advanced it by hand.
EWAN: How many sheets?
ADA: The press tally says five hundred.
EWAN: Five hundred duplicate notes.
ADA: Worth one pound each to the families. Worth paper and ink to the people selling them.
EWAN: I will take the tally.
ADA: You will take nothing that names this shop.
EWAN: Then Gregor continues.
ADA: If you confront him with paper, he burns paper. You need something he cannot call a clerk's mistake.
EWAN: The damaged plate.
ADA: The original would be missed before breakfast.
EWAN: Can you strike an impression?
ADA: An impression can be dismissed as a copy.
EWAN: Then make me a duplicate.
ADA: Copper costs fourteen shillings. Engraving it costs a night. Discovery costs me everything.
EWAN: I have three shillings.
ADA: Keep them. Poverty is not persuasive when we are discussing mine.
EWAN: What do you want?
ADA: When this breaks, you say publicly that I found it. Not you. Not some banker. Me.
EWAN: You want your name tied to a fraud.
ADA: I want my skill tied to its exposure. Men forgive skill after they have finished condemning the woman who used it.
EWAN: You have my word.
ADA: Your word is printed on sixty acres that do not exist.
EWAN: Then take the only thing I have not sold. My name.
ADA: Your name is already on the certificates.
EWAN: Exactly.
SFX: Engraving tool bites into copper.
NARRATOR: For four hours, Ada copied every nick, leaf and false symbol. Ewan worked the bellows and listened for footsteps.
SFX: Distant church bell strikes four. Engraving stops.
EWAN: If Gregor discovers this—
ADA: He will be courteous. That is when he is most dangerous.
SFX: Fist pounds on the street door.
MASTER PRINTER: Ada! Open this door!
ADA: He is early.
EWAN: Hide the plate.
MASTER PRINTER: I have the constable with me!
ADA: The constable drinks with my father. That is not the same as law.
EWAN: Come with me.
ADA: Through the paper hatch. You first.
EWAN: I will not leave you.
ADA: Then we are both caught, and Gregor keeps printing.
SFX: Door shudders under another blow.
MASTER PRINTER: Open now!
ADA: Ewan, when you hear the bolt, run.
EWAN: And tomorrow?
ADA: Come when the shop opens.
SFX: Small metal plate wrapped in cloth.
ADA: Take the duplicate plate, Ewan.
NARRATOR: The next morning, her father's print shop had been emptied.
NARRATOR: Ada had vanished.
