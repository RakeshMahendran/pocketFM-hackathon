# Episode 7 — The Seal in the Storm

SFX: [en] Hurricane wind tears canvas loose. Timber cracks. Rain hammers metal.
SETTLER: [en] Clerk! Your country promised us houses. My daughter is standing in water. Give us the keys!
EWAN: [en] There are no houses.
SETTLER: [en] Then you sold us graves.
INES: [en] Everyone away from the shelter! The roof is lifting!
SFX: [en] A wooden beam crashes. People scream.
EWAN: [en] Take the children toward the trees!
INES: [en] Not the trees. The ground is sliding. Follow my voice and keep low.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] My husband cannot stand.
EWAN: [en] Put his arm over my shoulder.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] You said there would be a hospital.
EWAN: [en] I know what I said.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Say what is here.
EWAN: [en] Nothing.
SFX: [en] Thunder detonates overhead.
NARRATOR: [en] By midnight, the hurricane had torn the abandoned trading shelter in half. One hundred and seventy-three settlers had arrived. Those still able to walk gathered on a strip of stone above the flood.
SFX: [en] Rainwater sloshes in a cooking pot. A spoon scrapes the empty bottom.
SETTLER: [en] Four handfuls of meal.
INES: [en] For how many?
SETTLER: [en] One hundred and sixty-one answered the count.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Thirty-eight are children.
EWAN: [en] Divide it among the children.
SETTLER: [en] And tomorrow?
EWAN: [en] There is a trading post north of the inlet.
INES: [en] Twelve miles in this weather.
EWAN: [en] You said they keep grain and salt fish.
INES: [en] They trade for recognised coin. Your families brought Briarhaven paper.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] We brought British sovereigns. Elspeth took them in Edinburgh and gave us these.
SFX: [en] Wet banknotes slap into Ewan’s hand.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Twenty pounds. Every note stamped by your bank.
EWAN: [en] There is no bank.
SETTLER: [en] Louder, clerk. Let the children hear what purchased their supper.
EWAN: [en] There is no Briarhaven bank. These notes cannot be redeemed.
VOICES: [en] Thief! Shame! He signed them! Take his coat! Search him!
SFX: [en] A fist strikes Ewan. He falls against wet stone.
INES: [en] Enough!
SETTLER: [en] He knew.
EWAN: [en] Yes.
SFX: [en] The crowd quiets under the rain.
EWAN: [en] I knew no Edinburgh bank would redeem them. I said so once, and then I kept preparing other papers because Gregor held my mother’s debt.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Your mother has a roof.
EWAN: [en] If we remain here, none of us will.
SETTLER: [en] You expect us to follow you?
EWAN: [en] No. I expect you to follow Ines. I will go to the trading post.
INES: [en] You cannot carry enough food alone.
EWAN: [en] Then choose six people who still have strength.
SETTLER: [en] I will not obey a Briarhaven clerk.
INES: [en] Good. Obey the woman who knows which river will drown you. Six people, rope around the waist, now.
SFX: [en] Wind gusts. Rope is pulled from a crate.
NARRATOR: [en] They left before dawn. Behind them, the settlers burned land certificates for warmth. The ink turned green before the paper blackened. Remember that.
SFX: [en] Boots drag through flooded mud. Fast river water roars nearby.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Stop. My rope is caught.
EWAN: [en] Hold still.
INES: [en] Water is rising. Cut it.
EWAN: [en] Then she loses the line.
INES: [en] Cut it, Ewan.
SFX: [en] Knife saws through rope. A surge of water. Ewan grunts.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] He has me!
INES: [en] Both of you, up the bank!
SFX: [en] Hands scrape over stones. Three bodies collapse in mud.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Why did you come after our ship?
EWAN: [en] To prove the fraud before you landed.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] You were late.
EWAN: [en] By forty-three days.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] Then do not be late again.
SFX: [en] A trading-post bell clatters in the wind. A heavy door opens on a chain.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Store is closed.
INES: [en] There are starving families at the southern inlet. We need meal, beans, salt fish and lamp oil.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Payment first.
EWAN: [en] We have Briarhaven banknotes.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Then you have kindling.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] These are twenty-pound notes.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Yesterday, three men offered me the same notes. Same number on every one. B-417.
EWAN: [en] Five hundred were printed with that number.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] You know your rubbish precisely.
EWAN: [en] I helped make it.
SFX: [en] The door chain tightens.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Then my courtesy to you is this: leave before the coastal patrol arrives.
INES: [en] We are not asking you to accept false money. Extend provisions against the ship’s stores.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] The ship is grounded and its cargo is under salvage claim.
EWAN: [en] Against my personal undertaking, then.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Your name?
EWAN: [en] Ewan Pell.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Occupation?
EWAN: [en] Clerk.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Property?
EWAN: [en] None.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] Thank you for saving my time.
SFX: [en] Door begins to close.
EWAN: [en] Wait.
SFX: [en] Leather pouch opens. Metal knocks against wood.
INES: [en] Ewan, no.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] What is that?
EWAN: [en] The official seal of Briarhaven.
INES: [en] The seal you proved was made in an Edinburgh workshop.
EWAN: [en] The settlers do not need proof tonight. They need food.
INES: [en] If you stamp a requisition, you use the fraud as authority.
EWAN: [en] I know.
INES: [en] If armed men honour it, they may later demand the territory named on it.
EWAN: [en] I know.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] My daughter has not eaten since yesterday morning.
SFX: [en] Nothing. Long.
EWAN: [en] Keeper, give me one sheet and an ink pad.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] I will give you neither.
EWAN: [en] Then I will write on the back of my land grant.
SFX: [en] Paper unfolds. Pencil scratches rapidly.
EWAN: [en] Forty sacks of meal. Twelve sacks of beans. Six barrels of salt fish. Medical spirits and oil. Requisitioned for the relief of Briarhaven settlers under emergency authority.
INES: [en] There is no emergency authority.
EWAN: [en] Tonight there is my name beneath it.
SFX: [en] Seal presses into softened wax.
SETTLER WOMAN: [en] You used it.
EWAN: [en] Yes.
EWAN: [en] Keeper, you have my signature, the seal and the names of one hundred and sixty-one survivors. Open the store.
SFX: [en] Paper is examined. Wind whistles through the doorway.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] This is carefully prepared.
EWAN: [en] Then honour it.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] I cannot.
EWAN: [en] Children will die twelve miles from your door.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] I am sorry for them.
EWAN: [en] Send the requisition to your coastal authority. Let them charge Briarhaven.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] That is precisely the problem, Mr Pell.
TRADING POST KEEPER: [en] The requisition is rejected because the coastal authorities have never recognized Briarhaven as a country.
