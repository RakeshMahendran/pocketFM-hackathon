# Episode 2 — Behind the Counting-Room Door

SFX: A ledger thumps onto a desk. A key turns in the outer office door.

EWAN: R-four-one-eight-seven was sold twice. Open Bell's private ledger.

PIPPA: Lower your voice.

EWAN: I want the entry before he destroys it.

PIPPA: And I want employment tomorrow morning.

EWAN: A subscriber paid two hundred pounds for a number now printed on Miles's bond.

PIPPA: I can count, Ewan.

EWAN: Then help me prove it was not a copying error.

PIPPA: Bell's drawer has two locks. I have one key.

EWAN: Who has the other?

SFX: A walking cane strikes the floor behind them.

BELL: The man whose private drawer you are discussing.

SFX: Nothing. Long.

BELL: Miss Shaw, upstairs. Kerr, remain.

PIPPA: Mr Bell, the counterfoil—

BELL: Upstairs.

SFX: Pippa's footsteps retreat. Door closes.

BELL: Let us be exact. You were dismissed from the salon table. You entered the accounts room. You opened paid subscription records.

EWAN: I found a duplicated serial.

BELL: You found paperwork beyond your station.

EWAN: The same bond number has taken four hundred pounds from two investors.

BELL: Has either investor complained?

EWAN: They do not know.

BELL: Then at present the only injury is to your sleep.

EWAN: Open your ledger.

BELL: That was almost an order.

EWAN: Open it, please.

BELL: Better.

SFX: Two keys enter two locks. A heavy drawer rolls out.

BELL: You may inspect the page. You may not touch it.

SFX: Ledger cover creaks.

EWAN: Issue three. Subscriptions received.

BELL: Read silently.

EWAN: R-four-one-eight-seven. Two hundred pounds. Bristol account.

SFX: Page turns.

EWAN: R-four-one-eight-seven again. Two hundred pounds. Miles Orrock.

BELL: A repetition.

EWAN: In different ink, entered eleven days apart.

BELL: Still a repetition.

EWAN: Your private ledger confirms both payments.

BELL: It confirms that two lines exist.

EWAN: One certificate number cannot secure two debts.

BELL: Ravenshore contains eight million acres. I think it can survive one overworked numeral.

EWAN: It is fraud if it was deliberate.

BELL: Such an ugly word from a man asking me for wages.

EWAN: I am asking you to cancel Miles's bond and return the second payment.

BELL: No.

EWAN: Then I will tell him.

BELL: Your mother's house is still burdened by your father's creditors, is it not.

EWAN: Leave her out of this.

BELL: I am trying to help. A dismissed clerk finds family obligations terribly expensive.

EWAN: That is a threat.

BELL: It is arithmetic.

SFX: Bell closes the private ledger.

BELL: We have a more immediate difficulty. Yesterday's bond register is unreadable.

SFX: Wet, swollen pages slap onto the desk.

EWAN: Water damage.

BELL: A porter upset a pail. You will recreate every entry from the counterfoils before morning.

EWAN: There are three hundred and twelve certificates.

BELL: Then your handwriting will finally justify its salary.

EWAN: I will not alter the duplicate.

BELL: Copy what the records say.

EWAN: The records say you accepted two payments against one number.

BELL: And if the register is not complete by eight, the records will say you abandoned your post after damaging it.

SFX: Bell walks to the door.

EWAN: You cannot keep me here.

BELL: The silver must be protected overnight. Surely you understand.

SFX: Door closes. Key turns from the outside.

EWAN: Bell!

SFX: Ewan pounds the locked door.

BELL: Through the door, Kerr. Three hundred and twelve entries. Do be accurate.

SFX: Bell's footsteps fade.

NARRATOR: Ewan wanted the truth entered once. Bell required the lie copied three hundred and twelve times.

SFX: Clock ticks. Pen scratches rapidly. Pages turn.

EWAN: R-four-one-seven-nine. One hundred pounds. R-four-one-eight-zero. Two hundred pounds.

SFX: Pen scratches. Clock continues.

EWAN: R-four-one-eight-seven. Bristol account. Two hundred pounds.

SFX: Pen pauses.

EWAN: R-four-one-eight-seven. Miles Orrock. Two hundred pounds.

SFX: Ewan draws a hard line beneath both entries.

EWAN: Duplicate serial retained exactly as found.

SFX: A folded paper slides under the locked door.

EWAN: Pippa?

PIPPA: Quietly.

EWAN: Bell has the key.

PIPPA: I know. I also know what left the office while you were in the salon.

EWAN: What is this docket?

PIPPA: Freight received at half past six. Six sealed cases.

EWAN: Destination: Alderwick Auction Rooms.

PIPPA: Bell's first public sale begins tomorrow.

EWAN: This says eight hundred certificates. The authentication column is signed E.K.

PIPPA: Those are not your strokes.

EWAN: I signed none of them.

PIPPA: A fresh shipment of Ravenshore bonds has already been sent to the auction house.

EWAN: And every certificate carries my forged initials.
