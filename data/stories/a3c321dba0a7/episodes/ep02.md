# Episode 2 — The Reassuring Seal

SFX: MEETING GAVEL STRIKES. CHAIRS SHIFT IN A PACKED SUBSCRIPTION ROOM.

IAIN: [language=en] This letter came from the coast. Read it before you take another pound.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Mr Bell is no longer employed by the Poyais office.

IAIN: [language=en] Then you cannot order me to sit down.

EDWIN: [language=en] Let him read.

MARA: [language=en] This meeting concerns the two-hundred-thousand-pound loan. Private correspondence is not evidence.

IAIN: [language=en] Salt water marked the envelope. A packet master recorded its arrival. The writer travelled with the first settlers.

ELSPETH: [language=en] The first party included frightened people unused to the coast. Confusion is not proof.

IAIN: [language=en] Nora Keel was the ship’s steward. She names the landing place, the stores unloaded and the settlers already ill.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Let us be practical. Read the alarming portion, and then we can return to business.

SFX: LETTER UNFOLDS.

IAIN: [language=en] “We landed where the government chart marks the capital. There was forest, an abandoned shed and no official to receive us. No harbour office recorded the ship. No government supplied the promised houses, hospital or road.”

VOICE IN ROOM: [language=en] No hospital?

EDWIN: [language=en] I was promised the post of chief surgeon.

ELSPETH: [language=en] And you may still hold it. One steward’s letter cannot erase a nation.

IAIN: [language=en] A nation should leave more than stationery in London.

SFX: UNEASY MURMURS RISE.

MARA: [language=en] Quiet. Mr Bell, what exactly do you demand?

IAIN: [language=en] State what you verified before you placed your firm behind the loan.

MARA: [language=en] I reviewed land revenues, customs projections and government guarantees.

IAIN: [language=en] Independently?

MARA: [language=en] They bore proper signatures and seals.

IAIN: [language=en] Did you contact a treasury in Poyais?

MARA: [language=en] No.

IAIN: [language=en] A customs authority?

MARA: [language=en] No.

IAIN: [language=en] A port officer?

MARA: [language=en] Correspondence was handled through the accredited London office.

EDWIN: [language=en] That means Cassian.

IAIN: [language=en] Say the whole figure, Mara. Two hundred thousand pounds depends upon what revenue?

SFX: NOTHING. LONG.

MARA: [language=en] The loan depends upon projected revenues of the Poyais state.

IAIN: [language=en] A state you independently verified?

MARA: [language=en] No. I have never independently verified that state.

SFX: THE ROOM ERUPTS. CHAIRS SCRAPE. SEVERAL SUBSCRIBERS SHOUT FOR THEIR MONEY.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Thank you, Mara. Precision is always useful.

SFX: A LEATHER CASE OPENS. HEAVY PAPER SLIDES ONTO THE TABLE.

CASSIAN: [language=en] This dispatch arrived through a separate channel this morning. It reports the settlers safe, the capital operational and the government receiving ships as planned.

EDWIN: [language=en] Who signed it?

CASSIAN: [language=en] The acting secretary at the capital.

IAIN: [language=en] Which capital?

CASSIAN: [language=en] The one your letter pretends does not exist.

MARA: [language=en] Give it to me.

SFX: PAPER PASSES HANDS. MARA RUNS A FINGER OVER EMBOSSED WAX.

MARA: [language=en] Government heading. Ministerial countersignature. Official seal.

ELSPETH: [language=en] Then the settlers are safe.

IAIN: [language=en] Read the date of departure.

MARA: [language=en] It is partially blurred.

IAIN: [language=en] Read the cargo references.

MARA: [language=en] Stores, medicines and tools were received.

IAIN: [language=en] My ledger lists those stores as still sitting in a London warehouse.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Your ledger was the work of an incompetent clerk.

IAIN: [language=en] Page forty-seven. Ask the warehouse agent.

SFX: LEDGER DROPS ON TABLE. PAGES TURN.

MARA: [language=en] Twelve crates of tools. Eight of medicines. Thirty sacks of seed.

IAIN: [language=en] None loaded on the first vessel. Rowan Pike’s manifest confirms it.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Mr Pike is a dock clerk seeking advancement.

IAIN: [language=en] And my sums?

MARA: [language=en] Your totals agree with the warehouse receipts.

IAIN: [language=en] Say it so the subscribers hear.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Mara, there is no need to indulge him.

MARA: [language=en] There is every need. My firm’s name is in this room.

SFX: MARA RISES. THE ROOM SETTLES.

MARA: [language=en] Iain Bell’s bond ledger is accurate. The cargo figures, subscription totals and unpaid balances are correct.

EDWIN: [language=en] And your validation?

MARA: [language=en] My validation relied upon papers supplied by Cassian’s office. I did not independently verify their origin.

VOICE IN ROOM: [language=en] The dismissed clerk kept the honest books.

SECOND VOICE: [language=en] Read his ledger, not the Cazique’s dispatch.

CASSIAN: [language=en] A clerk has checked addition. He has not disproved a government seal.

IAIN: [language=en] Give me the dispatch.

CASSIAN: [language=en] It is sovereign correspondence. You no longer have clearance.

MARA: [language=en] He has earned the right to inspect the paper that contradicts his accounts.

CASSIAN: [language=en] Rights are expensive things to invent in a subscription room.

SFX: CASSIAN SNATCHES UP THE DISPATCH. MEETING GAVEL STRIKES REPEATEDLY.

NARRATOR: [language=en] The meeting broke apart with no new subscriptions taken. For the first time, Mara had chosen Iain’s figures over Cassian’s assurance—and done it before every buyer in the room.

SFX: OFFICE DOOR UNLOCKS. NIGHT STREET NOISE. IAIN AND AGNES ENTER.

AGNES: [language=en] You were locked in here, publicly dismissed, and you still came back.

IAIN: [language=en] I need the seal inventory.

AGNES: [language=en] You need sleep.

IAIN: [language=en] The dispatch seal had a nick above the crown. I have seen it before.

SFX: DRAWER PULLS. WOOD STRIKES WOOD.

IAIN: [language=en] The lock is split.

AGNES: [language=en] Say what is missing.

IAIN: [language=en] The brass government die.

SFX: IAIN SETS THE DISPATCH IMPRESSION BESIDE THE EMPTY DIE CASE.

NARRATOR: [language=en] The seal on Cassian’s reassuring dispatch was cast from the brass die stolen from Iain’s desk the night before.
