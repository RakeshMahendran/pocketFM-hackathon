The Rosenheim Poltergeist: The Office That Accused Everyone

SUPERNATURAL INTRUSION · 1967–1970 · Rosenheim, Bavaria, West Germany
43
out of 50
~120 episodes

Phones ring by themselves, lights swing, bills vanish, and electrical instruments misbehave inside a law office until engineers, parapsychologists, skeptics, and staff members begin investigating one another.

What was actually done

A workplace experienced alleged unexplained disturbances, while competing investigators argued over psychic activity, fraud, mismeasurement, and deliberate tricks involving the office’s fixtures.

Engine — why it keeps generating

The office remains a sealed arena where each new disturbance produces a new suspect, a new investigator, and a new theory that can be true only if someone else is lying.

The fear a listener recognises

The fear is not a haunted castle but a normal workplace where every ringing phone can prove that a colleague is lying.

Why it was passed over

The case is evidentially disputed, so the serial needs a deliberate believer-versus-skeptic structure rather than pretending the haunting is settled fact.
