The Tichborne Claimant

DENIED IDENTITY · 1866–1898 · Australia and England
50
out of 50
~180 episodes

A massive man from Australia claims to be the missing heir to an English estate, wins one grieving mother, divides an entire family, and turns his identity into the longest courtroom war of his age.

What was actually done

Arthur Orton presented himself as the lost Sir Roger Tichborne, secured Lady Tichborne’s support, attracted witnesses and money, lost the civil claim, then faced a perjury prosecution and continued campaigning after conviction.

Engine — why it keeps generating

Every supporter’s testimony creates a counter-test, every contradiction produces a new explanation, and the claimant’s public movement survives even after the courtroom rejects him.

The fear a listener recognises

The listener feels the primitive shock of a stranger standing before a mother and saying he is the child everyone else buried.

Why it was passed over

It is arguably the strongest raw serial engine here, but the exact event has already been made into a dramatic film and must be blocked.
