George Psalmanazar and the Country He Invented Inside Himself

SECRET STATUS · 1702–1764 · England, France, and the learned societies of early eighteenth-century Europe
46
out of 50
~250 episodes

A penniless European drifter becomes England’s celebrated native informant from Formosa, inventing a language, religion, calendar, and childhood until scholars begin asking questions he cannot answer.

What was actually done

A man with an unknown real name presents himself as a displaced Formosan, then publishes a full cultural system and performs it publicly for years.

Engine — why it keeps generating

Every lecture, patron, translation request, and theological challenge requires him to expand the invented identity while concealing the absence of any verifiable past.

The fear a listener recognises

Listeners understand the panic of having to invent more personal history every time someone asks one ordinary question about your past.

Why it was passed over

The deception is intellectually rich but lacks a single physical antagonist, so the adaptation must turn rival scholars and patrons into active hunters.
