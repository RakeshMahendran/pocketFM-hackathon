Poyais: The Country That Had to Exist on Paper

THE LONG DECEPTION · 1821–1834 · London, Scotland, France, and the Caribbean coast of Central America
47
out of 50
~300 episodes

A decorated soldier invents an entire nation, sells its bonds and land, recruits settlers, and must keep the country alive in London after the first ships discover nothing but jungle.

What was actually done

Gregor MacGregor claimed to be the Cazique of Poyais, manufactured government bonds, land grants, maps, promotional literature, constitutional details, and a colonial administration for a fictional country.

Engine — why it keeps generating

Every new investor, official, emigrant, and creditor forces MacGregor to add another layer to the imaginary state before the previous lie collapses.

The fear a listener recognises

The instantly recognisable fear is discovering that the country, job, property, and future you paid for exists only because strangers keep pretending it does.

Why it was passed over

Its strongest settlement material can become grim and repetitive unless the serial keeps returning to the parallel London machinery sustaining the lie.
