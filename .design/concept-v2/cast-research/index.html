RESEARCH & IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

What informed these screens

Visible choices, quieter evidence

Names, roles and selection remain visible. Full biographies and beat lists are secondary. This applies NN/g’s recognition-over-recall and progressive-disclosure guidance.

Recognition and recall ↗
Progressive disclosure ↗

Selection is not generation

Card selection changes the inspected character. A distinct primary action continues the workflow. Option B exposes direct selection and has no automatic carousel movement.

Carbon selectable tiles ↗

Actual 3D assets

These portraits were rendered from Kenney’s CC0 Blocky Characters GLB files using Three.js. Each used model includes a rig and 27 animation clips. Stock identities and clothing are stand-ins, not the final cast art.
