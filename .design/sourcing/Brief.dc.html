The Court That Never Was
A man the bar council jailed for practising law without a degree rents an office, calls it a tribunal, and settles other people's land disputes for five years.
The engine
Every judgment he delivers has to be protected by the next one. The moment any losing party takes his order to a real court, it ends.
The case against it
The protagonist is a fraud with real victims. A season that enjoys his cleverness too much becomes a story that thinks defrauding poor litigants is charming.
