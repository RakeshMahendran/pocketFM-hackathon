# The demo narrative

One story, carried through every stage of the pipeline, so each folder holds the
real input to the next one. Nothing here is assembled after the fact.

**The story.** A man the bar council once jailed for practising law without a
degree rents an office, calls it a tribunal, and spends five years settling other
people's land disputes — until the litigants who won discover their victories
were never real.

## Stages

| Folder | Stage | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| `01_research/` | the research agent | `candidate.json` — the real event, graded and cleared |
| `02_dossier/` | the expander | timeline, cast, season plan, clearance |
| `03_serial/` | the serial writer | 12 episodes, 783 lines, 49 canon beats |
| `04_views/` | character views | knows / blind / gaps, per beat id, all 12 characters |
| `05_spinoff/` | promotion + spinoff writer | Manjula's bible, and one episode generated on a crossing point |
| `06_validation/` | the validator panel | the verdict on that episode |
| `07_audio/` | the audio chain | episodes 1 and 2, mastered |

## The claim this is built to demonstrate

Every litigant who won a case in that room walked out happy and does not know the
judgment was nothing. Their ignorance is not a device invented to make the
spinoff work — it is the fact of the case.

Manjula wins her father's land in mainline episode 6 and tells the whole lane
that a court finally listened. In episode 12, Morris is offered a lighter charge
if he lists every award as worthless, and he refuses on hers alone, knowing what
it costs him. **She is never told.** That is `hidden_from` doing the work: her
serial can run ten episodes without contradicting the mainline, because
everything she would need to contradict it is a beat she was never in.

`05_spinoff/episode_b024.json` shows the mechanism: `x_manjula_b024_002` carries
`crossing_of: b024` — the same beat as mainline episode 6, in both serials at
once. Objective facts identical, meaning opposite. Everything the spinoff wrote
is `branch_canon`; `core_canon` is untouched.

## Provenance — what was generated by what

| Stage | Written by |
|---|---|
| `01_research/candidate.json` | **by hand**, from web research, in the research agent's schema |
| `02_dossier/dossier.json` | **by hand**, from web research, in the expander's schema |
| `03_serial/` episodes and direction | **by hand** — dialogue, emotion, intensity, pace, beds and stings |
| `03_serial/beats.json` | `gpt-5.6-sol`, the serial writer, prose and canon in one call |
| `05_spinoff/` bible and episode | `gpt-5.6-sol`, promotion and the spinoff writer |
| `06_validation/` | `gpt-5.6-sol`, the panel |
| `07_audio/` | Sarvam `bulbul:v3` for voice, ElevenLabs for effects and stings |

Two things follow from that table and should be said rather than glossed. The
research agent did not find this event, and the expander did not build this
dossier — both were authored to their own schemas and validate against them. And
the episode dialogue is hand-written; what the pipeline generated is the canon
underneath it, which is the part the spinoff half actually runs on.

Names are the real ones from the reporting. That is a deliberate exception to
`CLAUDE.md` hard rule 4, taken for a staged demo rather than for publication.

## Gaps

`06_validation/` holds the clean verdict but no leak proof — the deliberately
unconstrained run that shows the panel catching a violation. Without it the
checker only ever shows green, which is the weaker demonstration.
`tasks.py leak` produces it.

Episodes 3 to 12 have no audio yet.
