# Episode 7 — "The Number"

SFX: district court registry, ledgers, quiet

NARRATOR: Every empire built on paper dies the same way. Not by police. Not by a rival. By one man, at one desk, whose only job is to check that a number is real.
    [tension 0.80 slow | bed tension | SWELL]

SFX: heavy bundle on desk

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Sir, this bundle came in the afternoon filing. There is one attachment I cannot place.
    [hesitation 0.58 normal | bed neutral]

HARDIK: Which attachment?
    [neutral 0.52 clipped | bed neutral]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: An award, sir. From some forum. The party has annexed it as proof of settled possession.
    [hesitation 0.58 normal | bed neutral]

SFX: paper unfolding

HARDIK: Read the cause title.
    [neutral 0.55 normal | bed neutral]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Arbitration and Dispute Resolution Forum. Before the Presiding Officer. Case number A-D-R-F, slash, one seven six, slash, twenty-three.
    [neutral 0.58 slow | bed tension]

HARDIK: One seven six. Meaning they are claiming one hundred and seventy-six matters.
    [tension 0.68 slow | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Is that a problem, sir?
    [hesitation 0.55 normal | bed tension]

HARDIK: It is a number. Numbers are either true or they are not.
    [neutral 0.62 slow | bed tension]

NARRATOR: Hardik Desai had been sitting at that desk twenty-two years. In twenty-two years, he had signed nothing, decided nothing, and refused nothing that was correct. His entire profession was one question. Is this document what it says it is?
    [reflective 0.70 slow | bed tension]

SFX: ledger being pulled from shelf

HARDIK: Check the list of arbitrators empanelled with this court.
    [neutral 0.60 clipped | bed tension]

SFX: pages turning

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Checking, sir... no such name.
    [hesitation 0.60 normal | bed tension]

HARDIK: Check the section-eleven appointments. Last five years.
    [neutral 0.65 clipped | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: No, sir.
    [hesitation 0.60 clipped | bed tension]

HARDIK: Check the retired list. Sometimes they continue as arbitrators privately.
    [tension 0.68 normal | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Sir, there is no such name anywhere.
    [fear 0.68 slow | bed fear]

HARDIK: And the case-number sequence? A-D-R-F.
    [tension 0.72 clipped | bed fear]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: This court is not issuing that series, sir. No court in this district is issuing that series.
    [fear 0.75 slow | bed fear | STING]

NARRATOR: One hundred and seventy-six matters. From a forum that did not exist. Filed into a real court, by people who had no idea.
    [tension 0.85 slow | bed tension | DROP]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Shall I return the bundle at the counter, sir? Defective annexure.
    [hesitation 0.60 normal | bed tension]

HARDIK: No.
    [tension 0.75 clipped | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Sir?
    [hesitation 0.58 clipped | bed tension]

HARDIK: If I return it at the counter, the party will remove that annexure and file again by evening. And then it is gone.
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Then... report it, sir? To the district judge?
    [hesitation 0.62 normal | bed tension]

HARDIK: Report what? That one annexure has a number I do not recognise? He will ask me how many such awards exist. And I will say — I do not know, sir. One.
    [hurt_anger 0.75 normal | bed tension]

NARRATOR: This is why it survived five years. Not because nobody noticed. Because the first man who noticed knew that one paper is not a case.
    [tension 0.80 slow | bed tension]

HARDIK: Route it for scrutiny. Do not return it. Do not mark it defective.
    [tension 0.72 clipped | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: And if the party asks?
    [hesitation 0.58 normal | bed tension]

HARDIK: The party will not ask. People who file a fake award never ask why it is taking time.
    [sarcasm 0.72 slow | bed tension | STING]

HARDIK: Tomorrow. First I want to see whether that number appears again.
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed tension]

RECORDS ASSISTANT: Where will you keep it, sir?
    [hesitation 0.58 normal | bed tension]

HARDIK: Not the file. Only the number.
    [tension 0.80 slow | bed tension]

SFX: pen on paper, drawer sliding shut

NARRATOR: He wrote A-D-R-F, slash, one seven six, slash, twenty-three on a slip of paper. He put it in his drawer. And he waited.
    [tension 0.85 slow | bed tension | DROP]

NARRATOR: Above a tyre shop, four kilometres away, Sarika Kamble called matter number one hundred and seventy-nine. Nobody in that room had ever heard the name Hardik Desai.
    [tension 0.90 slow | bed tension | BUTTON]
