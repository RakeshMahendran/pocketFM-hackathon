# Episode 8 — "What Your Signature Means"

SFX: office room, morning, files

NARRATOR: Fourteen months. Fourteen salary envelopes. And Sarika Kamble had never once asked what she was signing — because for the first time in her family's history, somebody had given her a designation.
    [longing 0.75 slow | bed longing | SWELL]

SFX: chairs, room settling

SARIKA: Matter number one hundred and eighty-two. Parties to enter.
    [urgency 0.68 normal | bed neutral]

ADVOCATE: Before we begin. I have one preliminary matter.
    [neutral 0.62 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: You are appearing again, counsel.
    [tension 0.70 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: I am appearing under protest, and I have told my client so in writing.
    [hurt_anger 0.72 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: Noted. What is the preliminary matter?
    [tension 0.72 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: Not for you, sir. For your registry clerk.
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed tension | STING]

SARIKA: Me?
    [fear 0.65 clipped | bed fear]

ADVOCATE: Madam. On every certified copy issued from this room, there is one line above your signature. Can you read it out for me?
    [neutral 0.65 slow | bed fear]

SFX: paper held up

SARIKA: I certify the proceedings recorded by me.
    [hesitation 0.60 slow | bed fear]

ADVOCATE: Thank you. Now tell me — what does certify mean?
    [tension 0.72 slow | bed fear]

SARIKA: It means... it is correct, sir. What is written is what happened.
    [hesitation 0.62 normal | bed fear]

ADVOCATE: And who has authorised you to certify anything?
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed fear]

SARIKA: Sir...
    [fear 0.75 slow | bed fear]

MORRIS: Counsel, address the forum, not my staff.
    [hurt_anger 0.78 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: I am addressing the only person in this room whose signature travels outside it.
    [tension 0.82 slow | bed tension]

NARRATOR: And that was true. His signature stayed on the paper. Hers went out. To landlords. To brothers. To builders. Into filings. Into a district court registry, four kilometres away, where a man had already written one number on a slip.
    [tension 0.85 slow | bed tension | DROP]

ADVOCATE: Sir, I will ask you the same question I asked in that back room. Where is the order appointing you?
    [tension 0.80 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: The parties consent.
    [tension 0.75 clipped | bed tension]

ADVOCATE: The parties consent to an arbitrator. Who made you one?
    [tension 0.82 slow | bed tension]

MORRIS: I constituted this forum.
    [sorrow 0.78 slow | bed sorrow]

ADVOCATE: Say it fully.
    [tension 0.75 slow | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: I constituted this forum myself. There is no external order appointing me as presiding officer.
    [sorrow 0.82 slow | bed sorrow]

NARRATOR: He said it out loud. In his own room. In front of his own clerk. And the strange thing was — not one party got up and left.
    [reflective 0.75 slow | bed sorrow]

ADVOCATE: Madam. Now you have heard it. Do you still certify?
    [tension 0.78 slow | bed tension]

SFX: room emptying, door closing, fan alone

NARRATOR: That evening, after everybody had gone, Sarika did not go home.
    [sorrow 0.70 slow | bed sorrow]

SFX: envelopes counted on desk

SARIKA: Fourteen.
    [sorrow 0.68 slow | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: What are you counting?
    [neutral 0.60 normal | bed sorrow]

SARIKA: Envelopes, sir. Fourteen months I am taking salary from this office.
    [sorrow 0.72 slow | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: Your work has been correct. Every register entry.
    [hesitation 0.65 normal | bed sorrow]

SARIKA: My father is a night watchman, sir. Twenty-eight years, one company. When they gave him a uniform, he wore it to a wedding.
    [longing 0.75 slow | bed longing]

MORRIS: Sarika—
    [hesitation 0.68 normal | bed longing]

SARIKA: I told everybody. My mother. My aunt. The whole chawl. Sarika is working in a registry. Sarika is a registry clerk.
    [sorrow 0.82 normal | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: That was not a lie.
    [hesitation 0.70 clipped | bed sorrow]

SARIKA: Then what was it, sir?
    [hurt_anger 0.80 slow | bed sorrow]

NARRATOR: He had an answer for the advocate. He had an answer for the builder. He had an answer for his own wife. He did not have one for this.
    [sorrow 0.85 slow | bed sorrow | DROP]

SARIKA: That first day. I asked you what proceedings. You remember?
    [hurt_anger 0.78 slow | bed sorrow]

MORRIS: I remember.
    [sorrow 0.72 slow | bed sorrow]

SARIKA: And you said — that is standard language.
    [hurt_anger 0.85 slow | bed sorrow | STING]

MORRIS: It is standard language.
    [sorrow 0.75 slow | bed sorrow]

SARIKA: Sir. Do not call me by my name now. Just answer one thing.
    [fear 0.85 slow | bed fear]

MORRIS: Ask.
    [fear 0.78 slow | bed fear]

SARIKA: Can I be jailed for this?
    [fear 0.90 slow | bed fear]

SFX: bag lifted, footsteps on metal stairs

NARRATOR: He was still thinking of the answer when she picked up her bag and walked down the metal staircase.
    [sorrow 0.78 slow | bed sorrow]

NARRATOR: And in a drawer, four kilometres away, the number A-D-R-F, one seven six, twenty-three was no longer alone.
    [tension 0.90 slow | bed tension | BUTTON]
