---
description: Prove the continuity guarantee by generating a deliberate violation
---

Generate a spinoff episode for $ARGUMENTS **without** injecting the constraint
set, then run the validator on it.

Expected: the validator reports leakage — the character demonstrating knowledge
of a beat in their `blind` list, quoted, with the beat named.

Save both the leaky output and the clean constrained run to
`tests/fixtures/`. These two files are the demo's proof that the continuity
claim is a mechanism and not a prompt.

If the validator reports nothing, the validator is broken. Fix it, not the
generation.
